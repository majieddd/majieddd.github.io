THE ONE RULE

One wave, identical for every commander still standing: same units, same instants, every base. What a kill becomes is your commander’s rite: it may return as itself, as a different soldier from your roster, as something incubating where it fell: or as nothing, for the two rites that march on a clock and on gold instead. The pair over NEXT is the whole war: YOU SENT is yours walking at them, INBOUND is theirs walking at you. Whatever dies on that second trip stays dead. Your rite is named on the bar above the summon buttons; the FIELD MANUAL has all five.

WHEN ONE GETS THROUGH

Reaching your base does not spend the life. The unit turns around and carries it back toward the spawn edge, slower than it walked in, and only crossing that edge makes the loss real: your ♥ reads (n⚑) for everything still in flight. Kill the carrier before it gets out and you never pay at all.

REACTIONS

Mark with one element, then hit with a different marking element within 4s:

🔥+❄ THERMAL SHOCK🔥+⚡ PLASMA🔥+☠ IMMOLATE ❄+⚡ SUPERCONDUCT❄+☠ PARALYSIS⚡+☠ CATALYSE ◐+🔥 COLLAPSE◐+⚡ RUPTURE◐+☠ BLIGHT ◐ onto any mark ENTROPY

The first six read the same in either order. ◐ VOID is the exception: the three named above are ◐'s own mark being struck, while ◐ landing on anything else is always ENTROPY. KINETIC ◆ and RADIANT ✦ neither mark nor trigger; straight damage is what they trade for it.

MONEY & PRESSURE

Gold still in hand when a wave finishes spawning pays 6%, capped at 35% of that wave’s own reward, and it is read before the reward lands: so it prices what you chose to hold. Each tower’s price rises with every copy you own, and every tower standing inflates every price on top of that. Resonant field buys the next wave more health for more bounty: your wave alone, a bet on your own defence and never a weapon aimed across the board.

WHEN THE LINE BENDS

Aura carriers: the Pylon column: Marshal, Anchor, Cadence, Bastion , barely fight. They project a field that resists slowing, refuses displacement, hastens or armours everything beside them, and killing the carrier strips it from the whole group the same frame. Bosses and minibosses take none of it anyway: no slow, no stun, no displacement , damage is the only answer. Escalations land every 10 waves, two a time past 20 and three past 30; you are offered three and take one, and the two you refuse are drawn first next time. Refusing defers, never discounts.
