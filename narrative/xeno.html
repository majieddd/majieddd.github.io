THE OATH (campaign start)

1. The Compact does not remember beginning. Conquest is not our policy. It is our metabolism, and a body does not vote on breathing.

2. For ten thousand cycles the herds fed the yield. Bodies. Ground. The bright signatures of consciousness, farmed and banked.

3. One herd was our finest work. Bred patient, bred tough, fenced with its own edited history. It never even rattled the fence.

4. Eleven months ago the fence broke from the inside. The herd built a fleet out of what we taught its genes to survive.

5. So the pasture widens. Do not dress it in flags: we are not angry, and we are not afraid. Stopping is not a thing the body knows. THE HARVEST CONTINUES.
