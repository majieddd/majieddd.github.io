THE OATH (campaign start)

1. No flags on the hull. No names on the manifest. The Free Roads were never founded, they accreted, one refusal at a time.

2. Every power out here owns something. We own passage. The Federation rings it, the Harvest farms it, and everything still moves through us.

3. Then Earth blew its own secret open, shot down its judgement day, and came off the porch swinging. Business has never been better.

4. Refugees one week, salvage the next, and cargo we have learned not to open. The roads carry whatever pays. That sentence used to be simpler.

5. So here is the job: keep every road open, and no throne, fleet or god closes a single one. What you carry on them is on you. THE FREE ROADS RUN.

THE DEPARTURE · interstitial, straight after the oath

RAKE, The Corsair — “No Flags”

Every power out here has a book of rules and a fence to read it behind. We have the roads. Nobody drafted us, nobody owns us, and nothing we do tonight will be because somebody made us. Remember that part. It matters later.

The Constellation obeys nothing. Which means everything it does is chosen, and chosen is a heavier word than it sounds.

ACT 1: BARNARD'S STAR garrisoned by HUMANITY

Somebody is standing in the doorway. The wreck yards where every crew has a dead friend, the refinery whose fuel is free at the gate, the anchorage that has never been on a chart, the shelter you do not shoot at, and Harbour Nine, which is not a base or a port but the place any of them can always go. The rule survives the act, and gets carved into the bay wall.

THE DISCLOSURE FILES · result screen, after the seat falls

GRIST, The Scrapper — “The Price Of True”

The Disclosure files are loose, and half the galaxy is bidding to bury them. I had the sealed collaborator list in my hold for six hours. Highest offer would have bought me a fleet. I posted it to every open channel for free. Do not ask me why. I am still doing the arithmetic myself.

The Scrapper had the sealed collaborator list in his hold for six hours. The bidding would have bought a fleet.

The crews open the mirror farm, the cloud docks, the canyon road and the water, and post every rate where anyone can read it. At Luna they take the relay and start giving away the only thing on the roads that was never for sale.

The Corsair laughed for a day. He has not laughed since. A ship nothing steers is not free. It is cargo.
