THE OATH (campaign start)

1. Before your histories began, the Federation of Light swore the Mandate: stand between life and what hunts it, and never rule what you protect.

2. Forty worlds ring under our shield. Forty registries defended, supplied, and waiting, as the Mandate requires, to rise on their own.

3. Then a herd broke its fence. The species we deferred, eighty years running, built a fleet in one winter and came out asking questions.

4. The First Speaker has ordered the rings extended. The field commanders have begun asking what, precisely, the rings are for.

5. Go and hold the line, commander. And if you must choose between the Mandate and the people it was written for, remember which one bleeds. THE MANDATE BEGINS.

THE DEPARTURE · interstitial, straight after the oath

SERAPH, The Radiant — “The Mandate, Recited”

We do not conquer. We stand between a world and what hunts it, we hand down what we safely can, and we wait for it to rise on its own. That is the Mandate. I have recited it over forty worlds. Recite it with me until you believe it, or until one of us finds out why we cannot.

The Federation claims worlds only to ring them with defence. The campaign tests whether that has ever been the whole truth.

ACT 1: THE PLEIADES garrisoned by THE XENO

Hold the Mandate's own home while something else sings in it. Every world here is an office of the Federation: the foundry that certifies protection, the registry of the forty, the ring anchor, the seed gardens, the tribunal, the deferral vaults. Retaking them means reading them, and the registry says protected forty, risen none.

THE MORAL OF THIS ACTDefence becomes occupation the moment the defended were never asked. Forty worlds protected, none risen, and the registry says so in our own hand.

MAIA

1. APPROACH pcut_10_light_1

Our own foundry, and something is singing in it that is not us. Go quietly, and whatever answers, do not sing back.

2. THE GROUND pcut_10_light_2

A hall of tuned crystal pipes standing upright in nebula light, where the Federation cuts the hymns that certify a world as protected.

3. THE ASSAULT pcut_10_light_3

Choir-ring emplacements braced between the pipes, and hard-light buttresses that ring aloud when they are struck. The defence and the instrument are the same object.

4. AFTERMATH pcut_10_light_4

The pipes are cracked and the hall is ours. We have certified forty worlds from this room. We could not certify this one.

5. NEW ORDER pcut_10_light_5

Maia sounds again, in a register the Mandate has no notation for. The Chorus wrote it, and she will not explain the second verse.

ELECTRA

Our own archive, under a dying star, with the Compact reading our files. Whatever they have learned about us, they learned it from us.

2. THE GROUND pcut_11_light_2

The Chorus pulled the registry afterwards. Protected: all forty worlds. Risen: none. She has stopped singing the second verse.

THE DISCLOSURE FILES · result screen, after the seat falls

AURELIA, The Chorus — “Forty Rings, No Sunrise”

I pulled the registry on every world we have ever ringed. Protected, all of them. Risen, none. And one page deeper: Earth, flagged for protection in 1947, deferred, deferred, deferred, while the harvest ran three generations. I certify our hymns, commander. I cannot certify that page.

The Federation knew about Earth and deferred it for eighty years. Protection that never arrives is called something else by the protected.

THE TURNING

One page in the human files carries our seal. Earth, flagged for protection, 1947. Deferred. Deferred. Deferred.

Three generations of harvest ran under that deferral. The word non-interference does not survive contact with the page.

Your fleet parked a defence ring around this system while my species was inventoried like grain. You call it non-interference. From inside the fence it looked like attendance. You watched, Radiant. The only power you actually defended was your own claim to be defending life.

We defended our claim to be defending life. The Archivist said it to our faces on an open channel, and nobody present could file an objection.
