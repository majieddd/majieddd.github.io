Proxima Centauri is a red dwarf about 0.12 solar masses. Proxima b is the confirmed planet: minimum mass roughly 1.1 Earths, orbiting at 0.0485 AU with an 11.2-day period. Proxima c is an unconfirmed candidate, minimum mass near 7 Earths, at about 1.5 AU with a roughly 5.2-year period. Proxima d is also an unconfirmed candidate, minimum mass about 0.26 Earths, at 0.029 AU with a 5.1-day period. Proxima flares often, with small flares nearly daily and major superflares every few months. These flares bathe the planets in intense X-ray and ultraviolet radiation that can strip atmospheres and irradiate any surface, making the habitable-zone planet b likely hostile to life despite its temperature.
