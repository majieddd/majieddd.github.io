The Law of One Ra material is the source for the Sirius social memory complex, which it describes as an entity grouping from the Sirius system. Norma Milanovich's channeled book "We, The Arcturians" is the origin of the Arcturians tradition. Billy Meier's Plejaren contact reports are the source for both the Pleiadian contacts and the Lyran root race of humanity, his material naming Lyra as the original human lineage. The Zeta Reticuli grey aliens originate from the Betty and Barney Hill abduction case, whose disputed star map allegedly pointed to Zeta Reticuli. Edgar Cayce is listed among the options but is not the source for any of these five claims.
