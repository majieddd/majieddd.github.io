BELIEF: We Free Captains came to keep the roads open. The powers let Apophis fall then rushed in with tolls and forms. A crew that shares its catch is freer than one begging a flag to eat. Nobody owns the dark between worlds, and we sail your skies to prove it.

COST: Your refineries pay us a tenth or burn, and old crews clock on under our watch. The Apophis wreck feeds our holds before your widows see a credit. You traded a far authority for a near one with a cutlass.

MORAL: The folks you call liberators still take a cut, and you forgive them, for at least they laugh with you first.

BLIND SPOT: We cannot see we have become the flag we sailed against, collecting the tolls we cursed and calling it family.
