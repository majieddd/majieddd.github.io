BELIEF: The Free Captains believe any lane strung with another power's debt is a road asking to be taken, and free crews owe nothing to absent landlords. They seized Zeta Reticuli because its tolls fed the Compact and the Accord, not its people.

COST: Reticulan haulers who already paid the old tolls now pay them again to the Captains, and stations that traded on Accord guarantees starve when the lanes close.

MORAL: People who fight to escape a toll rarely stop collecting one once the booths are theirs.

BLIND SPOT: The Captains still call Zeta a liberated road, and cannot see they have become the landlord they sailed against.
