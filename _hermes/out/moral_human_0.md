BELIEF: We were nearly erased while they watched and knew. We hold Earth ourselves now, because no accord or compact will lift a finger for us. Trust died at Apophis.

COST: The Free Captains who ran supply through the debris, and the Luminous missionaries who came to help, are seized as collaborators. Their ships and clinics are taken, their people locked up, for the crime of not being us.

MORAL: A people who survive a betrayal will punish the nearest stranger before they question their own.

BLIND SPOT: We call this liberation, but we now watch our own blocs the way we watched them, and we do not notice the lock we have built.
