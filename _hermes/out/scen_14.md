SCENARIO: Celaeno is a young blue star still wrapped in its birth nebula, so fine luminous dust is always falling through the weak open sky and settling on the carved benches.

VISUAL: A pale ring of tiered judgement seats under a sky full of slow blue-gold motes, dust piling like snow on every empty bench.

TACTICAL: The falling dust coats every surface, so any defender who moves leaves a glowing trail and a freshly vacated bench stays lit for minutes, giving away positions.
