SCENARIO: Alcyone is a multiple star, and the golden rings are cut to focus its companion suns. Only at the rare conjunction of all the sisters does the empty central floor flood with focused starlight, searing anything standing in the hollow.

VISUAL: The companion suns wheel into alignment and the bare floor erupts in a column of golden, killing light.

TACTICAL: Defenders fight from the ring balconies and bait attackers onto the center, because the conjunction turns the open floor into a timed kill zone no army can hold.
