BELIEF: Sirius is a danger to itself, unregistered and unwarded, so the Accord must extend its registries and protections before a worse fate finds it unprotected.

COST: the Sirian worlds are filed into mandates they never requested, their own governance deferred onto Luminous clerks for decades, their choices awaiting approval.

MORAL: people who only ever wanted to keep you safe can leave you with less of yourself than any enemy ever took.

BLIND SPOT: they still believe the Sirians should be grateful, and cannot see that gratitude was never the point of the paperwork.
