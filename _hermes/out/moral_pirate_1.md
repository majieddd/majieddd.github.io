BELIEF: We took the Pleiades because a road is only free if no one owns it. The Core taxed those lanes blind, so we keep them open for every crew.

COST: The station families who never flew a flag now pay us in salvage or sit dark. Quiet haulers who wanted only to eat got written into our toll rolls, refusers lost their drives.

MORAL: You do not stop being what you fled just because you ran. The captain who hated the taxman becomes him the moment he names the rate.

BLIND SPOT: We still call it the Free Roads while we decide who may pass, never asking what we became to those now paying us.
