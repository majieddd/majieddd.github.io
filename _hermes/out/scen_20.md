SCENARIO: The world orbits one star of a wide binary, so twin Zeta-2 hangs fixed in the same sky patch forever. When Zeta-1 sets and only the twin lights the pens, the shifted spectrum unlocks the pods and the captive units stir for a fixed span, then seal.

VISUAL: A hard white second sun frozen in the violet dome, casting twin shadows across the glowing rows of pods sunk in living chitin.

TACTICAL: The pods unlock on a fixed pulse from the twin's light, so the defender times a sealed wall or a sudden swarm on a schedule no attacker can change.
