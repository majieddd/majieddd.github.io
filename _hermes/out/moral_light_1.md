BELIEF: They believe registration is rescue. Logging a world in the Luminous Accord shields it, so bringing the Pleiades into the registry is mercy, not conquest.

COST: The Pleiades pay with their own choices. Every local decision now waits on a mandate filed light years away, and promised protections arrive decades late, if at all.

MORAL: Being cared for by someone who never asks what you want can feel more like being owned than being saved.

BLIND SPOT: They cannot see their registry is the wound, not the bandage, and that those they file away are less protected than shelved.
