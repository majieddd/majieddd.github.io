BELIEF: We took Pleiades because we hit first. They all knew Apophis was coming and said nothing. We hold this system so no one decides our end but us.

COST: The Pleiades colonists filed every Federation form and paid every levy. Our guns still came through their sky. They lost their homes and their children's names on a debt they never owed.

MORAL: People who have been betrayed do not grow careful. They grow certain that hurting first is the only honest language left.

BLIND SPOT: We call it conquest but we are building the same silent watchtower we hated. We cannot see we are becoming the ones who knew and did not warn.
