BELIEF: The Luminous Accord believes Apophis proved humanity cannot protect itself, so the Earth System must be filed under mandate before the next delivery arrives. They are not conquering. They are registering a system that needs protection.

COST: Earth's worlds enter the registries and every choice is flagged, reviewed, and deferred. A farmer's water right waits on a committee that meets in 2041. No one on Earth was asked.

MORAL: People hand over their freedom for the comfort of being kept safe, then name the cage a shelter.

BLIND SPOT: The Accord cannot see that its protection and its occupation are one act. It believes the mandate is a gift it has not yet finished unwrapping.
