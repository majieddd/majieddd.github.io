SCENARIO: Zeta Reticuli is a metal-poor binary, so the planet formed with almost no heavy elements. The vaults' faint glow comes from spectrum lamps keeping the banked lineages alive, and the only native metal for leagues is locked inside the honeycomb walls themselves.

VISUAL: Twin-sun light spills across a honeycomb of cells where bright metal threads run through the transparent walls like veins.

TACTICAL: A defender can shatter cells to free the metal for improvised barricades, but every broken cell kills a lineage the vault was built to keep.
