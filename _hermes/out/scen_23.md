SCENARIO: Zeta-2 b orbits Zeta-2, but sister star Zeta-1, a near twin sun, hangs bright overhead. The spire's membrane resonates only to the beat when both suns share the sky, singing on a double star clock no other world keeps.

VISUAL: A fused column of pale bodies and thin membrane flares as twin suns rise behind it, throwing crossing shadows across the plain.

TACTICAL: Defenders ride the spire's surge to blind optics and hide movement, but when the second sun sets the membrane falls silent and the column marks their line with one hard shadow.
