SCENARIO: Sterope sits inside the Pleiades reflection nebula, so fine dust perpetually sifts through the vault seams. Any door pried open reseals itself under fresh drift within hours, and the later the stamp date, the deeper it gets buried, inverting the vault order.

VISUAL: Row upon row of stamped iron doors stand half swallowed by glowing blue dust, the newest seals already vanishing beneath the oldest.

TACTICAL: A defender who opens a vault to use its pages must abandon it forever, since the entrance reseals and shifts position under settling dust before they can return.
