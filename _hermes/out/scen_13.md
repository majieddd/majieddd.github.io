SCENARIO: Merope plows through the Pleiades interstellar dust cloud at speed, so the light gardens sit wrapped in the blue Merope Nebula. Fine dust streams onto the hard-light panels endlessly, and where the system crosses a denser filament a blinding blue glare flares across every terrace.

VISUAL: Tier upon tier of seed terraces glow under arched hard-light panels while a vast blue dust haze pours between them toward the black sky.

TACTICAL: Dust veils and nebula flares blind long sightlines, so defenders fight terrace by terrace where the panels are the only cover and the light dies without warning.
