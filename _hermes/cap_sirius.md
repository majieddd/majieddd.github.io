1 SIRIUS A I: We found the garden still pruning itself, though nothing inside had drawn breath for a thousand years.

2 SIRIUS A II: The foundry never stopped pouring, and we ate the rations it stamped out without a face behind them.

3 THE ASH FIELD: They built the archive from the star's own shed skin, and the dust still settles on every name.

4 SIRIUS B I: They sealed the sick here and left the doors open; we never knew if it was mercy or forgetting.

5 THE DIAMOND SHELF: We walked the diamond shelf where they mend their husks, and the floor rang like a struck glass.

6 THE COMPANION: The Companion keeps passing the work down the line, as if the hands that took it still stood there.

7 THE DOG STAR: The orders all rise from the Dog Star, and we never once saw who signed them.
