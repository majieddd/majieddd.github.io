Arcturus is a K0 III red giant, about 25.4 times the Sun's radius and roughly 170 times as luminous. It lies some 36.7 light-years away. Peculiarly, it is an old, metal-poor Population II star on a highly inclined orbit, moving through the Milky Way at about 122 km/s; it belongs to the Arcturus stream, a group thought to be debris from a dwarf galaxy swallowed by the Galaxy long ago. No planets are confirmed around it. A substellar companion was once claimed from radial-velocity jitter, but that signal is unconfirmed and generally attributed to stellar pulsation rather than a planet, so the planet question remains unconfirmed.
