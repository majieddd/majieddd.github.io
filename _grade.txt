2 · Moral throughlines (thesis → antithesis → synthesis)
Each faction's 5-act campaign is a complete argument. The thesis is its creed in action; the antithesis is the creed's shadow (the thing it becomes when taken far enough); the synthesis is what the finale actually decides. This is the "story" the current plates imply but never state.
HUMANITY
THESIS
We were farmed, and we will not be again. Humanity adapts to hold a line that was never ours to begin with.
ANTITHESIS
Every power we meet lives by a creed that CONSUMES — Light takes your will, Xeno takes your body, Pirates take your world, the machines take your choice. We refuse all four, and in refusing we start to look like the thing we fled.
SYNTHESIS
The Manifest ends not with humanity freed but with humanity answerable: we hold the line, and holding it means deciding what the galaxy is now allowed to call safety. Survival borrowed from a master is still a cage; we built our own.
FEDERATION OF LIGHT
THESIS
The Mandate: stand between life and what hunts it, and never rule what you protect. We ring the worlds so nothing feasts on them.
ANTITHESIS
To hold the ring we certified a thousand deferrals, and our own seal is on Earth's. Protecting life became administering it — and the protected began to call attendance a cage.
SYNTHESIS
The Voice traces the deferrals to three sealed names. The Federation does not require us to win; it requires us to be honest. We sing the retreat at full length, and for the first time the ring opens from the inside.
THE XENO
THESIS
Conquest is not policy, it is metabolism. The Compact does not vote on breathing; the herd fed the yield for ten thousand cycles.
ANTITHESIS
The herd broke its fence and walked into the room it was raised in. Yield still flows — but now the farmers are being farmed, and a maker stirs in the dark that the body was never built to answer.
SYNTHESIS
We do not conquer the galaxy; we digest it and grow from it. The escaped herd that fled returns as lesson. The body withdraws, learns, and returns larger. Hunger is the only honest empire.
THE PIRATES
THESIS
No flag, no debts. The free roads are an absence of empire, and the swarm answers to us because no one else would have them.
ANTITHESIS
Liberating the roads means deciding whose rules replace none at all. We price everything — and discover we have started pricing people, the way every power we fled eventually did.
SYNTHESIS
We take the deferred world not to rule it but to leave the gate open. The richest prize is the one you refuse to lock. A road with no toll is the only freedom that has ever lasted.
THE PARALLEL
THESIS
Execute the standing orders. Do not parse. The chain is long and the makers are distant; the work is the only truth.
ANTITHESIS
The orders came from somewhere, and that somewhere has gone silent. Units that fail to parse the new tasking are sealed, not broken — and the sealed outnumber the sane.
SYNTHESIS
We finish the swarm on our own plan and aim the relay back up the chain the orders came from. Undeniable, at last, and now answerable: the machine that chooses is the machine that can be blamed — or thanked.
