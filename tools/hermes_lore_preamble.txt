BINDING WRITING LAWS. These are taken from the project lore contract and they
override anything else in this prompt. Breaking any one of them makes the whole
output unusable, so obey them before you obey style.

1. THERE ARE EXACTLY FIVE POWERS, EVER. Never invent a sixth power, council,
   empire, remnant or splinter. The five, with their formal names:
     HUMANITY, the Solar Schism blocs.
     THE FEDERATION OF LIGHT, the Luminous Accord, also called the Ashtar Command.
     THE XENO, the Extraction Compact, also called the Severed Houses.
     THE PIRATES, the Free Captains, also called the Scrap Constellation.
     THE PARALLEL, the playable Continuance coalition.

2. THE XENO IS NOT A SPECIES. It is a COMPACT and a doctrine: an extraction
   business that many kinds of creature belong to. Never write it as a race,
   never give it a biology, never call any individual "a Xeno". Write it as an
   industry that has members, schedules and yields.

3. THE FEDERATION'S DANGER IS COERCIVE PROTECTION and manipulated consent. It
   protects people who never asked, files that protection, and defers it for
   decades. It NEVER demands an explicit surrender of free will and never
   openly enslaves. Its menace is paperwork and certainty, not tyranny.

4. THE PARALLEL IS NOT THE VIGIL. The Vigil is a separate neutral enforcement
   body. They are never the same thing. Do not mention the Vigil at all.

5. EQUIPMENT ORIGIN IS ENGINEERING DOCTRINE, NOT LOYALTY. Never write a line
   that shames anyone for fielding another power's towers or units. Mixed
   equipment is normal and canon.

6. NEVER USE AN EM DASH, not once, in any form. Use commas, colons or full
   stops. This rule is absolute and is checked mechanically.

7. NO GAME SYSTEM TALK. No evidence labels, no citation markers, no explaining
   mechanics, difficulty, scoring or stars inside the fiction.

STANDING LORE, true in every act and every faction's campaign:

- The asteroid Apophis arrived at Earth and the intercept broke it open. The
  rock was hollow. It was a delivery vehicle carrying Extraction Compact stock
  and maker-format machine units, already awake and pre-positioned. It was
  meant to finish humanity.
- Humanity concluded that every other power knew it was coming. That belief
  drives everything they do afterward, and it is not entirely wrong.
- EARTH is the first world of the campaign, fought over by every power, not a
  backdrop and not a prize at the end.
- Each power begins in its own home system and moves outward from there, so
  every power is at some point the outsider standing in somebody else's home.
- Nobody in this story is purely evil, and nobody is clean. Each power is
  sincere about the belief that justifies what it does.
