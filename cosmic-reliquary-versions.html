LATESTFIXEDv1.3 Shading, shadows, dead features, and a mote colour bug
  • Fixed after ship: ambient dust was painting the whole board. The dust motes read palette.key, the hex STRING kept on the palette for CSS ("#38e8ff"), instead of palette.keyRgb, the 0..1 array. Indexing a string returns single hex-digit characters: key[0] is "#", which coerces to NaN, and key[1]/key[2] are numeral characters like "3" and "8" that coerce to the integers 3 and 8, not the fractions those digits represent. Every one of 190 small, partly-NaN, roughly ten-times-over-range motes blew out the bloom threshold, and the blur spread each into a wash big enough that 190 overlapping washes covered nearly the whole board in a shifting colour haze, identically for all five factions. Confirmed by reading a live particle straight off the GPU buffer (green landed at 1.467, blue at 2.936, red at NaN), fixed at the source, and gated: every faction's motes are now populated and checked finite and in range, with the original bug planted back in to confirm the gate actually catches it (2,760 bad readings) before confirming the fix clears it.
  • The wet specular was never running. Its exponent was 90 against a shader threshold of 0.35, which needs the half vector within 8.7 degrees of the surface normal. Every mesh here has flat per-face normals, so there is no gradient for a lobe that narrow to land on. Switching the whole term off changed the frame by 0.0001. Swept against v1.2 in one browser session: the picture is now 1.8% brighter, with 4.9% more micro-contrast and 41.6% more overall contrast, all at once.
  • Shadows are separate from form. They were being computed and then folded into the same posterise as the light ramp, so a shadowed surface quantised to the band it was already on and the shadow vanished. Form and shadow are now two ramp lookups blended by a soft mask, with nine-tap rotated filtering and a shadow box fitted to the board rather than chasing the camera.
  • Paint has height. The texture atlas spent a quarter of itself storing the number 255. That channel now carries an impasto ridge mask, and the sheen sits on the crests instead of floating over them.
  • Soft particles, ambient air, frustum culling. Every particle quad now fades into what it meets instead of ending in a hard line; the space between camera and board carries drifting dust; zoomed in on a full board, 196 draws becomes 122 with zero pixels differing from the unculled frame.
  • Every tower is tested for what makes it different, not just that it deals damage. Found VAULT shipping a NaN in its own stat block, fixed the same way.
