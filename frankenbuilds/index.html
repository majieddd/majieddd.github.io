Price history

Every tracked component is re-priced from its live listing once a day. Each card plots the asking price over time (the price-shift dynamics) and the same series normalized to the unit you actually buy — $/GB of VRAM for a GPU, $/GB of RAM for memory, $/W for a supply, $/GPU-slot for a board, frame, riser or shroud — so a $75 card and a $22 board can be compared on what is getting more expensive.

Hover a chart to read a day's exact price. The dashed line is the series average. A flat chart means the component has only been recorded for one day so far — the history grows one point per day.

Throughput formula: total = (GPU HBM bandwidth × GPU count) + system DDR bandwidth. DDR = channels × 8 bytes (64-bit channel) × MT/s ÷ 1000. 8-channel DDR4-2400 = 153.6 GB/s; 16-channel DDR4-2666 = 341.1 GB/s.
