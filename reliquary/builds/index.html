LATESTv1.2 Performance and locomotion
  • Legs actually work. The IK euler conversion was a half turn out in two places. The errors cancel for axis-aligned directions, so bones pointing straight down looked right and nothing threw, but for any other direction the vertical component inverted. Measured on a strider whose whole leg is 2.88 units: the gap between the requested foot position and the delivered one was up to 5.01 units. The legs were not walking badly, they were in arbitrary poses. Now 0.000.
  • Feet are nailed to the ground. The gait rate is derived from stride rather than hand-tuned, and a planted foot solves to the world point where it actually landed. Foot travel against body travel per frame went from 0.45 to 0.52 down to 0.00.
  • Much faster. Draw time 5.62ms to 2.1ms: small units no longer redraw into the shadow map (151 shadow draws to 61), per-item uniforms packed into two vec4s, and tower stats cached instead of rebuilt 120 times a second each.
  • Adaptive resolution. Device pixel ratio capped at 1.5 and the render scale walks itself toward a 60fps budget, so the post chain stops asking a 4K panel for four times the pixels.
  • Brighter. The lit end of the ramp carries it rather than exposure, so the picture gains light and contrast at the same time instead of going grey.
