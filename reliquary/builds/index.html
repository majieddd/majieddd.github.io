LATESTv1.3 Shading, shadows and dead features
  • The wet specular was never running. Its exponent was 90 against a shader threshold of 0.35, which needs the half vector within 8.7 degrees of the surface normal. Every mesh here has flat per-face normals, so there is no gradient for a lobe that narrow to land on. Switching the whole term off changed the frame by 0.0001. One of the three pillars of the art direction had been invisible since the first build. Swept against v1.2 in one browser session: the picture is now 1.8% brighter, with 4.9% more micro-contrast and 41.6% more overall contrast, all at once.
  • Shadows are separate from form. They were being computed and then folded into the same posterise as the light ramp, so a shadowed surface quantised to the band it was already on and the shadow vanished. Form and shadow are now two ramp lookups blended by a soft mask, with nine-tap rotated filtering and a shadow box fitted to the board rather than chasing the camera.
  • Paint has height. The texture atlas spent a quarter of itself storing the number 255. That channel now carries an impasto ridge mask, folded out of the high-frequency octave, and the sheen sits on the crests instead of floating over them.
  • Soft particles. Every quad used to end in a hard straight line where it met the ground. The fade reads the depth the opaque pass already writes.
  • Air. A recycled pool of drifting motes, so the space between the camera and the board is a place rather than a vacuum.
  • Frustum culling. Zoomed in on a full board, 196 draws becomes 122, and zero pixels differ from the unculled frame.
  • Every tower is tested for what makes it different. Not just that it deals damage: that cryo slows, that mortar cannot hit air, that flak does more to air than ground, that maw executes, that railgun pierces. It found VAULT shipping a NaN.
  • Turrets scan when idle, units breathe when held, and the dead collapse rather than dissolving at attention.

v1.2Performance and locomotion
  • Legs actually work. The IK euler conversion was a half turn out in two places. The errors cancel for axis-aligned directions, so bones pointing straight down looked right and nothing threw, but for any other direction the vertical component inverted. Measured on a strider whose whole leg is 2.88 units: the gap between the requested foot position and the delivered one was up to 5.01 units. The legs were not walking badly, they were in arbitrary poses. Now 0.000.
  • Feet are nailed to the ground. The gait rate is derived from stride rather than hand-tuned, and a planted foot solves to the world point where it actually landed. Foot travel against body travel per frame went from 0.45 to 0.52 down to 0.00.
  • Much faster. Draw time 5.62ms to 2.1ms: small units no longer redraw into the shadow map (151 shadow draws to 61), per-item uniforms packed into two vec4s, and tower stats cached instead of rebuilt 120 times a second each.
  • Adaptive resolution. Device pixel ratio capped at 1.5 and the render scale walks itself toward a 60fps budget, so the post chain stops asking a 4K panel for four times the pixels.
  • Brighter. The lit end of the ramp carries it rather than exposure, so the picture gains light and contrast at the same time instead of going grey.
