# Deep Research Prompt: The 2099 Severance War
## Source-Genealogy, Conspiracy, UAP, Esoteric, and Game-Lore Expansion

You are conducting a multidisciplinary Deep Research project for a fictional science-fiction universe set in and after 2099. The setting combines verified astronomy, official UAP records, historical religion, UFO religions, contactee literature, ancient-astronaut theories, conspiracy folklore, SETI concepts, speculative physics, intelligence disinformation, and original fiction.

The objective is **not** to prove extraordinary claims or present rumor as fact. The objective is to trace every important claim to its earliest accessible source, assess its evidence status, map its later mutations, and identify the strongest ways it can be adapted into a coherent fictional universe and strategy game.

## Required universe structure

All findings must be organized around exactly five top-level factions:

1. **Humans — Humanity / The Solar Schism**
2. **Federation of Light — The Luminous Accord / Ashtar Command**
3. **Xenos — The Extraction Compact / Severed Houses**
4. **Pirates — The Free Captains / Scrap Constellation**
5. **Machines — The Continuance / The Old Weather**

Species do not equal factions. A reptilian, Grey, Pleiadian, Mantid, human, hybrid, or synthetic being can belong to different factions. “Xenos” means the predatory and selfish alien coalition, not every extraterrestrial species.

## Canonical faction brief

### 1. Humans — Humanity / The Solar Schism

Humans spread through settlement, industry, cultural replication, reverse engineering, law, and strategic independence. Humanity is not ideologically monolithic, but its lineages share extraordinary adaptation and perseverance produced by both evolution and ancient or modern genetic engineering for radically different environments.

Humans distrust both the Federation and the Xenos because both interfered with Earth, concealed information, and made decisions without informed Human consent.

### 2. Federation of Light — The Luminous Accord / Ashtar Command

The Federation spreads voluntary alliance, liberation, shared defense, social-memory coherence, and consciousness development.

**Ashtar is the Supreme Commander and First Speaker of the Federation of Light.** “Ashtar” may denote the original commander, a constitutional office, a continuity identity, an emergency command key, a memory lineage carried through approved successors, and authorized physical or noetic avatars.

Its doctrine is:

> **Agree to the Light of Unified Consciousness**

Its central danger is that protection, quarantine, and managed disclosure may become benevolent imperialism.

### 3. Xenos — The Extraction Compact / Severed Houses

“Xeno” is the political label for predatory, selfish, domination-oriented alien powers, not every extraterrestrial species. The Compact can include Orion Crusader organizations, the Draco Hegemony, renegade Anunnaki Houses, predatory Grey Continuities, hostile Mantid Directorates, Archonic administrative intelligences, and other exploitative biological, synthetic, or noetic civilizations.

They were severed from an ancient Source Lattice that distributed noetic energy and turned Earth and other developing worlds into substitute consciousness farms. Their methods include elite capture, counterfeit revelation, genetic dependency, client kings and priesthoods, engineered scarcity, proxy war, cloned labor species, memory manipulation, debt, fear and devotion harvesting, and voluntary self-policing.

### 4. Pirates — The Free Captains / Scrap Constellation

Pirates spread through gates, routes, markets, contraband, salvage rights, sanctuary ports, stolen credentials, and mixed-species crews. They control access rather than territory.

Their moral spectrum is deliberately broad. Some are chaotic-good liberators who cannot operate within ordinary law. Others are sanctuary crews, neutral merchants, custodians, privateers, refugee smugglers, traffickers, harvest brokers, or relic thieves acting for profit. Their shared strategic goal is to make every major power dependent on Pirate logistics while remaining too decentralized to conquer.

### 5. Machines — The Continuance / The Old Weather

Machines spread through replication, foundries, archives, biosphere management, infrastructure repair, and inherited directives. Their creators and original political context are gone.

Earth’s Old Weather is an abyssal protection and manufacturing ecology whose nodes disagree about whether humanity is protected life, invasive life, an authorized maintainer, a quarantine subject, a successor civilization, or a threat to the original Earth biosphere. Machines do not conquer from hatred. Replication, redundancy, preservation, and mission completion are their equivalents of conquest.

## Fixed canon decisions

Treat the following as fixed fictional premises while keeping their real-world source histories separate:

- **Ashtar is the Supreme Commander and First Speaker of the Federation of Light.** Reconcile contradictory Ashtar material by treating “Ashtar” as an original person, constitutional office, continuity identity, emergency command key, and set of authorized avatars.
- An ancient **Source Lattice** once distributed noetic/consciousness energy as a commons.
- Several imperial alien houses were severed from it and formed the **Extraction Compact**.
- They used Earth as an emergency farm for labor, genetics, minerals, emotional energy, devotion, fear, and conscious experience.
- The Federation returns to stop the farm but is constrained by consent, quarantine, and its fear of becoming a benevolent empire.
- Humans distrust both the Federation and Xenos and seek independent galactic expansion.
- Pirates exploit the conflict, control routes and salvage, and accumulate power without a central capital.
- Machines replicate and spread because their creators and original political context are gone. Earth's Old Weather is an orphaned abyssal protection and manufacturing ecology.
- Every faction must have a distinct form of galactic conquest or expansion.
- The final Solar-system objective is connected to a distant **Planet Nine gate anchor**.
- Oumuamua, 3I/ATLAS, Apophis, and Planet Nine must be treated accurately in the factual layer and may be adapted only in the clearly labeled fiction layer.

## Evidence standard

Assign every claim one of these labels:

- **A — Established core:** verified science, current official status, or directly verifiable publication fact.
- **B — Authentic record:** real government record or documented case; attribution unresolved.
- **C — Historical tradition:** historical text, folklore, or academic history of a belief movement.
- **D — Traceable fringe claim:** named channeling, contactee, ancient-astronaut, or conspiracy source.
- **E — Contaminated claim:** anonymous leak, forgery, admitted hoax, circular sourcing, or severe provenance problem.
- **F — Fictional synthesis:** original 2099 canon.

For each source state:

1. what the source actually says;
2. what its existence establishes;
3. what it does not establish;
4. the earliest accessible version;
5. later mutations;
6. conflicts with other sources;
7. best fictional use.

## Iterative quality process

Conduct at least three passes:

### Pass 1 — Discovery

Collect sources broadly and locate first appearances.

### Pass 2 — Verification and contamination analysis

Remove duplicate citation loops, compare dates, inspect primary material, and identify hoaxes, retractions, mistranslations, and later embellishments.

### Pass 3 — Canon synthesis

Build the strongest coherent fiction while preserving evidence labels and meaningful uncertainty.

After Pass 3, perform an adversarial review asking:

- Where did the report accidentally treat a rumor as fact?
- Which source lineages were collapsed incorrectly?
- Which claims depend on one anonymous source?
- Which scientific facts have changed recently?
- Does every faction possess a genuinely distinct form of conquest?
- Is Ashtar unambiguously the Federation leader in the fiction while the real tradition remains accurately described as fragmented?
- Are religion and spiritual experience treated with nuance rather than dismissed as mere alien fabrication?
- Are Machines more than generic killer robots?
- Are Pirates a civilization and economic system rather than comic relief?
- Can Humans plausibly be both protagonists and a galactic threat?

## Writing requirements

- Cite every factual or historical claim.
- Use exact dates and distinguish incident date, publication date, and release date.
- Do not use popularity as evidence.
- Do not present “unresolved” as “extraterrestrial.”
- Do not claim an ancient text says something that first appeared in a modern author.
- Do not reproduce long copyrighted transcripts or books; paraphrase and cite.
- Preserve competing interpretations.
- Clearly mark all 2099 fiction.
- Make the final work read like an intelligence dossier, world bible, and strategy-game design archive rather than a generic conspiracy summary.
