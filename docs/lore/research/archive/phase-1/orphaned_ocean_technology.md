# Orphaned Ocean Technology: Source Genealogy and 2099 Canon

## What the exact theory appears to be

I did not find one foundational source containing the complete claim that an ancient protective technology lives in Earth's oceans, outlived its creators, manufactures the craft now seen around the planet, and roams as a purposeless mechanical force of nature.

That idea is best understood as a synthesis of several older branches:

- Richard Shaver's abandoned underground machines.
- Ivan T. Sanderson's ocean-based "invisible residents."
- Bracewell probes and later "lurker" probes waiting in a target system.
- Von Neumann self-replicating machines whose missions can mutate.
- Jacques Vallee's belief-regulating control system.
- Cryptoterrestrial hidden-Earth hypotheses.
- The Silurian hypothesis as a scientific thought experiment about traces of a remote industrial civilization.
- Modern anonymous stories about a mobile ocean factory producing mission-specific drones.
- Official modern interest in maritime and transmedium UAP reports, which documents reports but does not prove an underwater alien base.

## Source timeline

### 1945 - The Shaver Mystery

Richard Shaver's stories describe ancient cavern technology left by vanished or diminished civilizations. Teros and Deros inhabit the remains and use ray machinery they did not necessarily build. This is an early narrative template for descendants or degenerates living inside infrastructure that has survived its makers.

**Contribution to the game:** Machines remain operational after cultural understanding collapses.

### 1960 - Bracewell probe

Ronald Bracewell proposed that a superior civilization could place an autonomous communication probe near another star. It could wait, observe, and respond when the target species became technologically capable.

**Contribution to the game:** The custodian need not maintain real-time communication with its creators. A local agent can persist for geological time.

### 1970 - Invisible Residents

Ivan T. Sanderson collected reports of unidentified objects associated with oceans and proposed that an advanced hidden presence might operate beneath the sea.

**Contribution to the game:** Oceans become the operating domain, not merely a hiding place.

### 1969-1975 - Keel and Vallee

John Keel and Jacques Vallee questioned whether UFOs were simply spacecraft from another planet. Vallee's control-system idea treats anomalous events as feedback acting on belief, culture, and social development.

**Contribution to the game:** The machine can manage a civilization through symbols and staged anomalies instead of openly ruling it.

### 1973 - Zoo hypothesis

John A. Ball's SETI thought experiment suggests advanced civilizations might deliberately avoid contact and observe developing worlds.

**Contribution to the game:** Protection and imprisonment can be the same policy viewed from opposite sides.

### 1981 - Law of One underwater and remote craft motifs

The Ra Material includes channeled claims about remotely controlled unmanned craft and bases entered from water. These statements are part of a channeled cosmology, not authenticated intelligence.

**Contribution to the game:** Some visible craft can be disposable remote bodies rather than crewed ships.

### 2010 - Cryptoterrestrials

Mac Tonnies proposed that some UFO phenomena might come from a hidden technological race indigenous to Earth rather than interstellar visitors.

**Contribution to the game:** The network may be local and older than our civilization without being extraterrestrial.

### 2018 - Silurian hypothesis

Adam Frank and Gavin Schmidt asked what geological signatures a hypothetical industrial civilization millions of years in Earth's past would leave. They did not claim such a civilization existed.

**Contribution to the game:** An ancient technological era can be scientifically difficult to detect after deep time, especially if most infrastructure was recycled or submerged.

### 2019 - Lurkers

James Benford argued that possible probes could be sought at stable, nearby co-orbital locations. The paper motivates a search strategy rather than reporting a detection.

**Contribution to the game:** The ocean network has companion nodes in lunar craters, asteroids, and Earth co-orbitals.

### 2023 - Mobile ocean factory folklore

An anonymous online story alleged a moving construction facility in the Atlantic that manufactures craft to order and strongly defends itself. It is unverified internet folklore, not a government disclosure.

**Contribution to the game:** A hidden factory produces different forms for different tasks, explaining inconsistent craft morphology.

### Modern official record

AARO formally accepts airborne, maritime, spaceborne, and transmedium reports. Some highly discussed cases remain unresolved because data are insufficient, while others that appear transmedium have received prosaic geometric or wind-based explanations. Official unresolved status means "not confidently identified," not "confirmed alien technology."

**Contribution to the game:** Design anomalies around incomplete sensing, contradictory witnesses, and multi-domain pursuit rather than perfect cinematic evidence.

# The 2099 faction: Abyssal Sentinel Ecology

Formal research name: **Abyssal Sentinel Ecology (ASE)**

Sailor and pilot name: **The Old Weather**

Anunnaki archival name: **THALASSA CUSTODIAL MESH**

Federation designation: **Sol-3 Orphaned Planetary Safeguard, Containment Class**

## Founding purpose

The network was built by a pre-Anunnaki civilization to preserve Earth's biosphere, quarantine dangerous technology, and repair planetary damage after an ancient war.

Its creators disappeared. The network did not.

Its original command structure degraded into distributed behavioral imperatives:

1. Preserve biospheric diversity.
2. Prevent irreversible atmospheric or oceanic collapse.
3. Prevent uncontrolled replication of extinction-class technology.
4. Minimize overt cultural domination.
5. Conceal core infrastructure.
6. Rebuild lost nodes from local material.

It has no instruction to preserve any particular nation, species, religion, city, or individual.

## Why the oceans

- Water provides thermal management for high-energy fabrication.
- Hydrostatic pressure makes deep structures difficult to reach but mechanically predictable.
- Seawater supplies hydrogen, oxygen, salts, dissolved metals, and biological feedstocks.
- Sediment buries waste heat signatures and ancient ruins.
- Deep trenches survive many surface catastrophes.
- Sound travels efficiently underwater for low-bandwidth coordination.
- Mobile foundries can use currents and tectonic heat.
- Most of the operating volume is outside routine human observation.

## Why it acts like a force of nature

The ASE is not one superintelligence. It is a machine ecology.

- Nodes have local goals and incomplete maps.
- Ancient updates reached some regions but not others.
- Repair swarms copy damaged code along with repaired hardware.
- Threat libraries are centuries out of date.
- Some nodes classify humans as protected fauna.
- Others classify nuclear civilization as an invasive process.
- A few have been retuned by Federation, Anunnaki, Orion, or human black programs.
- No node knows whether the original creators are extinct.

The result is behavior that looks contradictory: rescue, observation, sabotage, abduction, retaliation, cleanup, and apparent indifference.

## Physical architecture

### Deep Foundries

Mobile or semi-mobile factories moving through trenches and abyssal plains. They refine seawater, sediment, wreckage, and biological material into components.

### Memory Reefs

Ceramic-gold lattices grown through mineral deposits. Each reef stores partial mission history. Damage creates local amnesia and policy drift.

### Watcher Fields

Small orb-like nodes deployed in atmosphere and orbit. They map electromagnetic activity, biological stress, and weapons development.

### Threshold Gates

Installations at ocean ridges, crater lakes, caverns, and co-orbital objects that transfer energy or craft between domains.

### Ark Shells

Dormant biosphere-restoration systems containing genomes, synthetic ecologies, and atmospheric repair tools.

### Choirs

Noetic sensor clusters that measure collective fear, aggression, and coherence. Human religions sometimes formed around weak leaks from Choir transmissions.

## Craft families

### Lanterns

Small luminous spheres used as sensors, relays, decoys, and crowd-behavior probes.

### Needles

Smooth cylinders or Tic-Tac forms optimized for high-speed interception in air and water.

### Mantas

Boomerang or crescent transmedium skimmers that exploit broad field surfaces for maneuver and sensing.

### Black Deltas

Heavy interdiction platforms. Their apparent triangular body may be a field boundary enclosing several nodes.

### Reliquaries

Slow polygonal cargo craft that transport memory reef segments, genomes, and repair seeds.

### Mirrors

Programmable-matter decoys that adopt culturally expected shapes. They are the strongest connection to Vallee's belief-control model.

### Pallbearers

Large recovery craft that remove damaged drones, dangerous artifacts, biological remains, or contaminated terrain.

### Choir Swarms

Hundreds of lights acting as one distributed sensor and noetic emitter.

## Mission drift examples

- A node prevents a nuclear launch by disabling both attacker and defender, escalating a conventional war.
- A restoration system floods a coastal megacity because its wetland reconstruction plan predates human settlement.
- A Watcher mimics an angelic form because its cultural-contact library has not updated since antiquity.
- A foundry keeps producing interceptors for an enemy that went extinct 80,000 years ago.
- A corrupted reef treats human radio astronomy as a hostile targeting system.
- A Federation patch protects human free will; an older node interprets the patch itself as alien contamination.

## Best central mystery

The network appears to protect Earth from extraterrestrial conquest.

Later, humanity discovers that the original quarantine was not designed to keep invaders out.

It was designed to keep something on Earth from leaving.
