# UAP + Xeno Lore Research Pack

Version: 2026-08-21

## Purpose

This pack expands the earlier Anunnaki, Galactic Federation, consciousness-harvest, and Law of One dossier into a source-traceable game-lore toolkit. It includes:

- 55 xeno/entity archetypes in CSV, JSON, and readable Markdown.
- A source map for alien-created religion and belief-control theories.
- A research genealogy for abandoned or autonomous ocean technology.
- A craft-design bible grounded in official UAP morphology categories, while clearly separating reports from proof of alien craft.
- A unified 2099 lore expansion.
- A manifest of official United States UAP archives.
- Bash and PowerShell downloaders that point to the exact National Archives ZIP URLs.

The phrase "chicken technology" in the request was treated as likely speech-to-text for "ancient technology" or "alien technology." I did not find a recognized UFO theory by the literal name "chicken technology."

## Evidence labels used throughout

- HISTORICAL MYTH/RELIGION: a real old text or religious tradition, not a verified alien account.
- CONTACTEE/CHANNELED CLAIM: attributed communication without independent verification.
- EXPERIENCER/ABDUCTION TESTIMONY: reported personal experience; not independently established species data.
- HOAX/INTERNET FOLKLORE: admitted, strongly evidenced, or anonymously circulated narrative.
- SCIENTIFIC HYPOTHESIS: a legitimate thought experiment or research proposal, not a detection.
- OFFICIAL RECORD: an authentic government record. Authenticity of the document does not authenticate every claim quoted inside it.
- OFFICIAL UNRESOLVED OBSERVATION: insufficiently identified observation, not proof of extraterrestrial origin.
- ORIGINAL FICTION SYNTHESIS: new worldbuilding assembled from multiple traditions.

## Official archive download note

The actual National Archives ZIP files are not bundled in this pack. The complete collection is far too large, with some Project Blue Book sets split into files of roughly 60-81 GB each, and this environment blocks direct ingestion of ZIP content. Instead, the pack includes exact official URLs, a size-tiered manifest, and resumable download scripts.

The starter script selection is approximately 210 MB. The medium set is roughly 1.84 GB. The full set is hundreds of gigabytes and requires a typed confirmation token.

## Core worldbuilding recommendation

Treat "species," "body type," and "faction" as different things.

A Grey body can be a manufactured interface used by several governments. A reptilian citizen can oppose Draco. A Nordic appearance can be a diplomatic projection. A Federation member can be morally compromised. A machine can preserve life while killing people. That complexity is what makes the setting feel like a universe rather than a monster manual.
