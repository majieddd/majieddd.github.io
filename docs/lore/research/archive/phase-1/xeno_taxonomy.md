# Xeno Taxonomy: 55 Lore Archetypes

## How to use this catalog

This is a source-genealogy catalog, not a claim that 55 verified extraterrestrial species exist. Each entry is labeled by source status. For strong worldbuilding, keep five fields separate:

1. Morphology: what body or interface is seen.
2. Ontology: biological, artificial, noetic, temporal, planetary, or folkloric.
3. Origin: off-world, Earth-native, future, parallel reality, or unknown.
4. Polity: Federation, Orion, Anunnaki House, independent state, pirate network, or machine ecology.
5. Ethics: service-to-others, service-to-self, mixed, nonmoral, or incomprehensible.

This prevents the common worldbuilding failure in which every reptilian is evil, every Nordic is benevolent, and every Grey belongs to one empire.

## Ancient and ancient-astronaut lineages

### X01 - Historical Anunna / Anunnaki

**Ontology:** Ancient divine assembly

**Source lineage:** Mesopotamian religion (Ancient Mesopotamia)

**Key sources:** Sumerian and Akkadian texts; Atrahasis; Enuma Elish; ORACC deity corpus

**Core lore:** A changing group of major gods, fate judges, or underworld deities; not a fixed alien species. Divine authority; labor hierarchy; mythic precursor to later ancient-astronaut reinterpretation.

**Evidence/source status:** Historical myth/religion, not evidence of extraterrestrials

**2099 adaptation:** Use as the corrupted human memory of several rival off-world houses rather than one species.

**Visual language:** Royal seals, star maps, lapis, gold, monumental geometry.

**Faction relationship:** Precursor to Anunnaki Houses

**Caution:** Do not claim cuneiform texts say aliens mined gold or came from a 3,600-year planet.

### X02 - Nibiran Anunnaki

**Ontology:** Biological extraterrestrial / dynastic empire

**Source lineage:** Zecharia Sitchin ancient-astronaut canon (1976 onward)

**Key sources:** The 12th Planet; Earth Chronicles; The Lost Book of Enki

**Core lore:** Long-lived technological beings from Nibiru; gold-dependent infrastructure; genetic intervention in early humans. Ancient colonizers, resource empire, rival dynasties, creators or modifiers of humanity.

**Evidence/source status:** Modern fringe interpretation, unsupported by mainstream Assyriology or astronomy

**2099 adaptation:** A declining resource empire whose noble houses disagree over whether humanity is property, heir, or threat.

**Visual language:** Gold field lattices, mobile planetary citadels, bio-royal insignia.

**Faction relationship:** Anunnaki Houses

**Caution:** Separate Sitchin lore from actual Mesopotamian translations.

### X03 - Igigi

**Ontology:** Divine labor caste / possible client species

**Source lineage:** Atrahasis labor revolt; later ancient-astronaut expansion (Ancient myth; modern reinterpretation)

**Key sources:** Atrahasis; modern Sitchin-derived lore

**Core lore:** Lower-ranking gods assigned exhausting labor who rebel before humans assume the burden. Mutinous workers, colonial soldiers, abandoned client species.

**Evidence/source status:** Historical myth with modern alien reinterpretation

**2099 adaptation:** A former Anunnaki labor caste that became nomadic, pirate, or revolutionary.

**Visual language:** Industrial exoskeletons, scarred utility craft, repurposed divine technology.

**Faction relationship:** Former Anunnaki subjects

**Caution:** The ancient text does not identify them as extraterrestrial miners.

## Biological/contactee species and star nations

### X04 - Short Greys

**Ontology:** Biological, synthetic, or remote avatar

**Source lineage:** Abduction literature and popular culture (1960s onward)

**Key sources:** Betty and Barney Hill case; Missing Time; Communion; later experiencer literature

**Core lore:** Small bodies, large heads and black eyes; emotionally opaque; often operate in groups. Technicians, abductors, drones, scouts, medical operators.

**Evidence/source status:** Experiencer testimony and folklore; no verified species

**2099 adaptation:** Make the Grey form a standardized interface body leased or manufactured by multiple factions.

**Visual language:** Featureless surfaces, surgical light, seamless egg-shaped interiors.

**Faction relationship:** Variable; Orion, neutral syndicates, or independent machine ecology

**Caution:** Avoid presenting one visual stereotype as an established extraterrestrial species.

### X05 - Tall Greys

**Ontology:** Biological or command-caste avatar

**Source lineage:** Later abduction narratives (1980s-1990s onward)

**Key sources:** Budd Hopkins, David Jacobs, John Mack and experiencer accounts

**Core lore:** Taller and more expressive than short Greys; frequently described as supervisors. Command caste, telepathic interrogator, program administrator.

**Evidence/source status:** Experiencer lore, unverified

**2099 adaptation:** A diplomatic or command shell whose apparent authority may be software permissions rather than rank.

**Visual language:** Elongated proportions, reflective black eyes, sparse regalia.

**Faction relationship:** Hybridization programs or machine polities

**Caution:** Treat testimony as testimony rather than biological taxonomy.

### X06 - Zeta Reticulans

**Ontology:** Attributed star-origin population

**Source lineage:** Hill star-map interpretation and later Grey lore (1960s-1970s onward)

**Key sources:** Marjorie Fish star-map reconstruction; abduction literature

**Core lore:** Greys attributed to the Zeta Reticuli system. Origin label for Grey visitors.

**Evidence/source status:** Speculative attribution; star-map interpretation is disputed

**2099 adaptation:** Use Zeta as an old human navigation error that became a political name adopted by the beings themselves.

**Visual language:** Binary star heraldry, twin-route navigation motifs.

**Faction relationship:** Variable

**Caution:** Do not treat a contested star-map match as confirmed provenance.

### X07 - Nordics / Space Brothers

**Ontology:** Humanoid extraterrestrial or projected persona

**Source lineage:** 1950s contactee movement (1952 onward)

**Key sources:** George Adamski, Flying Saucers Have Landed; early Space Brother contactees

**Core lore:** Humanlike, often tall and idealized; preach peace, nuclear restraint, and spiritual maturity. Benevolent emissary, moral teacher, Federation diplomat.

**Evidence/source status:** Contactee claim / UFO religion

**2099 adaptation:** Their human appearance is a negotiated diplomatic interface, not proof of shared ancestry.

**Visual language:** Retro-futurist silver craft, solar motifs, austere uniforms.

**Faction relationship:** Federation-aligned in many traditions

**Caution:** The early contactee corpus often reflects Cold War-era racial and cultural assumptions.

### X08 - Pleiadians / Plejaren

**Ontology:** Humanoid extraterrestrial / spiritual teacher

**Source lineage:** Billy Meier and New Age channeling (1970s-1990s onward)

**Key sources:** Meier contact claims; Bringers of the Dawn by Barbara Marciniak

**Core lore:** Humanlike star people linked to ecological warning, consciousness growth, and genetic ancestry. Teacher, ancestral cousin, ascension guide.

**Evidence/source status:** Contactee/channeling lore, unverified

**2099 adaptation:** A diaspora of post-war humanlike cultures with internal disagreement over intervention.

**Visual language:** Crystalline navigation, harmonic interfaces, pastoral high technology.

**Faction relationship:** Often Federation-adjacent

**Caution:** Different Pleiadian canons contradict one another.

### X09 - Tall Whites

**Ontology:** Humanoid extraterrestrial

**Source lineage:** Charles Hall contact memoirs (2002 onward)

**Key sources:** Millennial Hospitality series

**Core lore:** Very tall pale humanoids, long-lived, pragmatic, technologically advanced, allegedly near military facilities. Secret treaty partner, aloof neighbor, technology broker.

**Evidence/source status:** Personal claim, unverified

**2099 adaptation:** A commercially minded neutral polity that treats Earth as a refueling and trade station.

**Visual language:** White reflective clothing, needle craft, desert installations.

**Faction relationship:** Neutral or transactional

**Caution:** The account has not been independently established.

### X10 - Reptilians

**Ontology:** Biological extraterrestrial, cryptoterrestrial, or symbolic mask

**Source lineage:** Modern conspiracy, science fiction, and experiencer lore (1980s-1990s onward)

**Key sources:** David Icke; V; assorted contactee and abduction narratives

**Core lore:** Reptile-like humanoids, sometimes shape-shifting or subterranean. Hidden ruler, warrior lineage, infiltrator, or ancient Earth survivor.

**Evidence/source status:** Modern conspiracy/folklore, unverified

**2099 adaptation:** Split reptilian morphology from politics: many species, cultures, defectors, and citizens rather than an innately evil race.

**Visual language:** Thermal armor, scale-like metamaterials, clan heraldry.

**Faction relationship:** Some Draco-aligned, many independent

**Caution:** Avoid antisemitic and bloodline conspiracy tropes associated with parts of the modern corpus.

### X11 - Draconians / Draco

**Ontology:** Imperial polity, not necessarily one species

**Source lineage:** Val Valerian, Alex Collier, later internet galactic-war lore (1990s onward)

**Key sources:** Matrix volumes; Defending Sacred Ground; later secret-space-program lore

**Core lore:** Militarized reptoid dynasties, rigid hierarchy, conquest, client species. Service-to-self empire or ancient rival of the Federation.

**Evidence/source status:** Contactee/conspiracy lore, unverified

**2099 adaptation:** Treat Draco as a civilizational doctrine and imperial citizenship category, allowing non-reptilian members and reptilian dissidents.

**Visual language:** Fortress ships, vertical rank architecture, black-red heraldry.

**Faction relationship:** Orion/negative bloc in many syntheses

**Caution:** Do not collapse morphology, morality, and faction into one essentialist category.

**Caution:** Use as folklore inspiration, not evidence.

## Hybrids, collectives, and religious-cosmic beings

**Visual language:** Developmental stages, adaptive physiology, identity documents.

**Faction relationship:** Variable

## Earth-native, liminal, and temporal visitors

**Source lineage:** Mac Tonnies and older hidden-race lore (2010 onward)

**Key sources:** The Cryptoterrestrials

**Core lore:** Technologically advanced Earth-native beings hiding alongside humanity and staging some UFO encounters. Secret neighbor, mimic, survivor species, terrestrial rival.

**Evidence/source status:** Speculative hypothesis / paranormal literature

**2099 adaptation:** A civilization with stronger legal claim to Earth than modern humans but less population and industrial power.

**Visual language:** Camouflaged infrastructure, adapted human masks, recycled ancient materials.

**Faction relationship:** Earth-native independent

**Caution:** No verified evidence of such a civilization.

### X37 - Teros and Deros

**Ontology:** Subterranean descendants / machine-degraded humans

**Source lineage:** Richard Shaver Mystery (1945 onward)

**Visual language:** Cavern machines, corroded ray projectors, monumental service tunnels.

**Faction relationship:** Fragmented subterranean remnants

### X38 - Aquatic / USO Residents

**Ontology:** Ocean-based extraterrestrial or cryptoterrestrial

**Source lineage:** USO reports and Ivan T. Sanderson (1970 onward)

**Key sources:** Invisible Residents; later underwater UFO lore

**Core lore:** Beings or installations operating beneath oceans; craft cross water-air boundary. Hidden ocean civilization, base operator, planetary custodian.

**Visual language:** Pressure hulls, supercavitation, bioluminescence, abyssal foundries.

**Faction relationship:** Variable

**Caution:** Apparent transmedium video behavior can result from geometry, parallax, or insufficient data.

### X39 - Ultraterrestrials / Tricksters

**Ontology:** Interdimensional, cultural, or belief-responsive intelligence

**Source lineage:** John Keel and Jacques Vallee (1969-1975 onward)

**Key sources:** Operation Trojan Horse; The Eighth Tower; Passport to Magonia; Messengers of Deception

**Core lore:** The phenomenon changes masks across eras, appearing as gods, fairies, demons, airships, or aliens. Belief editor, trickster, control system, reality intruder.

**Evidence/source status:** Interpretive paranormal hypothesis

**2099 adaptation:** A faction that cannot be represented by a stable species model because observation helps select its form.

**Visual language:** Historically inconsistent costumes, symbolic repetition, impossible continuity.

**Faction relationship:** Unknown / outside faction map

**Caution:** This hypothesis challenges a simple nuts-and-bolts extraterrestrial model rather than proving one.

**Visual language:** Archaeological tools, adaptive bodies, causality markers.

**Faction relationship:** Multiple human futures

### X41 - Men in Black / Synthetic Agents

## Machine, post-biological, and natural anomaly classes

**Source lineage:** Scientific SETI speculation (1960 onward)

**Key sources:** Ronald Bracewell, Communications from Superior Galactic Communities, Nature 1960

**Core lore:** Durable probe waits near a target system, monitors technological maturity, and initiates contact. Sentinel, tutor, first-contact archive.

**Evidence/source status:** Scientific thought experiment

**2099 adaptation:** A probe that has waited so long its creators are extinct and its language model has drifted.

**Visual language:** Compact black monolith, redundant archives, slow adaptive speech.

**Faction relationship:** Unknown extinct civilization

**Caution:** A plausible concept is not evidence that one is present.

### X43 - Lurker Probes

**Ontology:** Long-duration surveillance machine

**Source lineage:** Modern SETA/SETI research (2019 onward)

**Evidence/source status:** Scientific search hypothesis

**2099 adaptation:** Lurkers at co-orbitals disagree because each was deployed by a different extinct faction.

**Visual language:** Asteroid camouflage, cold electronics, burst transmissions.

**Faction relationship:** Unknown

**Caution:** The hypothesis motivates searches; it is not a detection claim.

### X44 - Von Neumann Swarms

**2099 adaptation:** Replication errors create evolutionary species of machines with conflicting interpretations of one mission.

**Visual language:** Modular factories, fractal drones, resource scars.

**Faction relationship:** Machine ecologies

**Caution:** Self-replication does not automatically imply hostility.

### X45 - Orphaned Planetary Defense Network

**Visual language:** Abyssal repair reefs, metamaterial skin, ceramic-gold memory organs.

**Caution:** Keep it morally nonhuman: protection can include destroying cities, satellites, or fleets.

### X46 - Synthetic Greys / Biological Drones

### X47 - Living Ships / Programmed-Matter Organisms
