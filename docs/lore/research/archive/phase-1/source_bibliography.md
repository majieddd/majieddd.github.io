# Source Bibliography and Reliability Guide

## Official United States sources

### National Archives UAP bulk downloads

- Bulk-download landing page: https://www.archives.gov/research/catalog/catalog-bulk-downloads/uap-bulk-download
- UAP collection guide: https://www.archives.gov/research/topics/uaps

Use: official archival corpora and metadata. Authentic records can contain unresolved, mistaken, secondhand, or speculative claims.

### All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office

- Home: https://www.aaro.mil/
- Official imagery: https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/Official-UAP-Imagery/
- Reporting trends: https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/UAP-Reporting-Trends/
- Congressional and press products: https://www.aaro.mil/Congressional-Press-Products/

Use: current official morphology, resolution categories, annual reports, and case imagery.

### Office of the Director of National Intelligence

- 2021 Preliminary Assessment and subsequent annual UAP reports are available through ODNI and NARA.

Use: official scope, uncertainty, collection limitations, and safety concerns.

### Defense Intelligence Agency FOIA Reading Room

- Reading room: https://www.dia.mil/FOIA/FOIA-Electronic-Reading-Room/

Search for AAWSAP/AATIP DIRDs on vacuum engineering, programmable matter, antigravity, metamaterials, negative mass, wormholes, cloaking, and warp drive.

### FBI Vault and CIA Reading Room

- FBI UFO files: https://vault.fbi.gov/UFO
- CIA Reading Room: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/collection/ufos-fact-or-fiction

Use: history of agency handling, foreign press, memoranda, and public-interest correspondence. Do not infer that archiving a claim validates it.

## Academic study of UFO religions

- Christopher Partridge, editor, *UFO Religions*, Routledge, 2003.
- Benjamin E. Zeller, editor, *Handbook of UFO Religions*, Brill, 2021.
- James R. Lewis, editor, *Encyclopedic Sourcebook of UFO Religions*.
- Oxford Handbook material on extraterrestrials and UFO religions.

Use: history, sociology, contactee movements, Ashtar, Raelism, Aetherius, Unarius, Heaven's Gate, and cargo-cult comparison.

## Ancient Mesopotamian baseline

- ORACC Ancient Mesopotamian Gods and Goddesses: https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/amgg/
- Primary myth translations: *Atrahasis*, *Enuma Elish*, and related cuneiform literature.

Use: what Anunna/Anunnaki and Igigi mean in historical texts. Do not import Sitchin's modern Nibiru/gold narrative into the ancient wording.

## Ancient-astronaut and alien-religion literature

- Erich von Daniken, *Chariots of the Gods?*, 1968.
- Zecharia Sitchin, *The 12th Planet*, 1976, and the *Earth Chronicles*.
- Robert K. G. Temple, *The Sirius Mystery*, 1976.
- William Bramley, *The Gods of Eden*, 1989.
- Tom DeLonge and Peter Levenda, *Sekret Machines: Gods, Man & War*.

Use: source genealogy and story architecture. These works do not establish their central extraterrestrial claims.

## Contactee and Galactic Federation traditions

- George Adamski, *Flying Saucers Have Landed*, 1953.
- Ethel P. Hill, *In Days to Come by Ashtar*, 1957.
- Tuella, *Project: World Evacuation*, 1982.
- Virginia Essene and Sheldan Nidle, *You Are Becoming a Galactic Human*.
- Sheldan Nidle, *Your Galactic Neighbors* and *Your First Contact*.
- Billy Meier contact corpus.
- Barbara Marciniak, *Bringers of the Dawn*, 1992.
- Norma Milanovich, *We, the Arcturians*, 1990.
- Lyssa Royal and Keith Priest, *The Prism of Lyra*.
- Alex Collier, *Defending Sacred Ground*.
- Charles Hall, *Millennial Hospitality*.
- Darryl Anka/Bashar channeling corpus.

Use: species catalogs, councils, missions, ethics, ascension, and contact politics. Label as contactee/channeling claims.

## Abduction and experiencer literature

- John G. Fuller, *The Interrupted Journey*, 1966.
- Budd Hopkins, *Missing Time*, 1981.
- Whitley Strieber, *Communion*, 1987.
- John E. Mack, *Abduction*, 1994.
- David M. Jacobs, *Secret Life*, 1992, and *Walking Among Us*, 2015.

Use: Grey, Mantid, hybrid, medical, missing-time, and infiltration motifs. Treat interpretations and testimony separately from verified biological claims.

## Law of One / Ra Material

- Official archive: https://www.llresearch.org/channeling/ra-contact
- *The Ra Contact: Teaching the Law of One*, Volumes 1 and 2.
- *The Law of One*, Books I-V.
- *The Ra Contact Resource Series: A Concept Guide*.
- Carla Rueckert, *Living the Law of One 101* and *A Wanderer's Handbook*.

Relevant themes: Confederation, Orion group, service-to-others/service-to-self polarity, social memory complexes, quarantine/free will, and alleged religious identity hijacking. It is channeled material.

## Consciousness-harvest and control-system sources

- Robert Monroe, *Far Journeys*, 1985, especially "Hearsay Evidence."
- Carlos Castaneda, *The Active Side of Infinity*, 1998, especially "Mud Shadows."
- Jacques Vallee, *Passport to Magonia*, *The Invisible College*, and *Messengers of Deception*.
- John Keel, *Operation Trojan Horse* and *The Eighth Tower*.
- Nag Hammadi texts: https://www.gnosis.org/naghamm/nhl.html

Use: Loosh, awareness predation, cultural masks, belief feedback, and counterfeit rulers. Keep their distinct claims separate.

## Hidden Earth and orphaned machine sources

- Richard S. Shaver, "I Remember Lemuria!" and the Shaver Mystery, beginning 1945.
- Ivan T. Sanderson, *Invisible Residents*, 1970.
- Mac Tonnies, *The Cryptoterrestrials*, 2010.
- Ronald N. Bracewell, "Communications from Superior Galactic Communities," *Nature*, 1960.
- James Benford, "Looking for Lurkers: Objects Co-orbital with Earth as SETI Observables," 2019.
- Gavin A. Schmidt and Adam Frank, "The Silurian Hypothesis," 2018: https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03748
- John A. Ball, "The Zoo Hypothesis," 1973.

Use: old infrastructure, hidden residents, durable probes, deep-time trace loss, and quarantine.

## Hoax and internet-folklore branches

- Ummo letters: 1960s onward; linked to an admitted hoax by Jose Luis Jordan Pena.
- Project Serpo: anonymous 2005 web claims; unauthenticated.
- Lacerta Files: 1999-2000 internet interview story; broadly treated as fiction/hoax.
- Anonymous 2023 "mobile ocean factory" story: unverified forum folklore.
- Project Blue Beam: unsubstantiated human-psyop conspiracy theory.

Use: disinformation mechanics, fabricated alien identities, modern myth formation, and unreliable dossiers.

## Science-fiction sources worth separating from alleged real-world lore

- Fred Saberhagen, *Berserker* stories.
- Arthur C. Clarke, *The Sentinel* and *2001*.
- Stanislaw Lem, *Solaris*.
- Roadside Picnic by Arkady and Boris Strugatsky.
- The Expanse protomolecule and gate-builder ecology.
- Mass Effect's Reapers and Citadel-cycle control.
- Halo's Forerunner installations and autonomous monitors.
- Destiny's paracausal factions and machine ecologies.

Use: openly fictional comparative design language, not evidence.
