# UAP-Inspired Craft Design Bible for the 2099 Setting

## Ground rule

Official UAP reports are a visual and behavioral reference library, not a catalog of verified alien vehicles. AARO has resolved many reports as balloons, satellites, birds, aircraft, and UAS. Unresolved cases often remain unresolved because data are incomplete. The game can borrow morphology without claiming the underlying observations prove exotic craft.

## Official-case-inspired behaviors

### Apparent splitting

Some footage can look as though one object divides when geometry, parallax, or two nearby objects create that impression. In the game, use this ambiguity deliberately: a pilot cannot initially know whether a craft reproduced, uncloaked a partner, or was misread by sensors.

### Apparent transmedium travel

A craft seeming to enter water should not always mean it did. The game should support false depth cues, horizon errors, and sensor disagreement. Real transmedium units become more frightening when most apparent cases have ordinary explanations.

### Swarm over sea

The FY2025 AARO report mentions one maritime report involving roughly 100 airborne observations and two likely uncrewed surface systems off Virginia that remained under investigation at publication. This is an excellent scenario pattern: an air swarm coordinated by apparently ordinary surface vessels, with no need to reveal the controller.

### Satellite flare confusion

Many lights can be explained by satellite flaring. In gameplay, adversaries can deliberately time operations against satellite trains, meteor showers, or exercises to hide anomalous signatures inside normal clutter.

These are legitimate official documents about speculative research topics. Their existence is not evidence that the United States or UAP possess working versions.

## Fictional technology mapping

### Vacuum metric drive

The craft does not push against air. It modifies the local path length around itself. Visual result: no exhaust, weak sonic signature, strange lensing, and abrupt apparent acceleration.

### Programmable-matter hull

The hull changes mission geometry. A sphere becomes a cylinder; a triangle separates into orbs; damaged sections flow into new load paths.

### Metamaterial sensory skin

The entire hull is antenna, radar aperture, heat exchanger, and optical surface. Windows and obvious sensors are unnecessary.

### Negative-inertia pod

Crew and equipment occupy a protected internal reference frame while the exterior field changes velocity. Damage to the field causes catastrophic inertial snap.

### Quantum-vacuum heat problem

Even exotic propulsion produces management problems. High-energy craft retreat into oceans because water absorbs heat and shields secondary radiation.

### Wormhole seed

A small probe does not carry a full drive. It prepares a local endpoint for a larger gate, making seemingly trivial orbs strategically important.

### Noetic navigation

Conscious observers help stabilize destination probability. This explains pilot selection, ritualized crews, and why some ships behave differently around large populations.

## Visual design rules

1. Give each faction a manufacturing philosophy, not merely a silhouette.
2. Keep visible propulsion rare; heat and field effects are more distinctive than rockets.
3. Let the same mission produce convergent shapes across unrelated species.
4. Use imperfect human sensor interpretation as part of the design.
5. Distinguish a craft's body from its active field envelope.
6. Make ocean craft show pressure, corrosion, mineral growth, and biological fouling unless a field prevents contact.
7. Treat lights as information: status, camouflage failure, sensor interaction, or noetic display.
8. Reserve perfect geometric craft for machine systems; biological polities should show growth, repair, and individuality.

## Faction craft language

### Anunnaki Houses

Gold-bearing field lattices, monumental asymmetry, dynastic emblems, thick long-lived structures, mobile refinery temples.

### Federation

Highly legible safety geometry, modular diplomatic interfaces, craft shaped around occupant species, visible consent and identification protocols.

### Orion / Archonic bloc

Nested command layers, hidden crew hierarchy, proxy drones, intimidating but culturally tailored displays.

### Abyssal Sentinel Ecology

Mission-specific forms, mineral and ceramic surfaces, no stable insignia, repair growth, ocean-derived material, inconsistent age.

### Human 2099 craft

Fusion-industrial ancestry, standardized docking, replaceable modules, exposed radiators, rapidly evolving reverse-engineered field systems.

### Pirates

Hybrid salvage, visibly incompatible technologies, stolen field cores, painted identity, redundant low-tech controls.
