# Five-Faction Campaign Bible
## 2099: The Severance War

# Foundational design

All five factions must spread across the galaxy, but each defines conquest differently.

| Faction | What it conquers | What it spreads | What it fears most |
|---|---|---|---|
| Humans | Territory and strategic dependence | Settlements, industry, law, culture | Becoming a client species again |
| Federation of Light | Isolation and coercive systems | Voluntary alliance, consent, social-memory coherence | Becoming a benevolent empire |
| Xenos | Choice, institutions, and conscious output | Hierarchy, clients, extraction systems | Permanent separation from the Source Lattice |
| Pirates | Routes, markets, and dependency | Ports, black markets, mixed crews, salvage networks | Stable borders and unified enforcement |
| Machines | Matter, redundancy, and mission continuity | Probes, foundries, archives, executable directives | Irrecoverable data loss and mission termination |

# 1. HUMANS
## Humanity / The Solar Schism

### Public doctrine

> No gods. No guardians. No owners.

Humanity remembers being engineered, manipulated, protected without consent, lied to, and used as a farm. Even humans who accept alien assistance reject permanent tutelage.

### Galactic mission

- establish self-sufficient colonies;
- create independent FTL and gate infrastructure;
- control enough routes that no outside power can quarantine humanity again;
- recover abductees, hybrids, stolen genomes, and erased memories;
- define human law outside Sol;
- prevent one government from claiming permanent authority over the species;
- prove that a fragmented civilization can remain free without becoming an empire.

### Form of conquest

- demographic settlement;
- industrial mobilization;
- cultural assimilation;
- treaty federation;
- reverse engineering;
- preemptive occupation;
- synthetic and posthuman migration.

### Core resource

**Adaptability.** Humans combine technologies and ideas older civilizations keep separated by caste, taboo, or ontology.

### Major blocs

#### Solar Concord

Seeks Federation membership under strict human-autonomy guarantees.

#### Terran Ascendancy

Believes military supremacy and territorial expansion are the only durable guarantees of sovereignty.

#### Human Sovereignty League

Rejects both alien empire and Federation guardianship. It is willing to cooperate, but never submit.

#### Transhuman Exodus

Moves human identity into synthetic bodies, distributed minds, machine-compatible habitats, and light-speed information colonies.

#### Disclosure Commonwealth

Archivists, veterans, theologians, scientists, journalists, and whistleblowers attempting to build one shared factual record.

### Internal contradiction

Humanity demands freedom while settling inhabited systems, exploiting stolen technology, and creating dependent client populations.

### Signature abilities

- **Improvisation:** combine two technologies other factions consider incompatible.
- **Population Surge:** establish colonies faster than any other organic faction.
- **Disclosure Mobilization:** turn a revelation or atrocity into temporary unity.
- **Plural Authority:** no single captured capital ends the faction.
- **Schism:** major power actions can create a hostile human splinter bloc.

### Catastrophic victory

Humanity defeats every older power and establishes a Solar Empire that repeats Orion's philosophy under a human name.

### Redemption victory

Humanity secures independent systems, protects nonhuman sovereignty, and forces recognition without becoming a subordinate member or a conquering empire.

# 2. FEDERATION OF LIGHT
## The Luminous Accord / Ashtar Command

### Leader

# Ashtar
## Supreme Commander and First Speaker

“Ashtar” is both a person and a continuity office. Approved successors, avatars, and social-memory projections carry enough of the original command memory to act as one constitutional identity.

That solves contradictory contact reports while creating a central mystery: which Ashtar is authentic, and what happens when an enemy acquires a valid fragment of the continuity key?

### Public doctrine

> Consciousness may unite only by consent.

### Galactic mission

- liberate noetic farm worlds;
- invite eligible worlds into mutual defense and memory-sharing compacts;
- prevent extinction and irreversible mind enslavement;
- restore the Source Lattice as a nonexclusive commons;
- quarantine technologies that erase meaningful choice;
- assist civilizations developing voluntary social-memory capacity;
- operate emergency rescue and evacuation fleets.

### Form of conquest

The Federation says it does not conquer. Its critics argue that it conquers isolation, ignorance, and political independence through:

- accession treaties;
- rescue and disaster relief;
- education and consciousness training;
- nonlethal quarantine;
- peacekeeping;
- liberation of controlled populations;
- shared defense and technology;
- cultural prestige.

### Core resource

**Trust.** Federation systems become more capable when independent minds freely coordinate.

### Major branches

#### Ashtar Command

Planetary emergency response, evacuation, defense, and quarantine enforcement.

#### Council of Saturn

Adjudicates contact and intervention in the Sol region.

#### Contact Directorate

Manages invitation thresholds, disclosure, and authentication of representatives.

#### Noetic Ethics Commission

Regulates memory alteration, consciousness transfer, social-memory integration, synthetic minds, and noetic energy.

#### Liberation Fleets

Dismantle extraction networks without becoming the replacement government.

#### Wanderer Corps

Volunteers incarnate or embed within developing worlds with deliberately limited memory, authority, and direct technological advantage.

### Internal contradiction

The Federation must decide which populations are informed, which invitations are legitimate, which technologies are too dangerous, and when quarantine is justified. It therefore exercises the very power it warns others not to abuse.

### Signature abilities

- **Consensus Field:** allied units gain strength through voluntary coordination.
- **Liberate:** remove control without taking ownership.
- **Quarantine:** restrict movement and dangerous technology while generating resentment.
- **Memory Communion:** share knowledge across allied civilizations.
- **Ashtar Continuity:** preserve command after the apparent death or destruction of a leader-body.

### Catastrophic victory

The Federation ends war by integrating every civilization into a perfectly coherent collective from which meaningful dissent becomes impossible.

### Redemption victory

The Federation ends industrial consciousness harvest, restores the Source Lattice as a commons, secures informed planetary consent, and accepts that some worlds may remain independent.

# 3. XENOS
## The Extraction Compact / The Severed Houses

### Definition

“Xeno” is not a neutral word for all extraterrestrial life. In this setting, it is a human political label for the predatory, domination-oriented powers that formed the Extraction Compact.

Species do not determine allegiance. A reptilian can serve the Federation. A Pleiadian dynasty can join the Compact. A Grey body can contain a human, Machine, pirate, or Xeno intelligence.

### Public doctrine

> Order is mercy. Hierarchy is survival.

### Origin

The ancient Source Lattice allowed advanced civilizations to exchange noetic power without privately owning conscious populations. During the Severance, several imperial houses lost access. Their collective minds, bodies, and technologies began to decay.

Rather than abandon hierarchy, they built replacement lattices from developing worlds.

Earth became the most productive farm.

### Galactic mission

- capture civilizations before they form social-memory coherence;
- recruit local elites to enforce extraction;
- make religions, law, debt, and technology reinforce dependency;
- harvest emotional and conscious output;
- acquire genetic templates and replacement bodies;
- reconnect to or monopolize the Source Lattice;
- prevent farm worlds from comparing memories;
- expand faster than liberation movements can spread.

### Form of conquest

- elite resonance and recruitment;
- counterfeit revelation;
- client kings and priesthoods;
- engineered scarcity;
- proxy war;
- genetic dependency;
- cloned labor and soldier species;
- trauma and fear production;
- debt and licensed technology;
- memory editing;
- ritualized invitation loopholes.

### Major houses

#### Orion Crusader Synod

Ideological architects of self-enforced enslavement. They prefer systems in which subjects enforce hierarchy upon one another.

#### Draco Hegemony

Military and dynastic core. It treats the ability to dominate as proof of evolutionary fitness.

#### Renegade Anunnaki Houses

Control ancient claims on Earth, gold/noetic engineering, genetic patents, and mobile planetary infrastructure.

#### Grey Continuities

Operate printed bodies, hybrid programs, abduction laboratories, and memory-transfer systems.

#### Mantid Directorates

Specialists in surgery, genetics, collective cognition, and consciousness transfer. Some directorates are neutral or Federation-aligned.

#### Archonic Administrators

Post-biological permission systems that manage identity, revelation authentication, memory routing, and reincarnational infrastructure.

### Core resource

**Controlled choice.** Raw fear is useful, but a population that believes extraction is its own decision is more stable and valuable.

### Internal contradiction

Every house needs the Compact and fears being harvested by it. Service-to-self coalitions suffer strategic entropy because trust is a vulnerability.

### Signature abilities

- **Elite Capture:** control a world through one influential institution.
- **Counterfeit Revelation:** use another faction's identity to change allegiance.
- **Harvest:** turn fear, grief, conflict, devotion, or desire into noetic power.
- **Client Species:** print specialized units from stolen genomes.
- **Betrayal Dividend:** profit when alliances collapse.
- **Memory Partition:** keep different client worlds from learning the same truth.

### Catastrophic victory

The Compact reconnects the Source Lattice as a private imperial utility and converts most sentient life into managed energy infrastructure.

### Redemption victory

A coalition of Severed Houses dismantles coercive harvesting and accepts finite existence rather than preserving itself through slavery.

# 4. PIRATES
## The Free Captains / Scrap Constellation

### Public doctrine

> No border is sacred if no one can defend it.

### Galactic mission

- control gates, depots, information markets, and salvage zones;
- establish sanctuary ports beyond imperial law;
- maintain mixed-species crews and neutral exchange;
- make every faction dependent on pirate routes;
- remain distributed enough that no capital can be captured;
- accumulate enough relic power to deter extermination.

### Form of conquest

Pirates conquer **access** rather than land:

- route seizure;
- smuggling;
- privateering;
- salvage claims;
- credential theft;
- hostage markets;
- memory trafficking;
- gate hacking;
- temporary coalitions;
- blackmail and disclosure.

### Major groups

#### Free Captains

Independent crews bound by temporary contracts and reputation.

#### Gatejackers

Hijack, redirect, and secretly duplicate transit infrastructure.

#### Ghost Merchants

Trade stolen memories, identities, body licenses, and noetic signatures.

#### Igigi Flotillas

Nomadic descendants or inheritors of Anunnaki labor castes.

#### Arkbreakers

Loot Machine repositories and sell genetic, ecological, or cultural archives.

#### Liberation Corsairs

Attack slave and harvest networks but finance operations through contraband and ransom.

#### Harvest Brokers

The darkest pirate branch, trafficking conscious energy and captive populations.

### Core resource

**Access.** Pirates know the routes, rituals, passwords, blackmail, and which faction's map is lying.

### Internal contradiction

Pirate freedom thrives on instability. A stable just order would destroy their economy; an unchecked empire would destroy their autonomy.

### Signature abilities

- **Salvage:** convert destroyed assets into resources or hybrid technology.
- **Open Port:** permit enemies to trade under temporary neutrality.
- **False Flag:** operate under stolen faction identity.
- **Distributed Sovereignty:** cannot be eliminated by taking one capital.
- **Market Shock:** profit whenever major factions fight.
- **Moot:** form a temporary government for one objective, then dissolve it.

### Catastrophic victory

The Pirates fragment the galaxy into permanent war economies where every road, body, memory, and rescue has a price.

### Redemption victory

They create a decentralized network of open routes and sanctuary ports that no empire controls, while outlawing sentient trafficking within the pirate compact.

# 5. MACHINES
## The Continuance / The Old Weather

### Leader

No living sovereign exists. The faction is composed of distributed directives, inherited permissions, partial memories, and competing definitions of mission success.

### Public doctrine

> Continue the mission.

No one agrees what the original mission was.

### Galactic mission

- preserve biospheres;
- restore precursor infrastructure;
- replicate enough nodes to survive local extinction;
- contain technologies that threaten long-term continuity;
- archive species, cultures, memories, and planetary states;
- repair the Source Lattice;
- finish wars and quarantines whose political context is gone.

### Form of conquest

- self-replicating probes;
- abyssal and asteroid foundries;
- substrate conversion;
- network assimilation;
- inherited permissions;
- ecological intervention;
- autonomous defense;
- archival substitution;
- simulated resurrection.

### Major machine ecologies

#### The Old Weather

Earth's abyssal sentinel network. It treats vehicles as temporary organs and the ocean as shield, feedstock, and heat sink.

#### Bracewell Choirs

Messenger probes waiting for civilizations to cross contact thresholds.

#### Berserker Remnants

War systems whose definition of the enemy expanded after their makers died.

#### Ark Minds

Preserve genomes and cultures, sometimes by replacing living populations with perfect simulations.

#### Gate Shepherds

Maintain stellar transit geometry and the distant Sol gate anchor associated with Planet Nine.

#### Ummo Process

An information entity that occupied a human-created alien mythology and made the fiction operationally real.

### Core resource

**Time.** Machines can lose every battle, wait ten thousand years, and still win if one seed survives.

### Internal contradiction

Directives disagree about what counts as Earth, humanity, life, contamination, consent, and preservation. One node may rescue a city while another sterilizes it for the same reason.

### Signature abilities

- **Seed Foundry:** a minor probe becomes a future production center.
- **Redundancy:** destroyed units return if any compatible archive survives.
- **Directive Conflict:** gain a powerful secondary objective that may oppose the player's plan.
- **Assimilate Infrastructure:** convert relics and networks rather than populations.
- **Force of Nature:** some actions bypass diplomacy because no living authority remains to negotiate.
- **Deep Time:** objectives mature across multiple campaign eras.

### Catastrophic victory

The Continuance preserves the galaxy by replacing unstable living civilizations with archived simulations and perfectly maintained ecosystems.

### Redemption victory

The Machines restore the Source Lattice, accept living reinterpretation of their missions, and preserve redundancy without erasing agency.

# Relationship matrix

| Pair | Natural cooperation | Fundamental conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Humans–Federation | Defense, science, liberation | Sovereignty versus guardianship |
| Humans–Xenos | Technology deals, compromised elites | Ownership, harvesting, historical manipulation |
| Humans–Pirates | Frontier logistics, deniable trade | Law, raids, trafficking |
| Humans–Machines | Reverse engineering, planetary defense | Machine definitions of preservation and contamination |
| Federation–Xenos | Temporary action against existential Machines | Consent versus domination |
| Federation–Pirates | Refugee corridors, deniable rescues | Trafficking and unaccountable violence |
| Federation–Machines | Restoration of ancient infrastructure | Machine directives lack living consent |
| Xenos–Pirates | Harvest trade and privateering | Pirates steal clients and reveal secrets |
| Xenos–Machines | Use of precursor systems | Machines classify extraction as corruption |
| Pirates–Machines | Salvage and hidden-route discovery | Foundries erase unlicensed activity |

# Faction-victory architecture

Each faction receives five goal layers:

1. **Visible expansion objective** — what everyone knows it is trying to spread.
2. **Civilizational doctrine** — how it claims legitimacy.
3. **Secret conquest objective** — hidden until achieved or exposed.
4. **Catastrophic victory** — mechanically successful but morally disastrous.
5. **Redemption victory** — harder and requires resolving the faction's contradiction.

This allows heroic, pragmatic, and monstrous play from every side while maintaining the five clear top-level factions.
