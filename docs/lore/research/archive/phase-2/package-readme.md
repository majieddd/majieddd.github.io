# 2099 Cosmic Conquest — Deep Research Addendum

This addendum expands the original UAP/Xeno Lore Research Pack with:

1. `research_dossier.md` — findings from the Law of One, Ashtar traditions, the Illuminati game, Black Vault, astronomy, religion-control theories, and the unified 2099 canon.
2. `five_faction_campaign_bible.md` — Humans, Federation of Light, Xenos, Pirates, and Machines, each with a distinct form of galactic conquest.
3. `video_species_extraction.md` — a claim-level extraction of the 20 alien-race archetypes discussed across the two requested videos.
4. `black_vault_research_route.md` — a structured research workflow for the Black Vault and related official archives.
5. `rabbit_hole_atlas.md` — evidence-coded scientific, governmental, esoteric, contactee, conspiracy, and folklore threads.
6. `game_system_blueprint.md` — original game mechanics inspired by network-control games without copying their exact rules or cards.
7. `deep_research_prompt.md` — a reusable prompt for the next research pass.
8. `source_evidence_matrix.xlsx` and `.csv` — a sortable source, claim, evidence, faction, and canon-use ledger.
9. `source_manifest.json` — machine-readable metadata for the pack.

## Evidence labels

- **A — Established core:** verified scientific fact, official current status, or directly verifiable publication fact.
- **B — Authentic record:** a real government file, documented case, or archived event whose extraordinary interpretation remains unresolved.
- **C — Historical tradition:** ancient text, documented folklore, academic history, or established history of a belief movement.
- **D — Traceable fringe claim:** named channeling, contactee, ancient-astronaut, or conspiracy literature with an identifiable source lineage.
- **E — Contaminated claim:** anonymous leak, admitted hoax, forged document, circular internet story, or material with severe provenance problems.
- **F — 2099 fiction:** original synthesis created for the setting.

## Important limitations

- A government file proves that a report or investigation existed; it does not automatically validate the extraordinary explanation recorded in it.
- The Law of One and Ashtar sources are analyzed as traceable esoteric/contactee corpora, not as independently verified extraterrestrial communications.
- The two YouTube caption feeds were not retrievable in this environment. The species file is a structured paraphrase reconstructed from preserved episode descriptions and detailed mirrors, not a verbatim transcript.
- The Black Vault contains millions of pages. This pack maps its most relevant branches and supplies an extraction system; it does not claim to have downloaded or individually read every page.
- The later Deep Research prompt may request many parallel research lanes, but this session did not literally operate 100 autonomous agents.
