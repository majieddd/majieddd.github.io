# Cross-cutting conclusions

## Species is not faction

The most useful correction to popular alien taxonomies is to separate:

- body morphology;
- biological lineage;
- political allegiance;
- consciousness architecture;
- profession or caste;
- technology platform;
- moral orientation.

A Grey can be a disposable drone, a living species, a future human, a synthetic body, or a remotely piloted shell. A Mantid can be a geneticist working for the Federation, a Xeno director, or an independent pirate surgeon. A reptilian can be Draco, Bellatrician, Federation, pirate, or unaffiliated.

## Motive categories

The twenty entries reduce to eight motive families:

1. **Guidance and uplift** — Arcturians, Pleiadians, some Nordics, Blue Avians.
2. **Resource extraction** — Anunnaki and predatory compact houses.
3. **Genetic survival** — Greys, Ebens, Mantid programs, hybrids.
4. **Imperial control** — Draco, Trantaloids, hostile elite networks.
5. **Stewardship and shelter** — Ant People, Telosians, some aquatic groups.
6. **Diplomacy and treaty access** — Tall Whites, Venusians, Alpha Centaurians.
7. **Diaspora and survival** — Lyran/Feline and displaced client species.
8. **Unknown or inherited mission** — Archquloids, Orange beings, ocean races, Machine-adjacent entities.

## Best canon principle

The videos should be treated as an unreliable human intelligence briefing assembled from incompatible source lineages. Some entries identify real civilizations; some identify body platforms; some are propaganda; some are human folklore; and some were created by Machines or Xenos to provide a believable identity for future operations.
