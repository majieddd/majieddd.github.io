# Game System Blueprint
## Original network-conquest mechanics for the five-faction universe

# 1. Design objective

The game should make fleets important but insufficient. Players compete simultaneously for territory, institutions, belief, routes, archives, bodies, and machine permissions.

The structural inspiration from conspiracy-network games is asymmetric goals, linked control, alignment effects, negotiation, proxy action, and betrayal. The rules below are original to this universe.

# 2. The two maps

## Galactic map

Contains:

- star systems;
- habitable worlds;
- colonies;
- gates;
- asteroid belts;
- machine foundries;
- noetic wells;
- archive sites;
- quarantine zones;
- pirate corridors.

## Influence lattice

Contains:

- governments;
- religions;
- corporations;
- research institutions;
- intelligence services;
- social-memory networks;
- cults;
- media systems;
- labor systems;
- black markets;
- machine permission trees.

Control on one map modifies action on the other.

# 3. Asset statistics

Every asset can have six ratings:

- **Force:** capacity for direct coercion or defense.
- **Influence:** ability to alter allegiance and policy.
- **Resistance:** difficulty of being controlled or corrupted.
- **Yield:** material, genetic, informational, or noetic production.
- **Mobility:** ability to project action across routes.
- **Memory:** ability to retain truth through deception, destruction, or regime change.

# 4. Alignment tags

Use tags rather than good/evil labels:

- Organic / Synthetic / Noetic / Hybrid
- Open / Secret
- Centralized / Distributed
- Coherent / Fragmented
- Voluntary / Coercive
- Ancient / Emergent
- Lawful / Outlaw
- Territorial / Networked
- Biological / Post-biological

Alignment matches can strengthen conversion, alliance, or infiltration. Oppositions can strengthen resistance or make betrayal more valuable.

# 5. Card families

## Worlds

Populations, biospheres, noetic signatures, and political conditions.

## Institutions

Governments, religions, companies, intelligence services, scientific bodies, and labor systems.

## Fleets

Military, rescue, exploration, privateer, and Machine swarms.

## Species and body platforms

Lineages, printable shells, hybrid templates, and social-memory entities.

## Relics

Anunnaki gold lattices, Archonic keys, Federation beacons, Machine seeds, and human reproduction vehicles.

## Archives

Declassified files, shared memories, blackmail, archaeological records, and corrupted histories.

## Agents

Commanders, prophets, hackers, theologians, defectors, diplomats, and false identities.

## Anomalies

Interstellar objects, time slips, plasma ecologies, haunted gates, mass visions, and sensor contradictions.

## Events

Disclosure, war, schism, revelation, asteroid passage, famine, liberation, mutiny, and machine awakening.

# 6. Core actions

## Claim

Establish legal or practical possession of a world, route, relic, or archive.

## Convert

Change an asset's allegiance or political polarity.

## Liberate

Remove control without assuming ownership. Federation and some Human or Pirate builds specialize in this.

## Corrupt

Insert a dependency, false permission, compromised belief, or hidden control node.

## Salvage

Turn destroyed or abandoned assets into resources or hybrid technology.

## Seed

Create a weak presence that matures into a future colony, religion, foundry, or network.

## Reveal

Expose a secret card. Revelation can remove control, destroy trust, or prove a faction's warning.

## Spoof

Use another faction's identity, transponder, religious symbol, or noetic signature.

## Harvest

Extract noetic output. This produces power but also evidence, unrest, and polarity consequences.

## Remember

Use archives or collective memory to resist identity theft, propaganda, and timeline alteration.

# 7. Control chains

Institutions can control other institutions, creating indirect power structures.

Example:

Xeno House → elite covenant → planetary government → media network → labor system → population.

Destroying the Xeno fleet does not free the population. Players must identify and break the influence chain.

Machine chains look different:

Deep Foundry → maintenance satellite → navigation standard → civilian infrastructure → automatic permission.

Pirate chains are route-based:

Sanctuary Port → smuggler guild → gate credential → colonial dependency.

# 8. Public and secret objectives

Each player receives:

- a public faction mission;
- a private campaign agenda;
- one hidden moral compromise;
- one redemption condition;
- one catastrophe condition.

This ensures players can understand each faction's general behavior without knowing its exact winning move.

# 10. Conflict modes

Every target can be attacked in multiple ways:

- military assault;
- economic dependency;
- political conversion;
- religious authentication;
- memory warfare;
- cyber or machine-permission attack;
- route isolation;
- noetic resonance;
- public disclosure.

The defender chooses whether to resist openly, conceal the attack, bargain, or allow limited access to create a trap.

# 11. Negotiation

Deals may include:

- temporary gate access;
- archive exchange;
- rescue rights;
- noninterference zones;
- recognition of a colony;
- release of captives or memories;
- shared attack on a foundry;
- promise not to expose a secret;
- transfer of a body license;
- recognition of an Ashtar credential.

Agreements are socially meaningful but not always mechanically binding. Breaking one creates a persistent reputation record that can matter more than the immediate gain.

# 12. The Revelation Stack as a game system

A Religion or Revelation card can contain face-down layers:

1. transcendent insight;
2. alien message;
3. machine protocol;
4. human interpretation;
5. institutional capture;
6. hostile spoof;
7. intelligence operation;
8. emergent egregore.

Research actions reveal layers. Different factions may benefit from different truths. Destroying the religion can disable a genuine planetary defense protocol; preserving it can maintain Xeno control.

# 13. Astronomy event chain

## Oumuamua — 2017

Place a dormant reconnaissance token whose operator is not initially known.

## 3I/ATLAS — 2025-2026

Create a public misinformation crisis. The visible object is natural, but a hidden Machine or Pirate asset enters behind it.

## Apophis — 2029

Activate the quarantine beacon and begin the long campaign clock.

## Planet Nine

Reveal the final gate-anchor system only after enough astronomical, Machine, and Anunnaki clues are assembled.

# 14. Campaign eras

1. **Rumor Era:** influence, archives, and secrecy dominate.
2. **Disclosure Era:** public legitimacy becomes a resource.
3. **Contact Era:** the five factions become visible.
4. **Expansion Era:** systems, routes, and colonies open.
5. **Severance War:** the Source Lattice becomes the central objective.
6. **Post-Lattice Era:** victory changes the rules of consciousness, not merely the political map.
