# Rabbit-Hole Atlas

## How to read this atlas

- **A:** established scientific fact or current official status.
- **B:** authentic government record or documented case; extraordinary attribution unresolved.
- **C:** historical text, folklore, or academic history of belief.
- **D:** traceable contactee, channeling, ancient-astronaut, or conspiracy literature.
- **E:** anonymous, forged, admitted-hoax, circular, or severely contaminated claim.
- **F:** original 2099 fiction.
