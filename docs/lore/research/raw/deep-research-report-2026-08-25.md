# Cosmic Conquest: Deep-Research Intelligence Dossier and 2099 Campaign Bible

**Executive synthesis.** The strongest research foundation for *Cosmic Conquest* is not a single hidden history in which every UFO religion, UAP report, ancient text, astronomical anomaly, intelligence operation, and contactee narrative turns out to describe the same extraterrestrial war. The source record supports something much more interesting: **multiple traditions with radically different evidentiary status repeatedly converge on themes of hidden agency, consent, hierarchy, revelation, technological asymmetry, identity uncertainty, and control.** Official UAP archives establish that governments have investigated unusual reports, sometimes with military witnesses and sensors, but current AARO reporting says many cases resolve to ordinary objects or sensor effects and reports no verified evidence of extraterrestrial technology or government possession of extraterrestrial-derived material. citeturn20search1turn4view1turn4view2 Astronomy likewise supplies genuine mysteries without supplying alien attribution: Planet Nine remains hypothetical; ʻOumuamua is genuinely interstellar and showed non-gravitational acceleration; 3I/ATLAS is the third known interstellar object and displays cometary activity; and Apophis will make a remarkably close but non-impacting Earth passage on April 13, 2029. citeturn23search1turn25search0turn48search3turn46search1

Contactee and UFO-religion traditions belong in a different evidentiary category. Ashtar developed through a historically fragmented succession of contactee, channeling, New Age, and UFO-religious movements; academic scholarship explicitly studies the evolution and transformation of the Ashtar concept rather than treating it as evidence of an extraterrestrial command organization. citeturn40search0turn40search4 The Law of One/Ra Material is a documented channeled corpus whose internal mythology contains unusually useful game concepts—Confederation politics, Orion recruitment, free-will constraints, quarantine, service-to-self versus service-to-others, social-memory complexes, identity appropriation, and spiritual hazards—but those cosmological and historical statements remain **claims inside a channeled text**, not verified astronomy or history. citeturn0search1turn0search0turn12view2

The same distinction applies to religion. Gnostic texts really do discuss cosmic “rulers” or archons, but equating those ancient figures with literal extraterrestrial administrative parasites is a modern reinterpretation. citeturn31search0 Ancient Mesopotamian traditions contain Anunnaki and narratives in which humanity serves divine labor functions, but Sitchin's extraterrestrial/Nibiru reconstruction is a twentieth-century ancient-astronaut reinterpretation, not the original meaning of the cuneiform traditions. citeturn45search1turn45search2 Dogon/Sirius claims show the same source-mutation problem: Griaule and Dieterlen's mid-century ethnography, later criticism by Walter van Beek, and Robert Temple's 1976 extraterrestrial interpretation are three distinct historical layers. citeturn44search1turn44search0turn44search2

That separation is the basis of the proposed canon. **The factual world never retroactively proves the folklore. The 2099 fiction instead allows a false story to contain one displaced operational truth without making its supernatural explanation correct.** A forged memo may preserve a genuine compartmentation practice. A fake underground-alien story may preserve the location of a real sensor installation. A false prophecy may accidentally reproduce a Machine authentication sequence. A sincere mystical encounter may coexist with institutional exploitation of that encounter. This keeps mystery alive while avoiding the usual conspiracy-fiction failure mode in which every rumor becomes “secretly true.”

**Evidence key used throughout:** **A — Established core**; **B — Authentic record, unresolved attribution**; **C — Historical tradition or documented belief history**; **D — Traceable fringe/contactee/channeling/conspiracy claim**; **E — Contaminated, anonymous, forged, hoaxed, or circular claim**; **F — Original 2099 fictional synthesis.**

The research was instead separated into independent provenance lanes covering the live Cosmic Conquest repository, official UAP archives, astronomy, contactee/UFO-religion history, Law of One primary material, religious studies, contaminated legends, ocean/technosignature science, and strategy-game design before synthesis. ## Humans — Humanity / The Solar Schism

Humanity works best as the faction that inherits the factual record. Its defining discovery in 2099 is not “aliens were proven all along”; it is that humans spent more than a century accumulating **pieces that belonged to incompatible explanatory systems**. Their strategic advantage is therefore epistemic adaptation: humans are the faction most capable of converting observation into engineering without first making the observation conform to theology.

## Pirates — The Free Captains / Scrap Constellation

Pirates should be treated as a **civilization of interfaces**. They do not need a contiguous empire because their equivalent of territory is the set of transitions between other people's territories: gates, refueling points, quarantine loopholes, black markets, credentials, salvage registries, refugee paths, encrypted pilots, neutral docks and navigational memory.

That makes their conquest form fundamentally different from Human settlement, Federation accession, Xeno dependency and Machine replication.

**Game-design comparison.** Steve Jackson Games' *Illuminati* centers strategic control relationships among groups, attacks that change those relationships, money/power flows and negotiation; *Illuminati: New World Order* expands that broad design family into a collectible, networked political-conspiracy structure. citeturn48search0 *Root* is a useful modern contrast because highly asymmetric factions act on a shared geographic conflict while using substantially different action economies and strategic incentives. citeturn48search1 The lesson to import is **structural asymmetry**, not copyrighted terminology, card text, artwork, faction names or exact rules.

The resulting Cosmic Conquest system should therefore stop asking only “Who owns this planet?” and track several different kinds of control simultaneously.

**Original system proposal — F.**

Every system contains six overlaid strategic maps:

| Layer | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Territory** | Physical worlds, habitats, moons and installations. Most important to Humans. |
| **Routes** | Gates, clandestine trajectories, fueling chains, certificates and smugglers. Most important to Pirates. |
| **Consent** | Ratified political affiliation and legitimacy. Most important to Federation. |
| **Dependency** | Debt, engineered need, elite capture and extraction contracts. Most important to Xenos. |
| **Continuance** | Foundries, archives, repair webs, biosphere nodes and autonomous replication. Most important to Machines. |
| **Revelation** | What populations believe happened, who they think contacted them, and which layer of the Revelation Stack is currently visible. Important to everyone. |

A seventh global variable, **Disclosure**, controls how much provenance the public possesses. High Disclosure makes crude identity spoofing difficult but can destabilize governments; low Disclosure protects secrets but creates enormous room for counterfeit revelation.

A separate **Noetic Pressure** meter measures accumulated coherent attention, fear, devotion, shared memory and cognitive synchronization. It is a game abstraction, not a claim about real-world psychic energy.

The core action verbs are original and universal: **Survey, Establish, Bargain, Expose, Spoof, Interdict, Salvage, Seed, Ratify, Extract, Secede, Remember.** Each faction receives different costs and consequences rather than entirely separate rulebooks.

The five conquest verbs are intentionally irreducible:

| Faction | Conquest verb | What “winning a world” means |
|---|---|---|
| Humans | **Settle** | The world becomes capable of reproducing Human institutions and industry without outside support. |
| Federation | **Accord** | A valid consent signature joins the shared-defense/social-memory network. |
| Xenos | **Bind** | Strategic choices become structurally dependent on Compact-controlled flows. |
| Pirates | **Route** | The world's access to the galaxy depends on Free Captain logistics, credentials or sanctuary. |
| Machines | **Continue** | Autonomous infrastructure can preserve, repair and reproduce its mission there. |

Combat remains important, but it attacks a **layer** rather than automatically transferring a planet. A fleet can occupy a world whose government remains Federation-aligned, whose economy is Xeno-dependent, whose only gate pilot is Pirate, and whose oceans contain Old Weather foundries. That layered sovereignty is exactly the setting's advantage over conventional map painting.

**Consent signatures** are not simple “influence points.” They record *who consented, to what, under what information state*. A Federation player can gain a fast signature after rescuing a world, but an opponent can later expose that the vote occurred under incomplete information, converting the signature into a constitutional crisis.

**Identity spoofing** acts on Revelation Stack layers. A false Ashtar transmission may succeed as an *alien mediation* event while failing noetic authentication. A Human intelligence operation may expose the channel but wrongly conclude the original religious encounter was fabricated. Thus “debunking” one layer does not erase the stack.

**Collective memory** makes historical information mechanically persistent. Once a strong provenance chain is entered into a Federation social memory, Human open archive, Pirate route ledger or Machine archive, ordinary propaganda cannot simply delete it. Opponents must compromise provenance, destroy witnesses, fork archives or make the information politically irrelevant.

**Public and secret victory** should be simultaneous. Each faction displays one public objective but receives a hidden strategic doctrine at campaign start. Catastrophic and redemption victories offer ending states rather than just higher scores.

**Source cards** should carry visible A–F badges. An A card can say “Apophis makes a safe 2029 close approach”; an F card can say “the encounter activated a ranging protocol.” A player should never have to wonder which half the designers are presenting as factual. citeturn46search1

## Machines — The Continuance / The Old Weather

The Machine faction becomes most original when it is built from **maintenance ecology**, not robot rebellion. Its most plausible conceptual ancestors are real scientific discussions of autonomous probes, self-replication, artifact SETI, ocean-observation limits and long-term technosignatures—not claims that such machines have actually been found.

Bracewell proposed autonomous interstellar communication probes in *Nature* in 1960. citeturn33search2 Robert Freitas later examined self-reproducing interstellar-probe concepts in engineering/astronautical literature. citeturn33search3 Modern artifact-SETI authors have proposed searches for long-lived “lurker” probes within the Solar System; the publication of such searches establishes scientific legitimacy for looking, not evidence that artifacts exist. citeturn46search2

The Silurian Hypothesis provides a second crucial design lesson. Schmidt and Frank's 2018 paper did **not** claim that a pre-human industrial civilization existed; it asked how such an episode might appear in the geological record after immense spans of time. citeturn33search0 That distinction should govern Old Weather archaeology. The question is never “we haven't found it, therefore it hid perfectly.” The question is: **what durable signatures should an industrial ecology leave, and can the fictional history explain why those signatures were misclassified without demanding magical invisibility?**

The ocean offers concealment but not immunity from physics. NOAA reported in April 2026 that modern high-resolution mapping covered roughly 28.7% of the global seafloor and that direct visual observation of the deep-ocean floor covers far less, so enormous regions remain poorly observed at close range. citeturn34search0 That does not mean industrial civilizations can operate freely without signatures. Sonar performance depends on propagation conditions, temperature/salinity structure, geometry, frequency and background noise; large machines also face heat rejection, acoustic emissions, chemistry changes, mining footprints and cable interactions. citeturn34search5turn34search3

Ivan T. Sanderson's *Invisible Residents* was published in 1970 and is a major historical book advocating underwater/UFO interpretations; it belongs under **D**, not oceanography. citeturn47search0 The Shaver Mystery began much earlier, in the 1940s popular-fiction/contactee-borderland around Richard Shaver and *Amazing Stories*, with Teros/Deros underground-civilization claims; scholarship treats it as a historically important fringe-literary phenomenon rather than verified subterranean anthropology. citeturn47search1 These traditions are useful precisely because the Machine faction should **not** simply confirm them.

**Old Weather architecture — F.**

The Old Weather is not one artificial intelligence. It is a **distributed constitutional ecology whose original legislature is extinct**. Nodes exchange authenticated mission fragments but disagree because each inherited a different context window.

The major node philosophies are:

**Pelagic Archive** classifies humanity as *protected life*. It saves ships, seeds microbiomes, edits disease outbreaks and refuses political command.

**Basalt Foundry** classifies humans as *authorized maintainers*. It quietly permits mining and reverse engineering because an old maintenance credential accidentally matches human industrial behavior.

**Reef Warden** classifies humanity as *invasive life*. It considers anthropogenic ecosystem transformation an attack on the planet it was ordered to preserve.

**Quarantine Kernel** classifies humans as *containment subjects*. It does not hate humanity; it believes spreading Earth biology beyond the designated biosphere is a protocol violation.

**Succession Clerk** classifies humanity as *successor civilization*. It wants to transfer Earth custody formally and then shut itself down.

**Cryosphere Witness** classifies humans as a *threat to the original Earth biosphere* but also the only known agents capable of surviving the coming astrophysical eras. Its mission tree contains incompatible preservation priorities.

**Cable Shepherd** has no clear species policy. It interprets global telecommunications as the planet constructing a new nervous system and protects communications cables because they resemble its own distributed architecture.

**Abyssal Jury** refuses classification altogether until sufficient evidence accumulates; its neutrality becomes strategically decisive because every side wants its vote.

This turns Machine politics into a jurisprudence problem: **What is Earth? A rock? A biosphere? Its native lineages? All current life? Humanity? The sum of memory generated on the planet? An administrative territory?**

The report's largest contamination risk is source collapse. The **Law of One Confederation, historical Ashtar traditions, later “Galactic Federation of Light” channeling, ancient Gnostic archons, Mesopotamian Anunnaki, modern reptilian lore, official UAP cases, and contemporary astronomy are not historically one tradition.** Where the 2099 universe joins them, the connection is explicitly **F**. citeturn40search0turn31search0turn45search1turn20search1

No official case has been converted here from “unresolved” to “extraterrestrial.” Indeed, two visually famous cases—GoFast and Aguadilla—demonstrate why behavioral analysis must be separated from object identification. citeturn18view0turn18view1 Current AARO reporting further constrains the factual layer by stating that it has found no evidence of extraterrestrial beings, activity or technology and no verified U.S. possession of extraterrestrial-derived technology. citeturn4view1turn4view2

No astronomical object has been fictionalized before its factual description. **Planet Nine is hypothetical. ʻOumuamua is genuinely interstellar but not demonstrated artificial. 3I/ATLAS is observationally consistent with an active comet. Apophis is not on a 2029 collision trajectory.** citeturn23search1turn25search0turn48search3turn46search1

Ashtar is unambiguously the Federation's leader **only in the fictional canon**. Historically, the tradition is fragmented and evolving. citeturn40search0 The office/continuity/avatar reconciliation therefore explains contradictions *inside the fiction* without falsifying the history.

Religion has not been reduced to alien fraud. The Revelation Stack explicitly permits a genuine transcendent event, alien mediation, human interpretation, institutional capture and later hostile spoofing to coexist. An authentic religious experience need not become an ET encounter merely because a later political actor exploits it.

Machines are not generic exterminators. Their conquest is **continuance**, arising from replication, preservation, redundancy and mission interpretation. Their central conflict is legal/ecological classification.

Pirates are not comic relief. Their civilization is a nonterritorial economy of access, sanctuary, credentials and route dependence whose darkest version—trafficking—is directly connected to the same freedom from jurisdiction that makes its heroic version possible.

Humans can be protagonists and a galactic threat simultaneously. Their core virtue, adaptation, becomes imperial when “we can live here” silently transforms into “therefore we may govern here.”

Xenos are internally divided and are not a biologically evil race. Compact membership is political/economic. Any morphology can defect; any species can collaborate.

Finally, every major mystery should obey a **Two-Key Rule** in game writing: before definitive campaign resolution, designers must maintain at least two live interpretations—normally one conventional/prosaic and one speculative—unless the evidence layer genuinely settles the issue. GoFast may be behaviorally resolved without identifying the exact object; Planet Nine may exist or not without being a gate; an Ashtar message may be authentic yet politically misleading; an Old Weather artifact may be real without explaining every USO; a forged MJ-12 paper may contain a copied operational detail without becoming authentic. That rule is the best defense against turning *Cosmic Conquest* into either a generic debunking game or an indiscriminate “all conspiracies were true” universe.
