
# Cosmic Conquest AI Canon Context 0.5.0

Use this as the compact context primer before editing game-facing content.

## Fixed premises

- Campaign begins in 2099.
- Five political factions only: Humans, Federation, Xenos/Extraction Compact, Pirates, Machines/Continuance.
- Conquest verbs: Settle, Accord, Bind, Route, Continue.
- Xeno is political, never a species label.
- Ashtar is Supreme Commander and First Speaker in fiction; historical Ashtar traditions remain fragmented contactee history.
- Old Weather is the ancient Machine ecology; Vigil is neutral legacy enforcement; Vigil is the playable Machine polity.
- Source Lattice is transit, authentication, shared-memory, and noetic-coherence infrastructure, not unlimited psychic energy.
- Religion is handled through the Revelation Stack, not a universal alien hoax.
- UAP records preserve provenance; unresolved never means extraterrestrial by default.
- Archive status and attribution status are separate. `active_archive`, `withheld`, `no_records`, and `appealed` do not identify an object.
- Black Vault OCR and transcripts are derivatives; the underlying government file remains the preferred source.
- Stable game IDs, mechanics, seeds, saves, and multiplayer determinism are not changed by lore.
- Myth systems are source genealogies and optional fiction layers, never automatic confirmation that a conspiracy was true.
- Reptiloid is morphology; the Serpent Court is a political continuity institution. No real population is an alien bloodline.
- Documented eugenics, MKULTRA, weather modification, secrecy, or disinformation do not establish one unified depopulation, mind-control, or world-government plan.
- Time manipulation is separated into retrocognition, information backflow, branch selection, and rare physical chronology transit.
- *Inside Job* is a design corpus for bureaucratic incentives and competing secret systems, not evidence.

## Game frame

A campaign is a five-system Source-Lattice pentad with seven registered world-slots per system. Each battlefield is a legacy jurisdiction. The Vigil releases neutral enforcement waves. Echo Reversal reconstructs a destroyed hostile as a temporary state echo sent toward the rival. Stars measure stability of victory; SOULS are Source-Oriented Unified Lattice Signatures.

## Archive fidelity rules

- A live catalog, a bulk export, an agency transfer, a processed mirror, and a local index may describe different valid release states. Record all dates instead of declaring one surface final.
- Media must be labeled as native sensor product, documented export, secondary display capture, altered copy, artistic reconstruction, or narrative-only record.
- A code name may identify a proposal, briefing, proposed compartment, approved program, funded operation, or completed operation. Never skip maturity levels.
- An appeal that compels a records search establishes the search obligation, not the alleged program.
- An ordinary laboratory result remains valuable evidence; an extraordinary provenance story does not outrank composition and custody.
- Narrative reports are first-class evidence objects but remain separate from sensor conclusions and attribution.
