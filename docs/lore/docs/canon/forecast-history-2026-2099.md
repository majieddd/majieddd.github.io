# Forecast History: 2026-2099

This chronology combines **verified contemporary anchors and current mission plans** with clearly marked fictional divergence. Future plans can change; the factual layer should be reviewed whenever missions, dates, or scientific status change.

| Date | Layer | Event and canon use |
|---|---|---|
| **2026** | A | The National Archives continues the rolling UAP Records Collection in Record Group 615; The Black Vault expands searchable FOIA archives. These become the ancestry of Vanta's Open Archive doctrine. |
| **August 30, 2026** | A, planned | NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is scheduled to launch. In canon, Roman-era survey practices normalize enormous public sky datasets and later make hidden-object claims harder to sustain. |
| **2027** | A, planned / F | NASA plans a low-Earth-orbit demonstration of commercial human landing systems and critical rendezvous/docking operations. Fiction makes interoperability standards politically valuable enough that lunar crews begin asserting jurisdiction over the infrastructure they maintain. |
| **2028** | A, planned / F | NASA targets Artemis IV as a crewed lunar surface landing and anticipates continued surface missions. Dragonfly planning also points toward a 2028 launch to Titan. Fiction adds the first durable legal split between Earth authorities and permanently off-world technical communities. |
| **April 13, 2029** | A / F | Apophis safely passes about 32,000 km above Earth's surface. OSIRIS-APEX studies it afterward. Fiction adds a tiny, openly archived timing residual that becomes meaningful only decades later; Apophis is not a weapon or impactor. |
| **2030** | A, planned or projected / F | Europa Clipper is scheduled to reach Jupiter; NASA plans to transition from the ISS toward commercial low-Earth-orbit stations; the IEA projects renewables plus nuclear reaching about half of global electricity generation. Fiction treats infrastructure standards, commercial habitats, and energy networks as the beginning of continuous multi-jurisdiction space governance. |
| **2030s** | Inference / F | LunaNet-style interoperable communications, navigation, search-and-rescue, and timing services expand around the Moon through government and commercial providers. Fiction turns network compatibility and authentication into the legal foundation of early off-world citizenship. |
| **2034** | A, planned / F | Dragonfly is planned to reach Titan. Fiction adds evidence that complex chemistry repeatedly produces structures useful to noetic interfaces without implying present-day life or artifacts. |
| **2036** | Inference / F | **Watcher Mesh Standards:** event-based optical sensors, multi-sensor triangulation, native-master hashing, and public calibration registries become routine. Fiction extends these practices into a Solar System provenance network. |
| **2040s** | Inference / F | Successors to Roman, Webb, Rubin, and direct-imaging observatories make enormous anomaly catalogs normal. Space-resource law remains contested while no single biosignature produces uncontested contact. |
| **2041** | F | **Candidate Zero:** unrelated systems image the same manufactured object in a dynamically quiet Solar location. It does not communicate and cannot yet be attributed. |
| **2046** | F | **Signal Winter:** multiple high-profile technosignature and UAP claims collapse under contamination, synthetic media, and provenance failures. Serious anomaly work retreats into restricted scientific/security programs. |
| **2050s** | Projection / F | Climate adaptation, distributed energy, aging populations, automation, and off-world industry produce many durable Human polities instead of one world government. |
| **2051** | F | **Open-Sky Compact:** rival states, habitats, universities, and commercial observatories standardize minimum anomaly metadata because no actor can monopolize Solar sensing. |
| **2054** | F | **Phase-Correlation Experiments:** multi-lab studies isolate a weak repeatable relationship between conscious state and a class of instrument residuals. This validates no historical paranormal system. |
| **2058** | F | **Noetic Authentication:** the residual becomes a reliable identity-continuity channel. It cannot read arbitrary thoughts and is not unlimited energy. |
| **2061** | F | **Abyssal Reply:** an oceanic legacy system returns a valid authentication response. It is later recognized as Old Weather, but at the time it provides permissions rather than explanation. |

| **2072** | F | **Solar Sovereignty Crisis:** lunar, Martian, Belt, and Jovian polities cease accepting Earth's automatic right to negotiate for all Humans. |

## Forecast lanes rather than one prophecy

The route from 2026 to 2099 is a scenario architecture. It should remain plausible under several real futures:

| Lane | Real trend anchor | 2099 consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Open infrastructure** | LunaNet, commercial stations, shared navigation standards, public sky surveys. | Human polities inherit strong interoperability norms and fight over who controls standards rather than whether standards exist. |
| **Fragmented sovereignty** | Commercial habitats, national programs, private launch systems, unresolved space-resource law. | Earth cannot automatically speak for lunar, Martian, Belt, or Jovian populations. |
| **Climate adaptation inequality** | Rising coastal risk and wide scenario ranges for warming and sea level. | Some Human factions emerge from public adaptation networks; others from fortified corporate continuity zones. |
| **Demographic divergence** | Global aging, migration-driven growth in many states, and a projected population peak late in the century. | Different settlements define family, personhood, work, and citizenship differently before alien contact. |
| **Energy abundance with bottlenecks** | Rapid low-emissions electricity growth alongside grid, storage, mineral, and geopolitical constraints. | Power becomes cheaper while trusted materials, cooling, computation, and route access remain strategic. |
| **Sensor custody** | Event-based sensing, triangulation, native-master hashing, calibrated metadata, and reproducible derivation. | By 2099, maintaining custody and provenance is a strategic resource distinct from identifying a target. |
| **Archive expansion** | NARA RG 615, AARO releases, FOIA archives, machine-readable public astronomy. | Disclosure is not one document dump; it is a century-long conflict over provenance, classification, and access. |
| **Noetic false starts** | Present anomalous-cognition claims lack an accepted engineering foundation. | The 2058 breakthrough follows decades of failures and validates authentication only, not every prior spiritual claim. |

Writers may vary the severity of climate, geopolitics, and technological progress, but they should preserve the structural outcome: by 2099 Humanity is interconnected, technologically capable, and politically incapable of granting one uncontested consent signature for the species.
