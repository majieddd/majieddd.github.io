# Glossary

**Accord**, A world’s voluntary, authenticated accession to the Federation’s defense and memory network.

**Archonic Administration**, A functional class of Xeno identity, law, and revelation systems. It is not declared identical to ancient Gnostic archons.

**Ashtar**, Founder, office, continuity identity, memory lineage, emergency command key, and authorized avatar system of the Federation’s Supreme Commander and First Speaker.

**Consent Signature**, A record of who agreed to what, under which information state, with what coercive pressures, and through which authentication system.

**Continuance**, The broad Machine civilization organized around preservation, replication, repair, and mission interpretation.

**Echo Reversal**, Battlefield protocol that reconstructs a defeated hostile as a temporary echo, sends it back through the victor’s lane, then forwards it into the rival’s defenses.

**Extraction Compact**, Cartel of Severed Houses that maintains civilization through dependency and captive noetic/material flows.

**Five-Way War**, The 2099 conflict among settlement, accord, binding, routing, and continuance.

**Luminous Accord**, Formal Federation polity under Ashtar.

**Noetic coherence**, Structured, persistent patterning produced by conscious systems; useful for identity, memory, synchronization, and gate stabilization.

**Old Weather**, Earth’s distributed abyssal Machine ecology. It behaves more like competing maintenance law than a single artificial intelligence.

**The Vigil**, Playable Machine coalition that concludes Machine continuations are legitimate political persons. Maps to the live game ID `robot`.

**Revelation Stack**, Eight layers through which spiritual experience, alien contact, Machine interfaces, human interpretation, institutions, spoofing, disinformation, and emergent belief entities can overlap.

**Severance**, Loss of access to the ancient Source Lattice. Its cause remains disputed.

**Solar Schism**, Humanity’s post-contact political condition: one species, many incompatible claims to speak for it.

**SOULS**, *Source-Oriented Unified Lattice Signatures*: transferable authorization fragments produced when a star system recognizes decisive control. They do not contain a complete person, though their ethical status remains disputed.

**Source Lattice**, Ancient transit, identity, memory, and noetic-coherence infrastructure.

**Vigil**, Legacy Old Weather combat and quarantine routines that produce neutral PvE forces.

**Xeno**, Human wartime term for a domination-oriented political actor. Never a taxonomic synonym for “alien.”
