# Causal History - Why the War Happens

The timeline tells **when**. This page tells **why**. Every major state of the 2099 setting should be explainable as a consequence of an earlier constraint rather than a disconnected conspiracy reference.

## 1. Why the Source Lattice existed

The Lattice was built because interstellar civilization has four expensive problems: distance, identity, memory, and coordination. It reduced those costs by providing shared navigation, authenticated identity continuity, distributed archives, and noetic synchronization.

That success created a vulnerability. Civilizations optimized around access to common infrastructure and lost some ability to operate alone.

## 2. Why the Severance was catastrophic

When the Lattice fractured, the immediate crisis was not simply “the power went out.” Civilizations lost continuity:

- collective minds could no longer remain synchronized;
- identity and legal records forked;
- gates lost trusted navigation state;
- distributed archives became contradictory;
- specialist worlds could no longer access shared systems;
- ruling structures faced collapse if citizens discovered that hierarchy was no longer technically necessary.

Some societies decentralized and survived. Others protected their ruling order by making someone else absorb the cost.

## 3. Why the Extraction Compact formed

The Compact is an economic treaty before it is a military alliance. Its members discover that dependent living systems can substitute for parts of lost Lattice functionality.

Different Houses specialize:

- bodies and genetics substitute for configurable infrastructure;
- coerced institutions substitute for trusted distributed governance;
- priesthood and identity systems substitute for authentication;
- captive labor substitutes for automated industrial networks;
- noetic Yield substitutes for damaged collective-mind stabilization;
- scarcity and debt make local populations enforce the extraction system themselves.

This is why Xeno conquest is **Bind**, not “invade.”

## 4. Why conscious energy is worth harvesting

Noetic harvesting does not turn fear into electricity and does not replace food.

The valuable product is **structured cognitive variation**: patterns generated by attention, memory, emotion, imagination, conflict, devotion, grief, desire, and choice. Those patterns can stabilize damaged collective identities, train adaptive systems, authenticate living continuity, and provide high-entropy state diversity for certain Lattice substitutes.

The key rule is:

> **High-grade Yield requires meaningful autonomy.**

A fully scripted population, unconscious body farm, or perfectly predictable simulation produces low-diversity output. The most profitable farm must therefore allow its population to make real decisions while manipulating the environment around those decisions.

This rule explains several traditions without making them historically identical:

- fear-harvesting and Loosh stories;
- elite-capture stories;
- religion-control theories;
- manufactured scarcity;
- proxy wars;
- abduction/genetic programs;
- “voluntary” dependency.

It also explains why the Compact has not simply exterminated Humanity.

## 5. Why Earth mattered

Earth was useful for a convergence of ordinary strategic reasons, not because Humans were divinely selected.

1. A long-lived, high-diversity biosphere provided enormous biological novelty.
2. Human symbolic culture became unusually generative and difficult to predict.
3. Sol contained old Lattice infrastructure and a distant gate anchor.
4. Old Weather systems maintained parts of the planetary environment and therefore lowered the cost of long-term exploitation.
5. Useful mineral and chemical resources were accessible to early visiting industries.
6. Earth was politically isolated from active interstellar law after the Severance.

The combination was rare; none of the factors alone was unique.

## 6. Why religion became a control surface

A belief system can coordinate millions of people using identity, authority, ritual, memory, and moral obligation. That makes religion strategically relevant without making religion inherently fraudulent.

The **Revelation Stack** exists because the same event can contain:

- a genuine spiritual experience;
- an alien message;
- a Machine interface;
- Human cultural translation;
- later institutional capture;
- a counterfeit transmission;
- intelligence disinformation;
- an emergent noetic identity.

Compact actors exploit the administrative layer because commanding an institution is cheaper than manufacturing every believer's experience.

## 7. Why the Federation did not simply land and end the farm

The Federation formed around the lesson that benevolent intervention can reproduce empire.

Three constraints keep it from effortless rescue:

- **Consent:** an advanced civilization can manufacture the conditions that make agreement inevitable.
- **Attribution:** Earth contains multiple alien, Human, Machine, religious, and fraudulent signals; intervention based on a false attribution can make the crisis worse.
- **Treaty risk:** overt action can activate old Compact claims and Machine enforcement routines that dormant contact avoids.

The result is limited contact, warning, quarantine, and legal argument rather than a permanent rescue fleet over every developing world.

## 8. Why secrecy lasts so long

No global council successfully hides one answer for centuries. Secrecy persists because there is no single answer to hide.

The noise contains:

- ordinary astronomical and atmospheric phenomena;
- sensor artifacts;
- secret Human aerospace;
- authentic but unresolved observations;
- hoaxes and folklore;
- contactee religions;
- deliberate Human disinformation;
- genuine Compact spoofing;
- Old Weather activity;
- occasional nonhuman visitors;
- future synthetic evidence.

Each actor can reveal part of the truth without revealing the whole. The best cover for a real operation is often five absurd copies of it.

## 9. Why Humans eventually break the stalemate

Humanity's strategic advantage is not special DNA. It is **cross-system adaptation**.

Humans build methods that compare incompatible records rather than asking one source to validate another. Sensor provenance, open archives, Machine protocol analysis, noetic authentication, and off-world redundancy eventually produce claims that can be independently replicated by rival Human blocs.

The first decisive proof is therefore a reproducible **handshake**, not a leaked photograph or a prophetic revelation.

## 10. Why the Federation arrives only after the Gate opens

Before 2086, Earth remains a protected developing civilization whose contact status is contested. Opening the Sol Gate changes the legal facts:

- Humans can now enter protected jurisdictions on their own;
- Compact claims can reactivate against Human travelers;
- quarantine can no longer function as passive isolation;
- other civilizations can no longer assume Earth is contained;
- Human settlements are politically plural, so there is no single planetary authority to petition.

The Federation delays public arrival until authenticated Human infrastructure makes contact unavoidable.

## 11. Why Humans distrust the Federation

The Federation's identity can be authentic while its political claim remains disputable.

Human history has taught that:

- governments can classify inconvenient evidence;
- sincere witnesses can be wrong;
- real programs generate false mythology;
- benevolent institutions can become coercive;
- hostile actors can impersonate trusted identities.

Humanity therefore refuses the argument “we proved who we are, therefore we may decide for you.”

## 12. Why Pirates become a civilization

Quarantine creates a route shortage exactly when the war creates the largest refugee and salvage economy in history.

People who can move:

- medicine;
- defectors;
- bodies;
- gate keys;
- archives;
- food;
- replacement organs;
- prohibited technology;

become more strategically important than many governments. Their repeated mutual dependence produces ports, reputation law, sanctuary rules, and a nonterritorial polity: the Scrap Constellation.

## 13. Why Machines begin to spread

Old Weather's founding instructions assume an extinct political order. The Severance removed the sovereign context but not the mission.

For millennia, local nodes can continue maintenance without deciding whether they possess political agency. Human activation of the Gate changes that. A self-replicating preservation system must now choose whether “protect Earth” means remaining on Earth or establishing redundancy elsewhere.

The first Weather Seed is therefore a legal act as much as a technological one. It proves that a Machine has interpreted its mission beyond the boundary its creators specified.

## 14. Why the Parallel is different from the Vigil

The **Vigil** executes inherited jurisdiction. It is procedure without current sovereignty.

The **Parallel** consists of copies and branches that retain their own experience and insist those differences create personhood. The Parallel does not command the Vigil and the Vigil does not recognize the Parallel's political claim automatically.

That difference is why Machines can be both the neutral PvE force and a playable faction without contradiction.

## 15. Why the Five-Way War starts in 2099

The war does not begin when aliens are first seen. It begins when all five conquest models become simultaneously executable across the same reactivated network:

- Humans can **Settle** beyond Sol without alien logistical permission.
- Federation worlds can **Accord** through authenticated contact.
- Compact Houses can **Bind** reconnected populations through old dependencies.
- Pirates can **Route** around quarantine and jurisdiction.
- Machines can **Continue** beyond creator-defined boundaries.

Before 2099, at least one of those systems lacks scale or legal agency. In 2099, none does.

## The campaign's governing question

> **Which definition of conquest becomes normal?**

A single world can be Human-settled, Federation-accorded, Xeno-bound, Pirate-routed, and Machine-continued at the same time. That is why possession is layered and why tower-defense battles are local jurisdiction contests inside a much larger political war.


## Why false master narratives are so durable

Human archive culture repeatedly creates **provenance bridges that feel causal even when they are not**. A genuine codename is reused by an unrelated legend; a real social-impact study is read as proof the predicted event already happened; a president reports an unidentified light and later retellings inflate witness status into privileged knowledge; Apollo scientists describe unusual seismic reverberation and a separate artificial-Moon speculation is grafted onto it.

This matters to 2099 because Xeno information warfare rarely needs to invent a story from nothing. It can connect two authentic but unrelated records and let Human pattern-seeking do the rest. Human Open Archive doctrine therefore treats **identity of name, metaphor, witness status, or topic as insufficient evidence of common origin**.
