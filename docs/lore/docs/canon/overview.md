# Canon Overview

## The premise

In 2099, the Sol Gate opens into a galaxy whose ancient commons broke long ago. The surviving Source Lattice recognizes infrastructure and identity records but cannot decide which political system deserves control. Five powers therefore conquer in five different ways.

| Faction | Verb | What conquest means |
|---|---|---|
| Humanity | **Settle** | Build self-reproducing institutions, industry, and jurisdiction. |
| Federation of Light | **Accord** | Join worlds through informed consent, shared defense, and memory. |
| Extraction Compact | **Bind** | Make strategic choices depend on controlled biological, economic, institutional, or noetic flows. |
| Scrap Constellation | **Route** | Control access, sanctuary, credentials, salvage, and movement. |
| Continuance | **Continue** | Replicate, repair, preserve, and interpret inherited missions. |

The neutral Vigil does not recognize modern banners. Its jurisdictions generate the PvE wave, and Echo Reversal turns destruction into pressure against rival claims.
