# Continuity Rules

These rules outrank individual dossiers, dialogue, scenario pitches, and research notes.

## Hard canon

1. **Exactly five top-level factions** exist in the game and universe bible.
2. **Species is not faction.** Greys, reptiloids, Mantids, humanoids, aquatics, synthetics, and noetic beings can appear in multiple powers.
3. **Ashtar leads the Federation** in fiction. Historical Ashtar traditions remain fragmented in research.
4. **The game conflict begins in 2099.** Later dates are sequels or campaign futures.
5. **The Vigil is a Machine splinter.** It maps to the live game’s `robot` origin.
6. **The Vigil is neutral PvE.** It is legacy Old Weather enforcement, not the same polity as the Vigil.
7. **Xeno is political.** It means an actor participating in domination, extraction, or engineered dependency.
8. **Noetics is not free energy.** It concerns patterned information, coherence, identity, memory, and field coupling.
9. **Religion remains layered.** The setting never announces that all faith was fabricated by aliens.
10. **Officially unresolved cases stay unresolved in the factual layer.** Fictional reuse must be labeled.
11. **Every major mystery keeps two live interpretations** until a campaign intentionally resolves it.
12. **Mechanics lead integration.** Lore explains the game’s strong existing asymmetries rather than demanding a rebalance merely for prose consistency.
13. **Mega-conspiracy is a source-merger category.** Real programs, historical traditions, fiction, hoaxes, and unresolved reports never collapse into one hidden authority by default.
14. **No real population becomes an alien bloodline.** Reptilian, cabal, depopulation, and bloodline material must be stripped of antisemitic, racist, anti-Catholic, anti-Masonic, anti-migrant, or other scapegoating mutations.
15. **Time manipulation has explicit classes.** Retrocognition, information backflow, branch selection, and physical chronology transit have different evidence, costs, and legal consequences.

## Compression rules for the game

The game may use short labels that compress a larger truth:

- `HUMANITY` represents the Solar Schism’s many blocs.
- `FEDERATION OF LIGHT` represents the Luminous Accord and Ashtar Command.
- `THE XENO` is legacy player-facing shorthand for the Extraction Compact.
- `THE PIRATES` represents the Scrap Constellation.
- `THE VIGIL` represents one playable Continuance coalition.

Compression is acceptable. Biological essentialism, unsupported factual claims, and contradictions with core mechanics are not.

## Promotion rule

A research concept becomes canon only when all four are stated:

1. The real source lineage.
2. The evidence class.
3. The fictional change made to it.
4. The playable function it serves.

## Chronology rules added in 0.5.0

16. **2099 is the playable convergence, not first contact.** Artifact proof, gate activation, informal living contact, and public Federation contact occur earlier so commanders, towers, and mixed technology have history before the campaign starts.
17. **Every future hinge needs a cause and consequence.** Do not add an event solely because a conspiracy reference is interesting. It must change capability, authority, evidence, dependence, or route access.
18. **High-grade noetic Yield requires meaningful autonomy.** This is the canonical reason extraction prefers manipulated societies over passive livestock or fully scripted simulations.
19. **Planet Nine and the Sol Anchor are separate objects.** Modern Planet Nine astronomy remains natural; the artificial installation is F.
20. **Proposal is not program; program is not operation.** Cold War studies, FOIA records, and alleged black projects must preserve maturity and provenance.
21. **Ancient Human sophistication is not an alien gap-filler.** Gobekli Tepe, Antikythera, and other archaeological surprises remain Human unless a separate F artifact is explicitly introduced.
