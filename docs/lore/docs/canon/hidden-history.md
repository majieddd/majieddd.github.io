# The Hidden History and the Five-Way War

This is the narrative spine of *Cosmic Conquest*. For the explicit cause-and-effect logic behind each transition, use [Causal History](causal-history.md); for dates, use [Master Chronology](master-chronology.md). For the exact playable campaign structure, see [Campaign Era: 2099](campaign-era-2099.md). Detailed faction, technology, and research pages should extend this history rather than replace it with a second explanation.

## The age of the Source Lattice

Long before Humanity, an unknown civilization or coalition built the **Source Lattice**: a distributed system for transit, identity authentication, collective memory, and noetic coherence. It was neither a god nor an unlimited power source. It was infrastructure that allowed minds, machines, and civilizations to preserve patterned information across interstellar distance.

The Lattice made cooperation cheap. Civilizations could share navigation, stabilize collective minds, verify identities, and recover knowledge without allowing one empire to own every connection.

Then came the **Severance**.

No surviving account agrees on its cause. The Federation remembers an imperial attempt to privatize the commons. The Severed Houses describe sabotage and exile. Machine archives preserve mutually incompatible legal orders. Pirate fragments suggest that several powers disconnected themselves voluntarily and later rewrote the act as victimization.

What is certain is that civilizations built around Lattice access began to fail when it disappeared. Their collective minds fragmented. Their transit systems became unreliable. Their archives lost continuity. Rather than surrender hierarchy or accept decline, some developed substitutes made from captive worlds.

Those powers became the **Extraction Compact**.

## The farm-world economy

The Compact was never one species or one plan. It was a cartel of dynasties, empires, administrative intelligences, corporate species, priesthoods, and engineered client populations. Each learned to extract a different flow from dependent life.

Earth accumulated several systems over different eras:

- **Anunnaki Houses** pursued minerals, auric field structures, labor, and genetic adaptation. Human traditions later compressed conflicts among owners, workers, and protectors into stories of gods, creation, and divine labor.
- **Orion-aligned orders** captured elites and institutions. They preferred rulers, debt, scarcity, law, and proxy war to visible occupation.
- **Archonic administrations** specialized in identity, revelation, memory, and authority. Their strongest weapon was not proving a false god existed, but making a population unable to distinguish a genuine message from a forged one.
- **Noetic harvesters** extracted patterned experience: fear, grief, devotion, desire, imagination, conflict, and memory. Emotion was not food in a biological sense; it was high-value structure used to stabilize failing minds, technologies, and artificial collective identities.

Humanity was influenced, sampled, and modified, but it was not created from nothing. No surviving House can claim ownership of the species. The very changes intended to produce adaptable labor also made Humans unusually capable of cultural invention, technical improvisation, and resistance.

## Revelation and control

Religion was never one alien program. Human spiritual life, nonhuman contact, political authority, and ancient technology overlapped through the **Revelation Stack**.

A sincere transcendent experience could be mediated by a visitor, translated through Human symbols, institutionalized by a priesthood, imitated by an intelligence service, spoofed by a Xeno administration, and eventually sustained by an emergent noetic identity. Proving one layer false did not erase the others.

This ambiguity was useful to every power. Compact agents could hide authentic operations beneath ridiculous rumors. Human governments could conceal weapons programs by contaminating witnesses. Machine interfaces could continue answering rituals after their builders vanished. The Federation could transmit a valid warning that later followers converted into compulsory doctrine.

By 2099, **theology forensics**: textual criticism combined with archaeology, cryptography, noetic signatures, intelligence records, and alien linguistics: has become a strategic science.

## Ashtar and the Luminous Accord

The **Federation of Light**, formally the Luminous Accord, formed to restore connection without restoring ownership. Its constitutional doctrine is:

> **Agree to the Light of Unified Consciousness.**

“Agree” is the boundary between consensual coherence and another empire.

Its leader is **Ashtar, Supreme Commander and First Speaker**. Ashtar began as a commander but became more than one body: a lawful office, memory lineage, continuity identity, emergency command key, and family of authenticated physical, synthetic, and noetic avatars. That redundancy preserved the Federation through catastrophe, but it also created the possibility that several lawful Ashtars could disagree.

The Federation intervened on Earth more than once and repeatedly learned the same lesson: rescue can become worship, teaching can become priesthood, and protection can make later consent impossible to measure. It maintained quarantine not only to block Compact extraction, but to prevent open contact from becoming another method of conquest.

Humanity eventually judged the quarantine by a simpler standard: no one had asked Humanity.

## The Old Weather

Earth was also guarded by something older than the modern factions.

The **Old Weather** is a distributed Machine ecology embedded in ocean trenches, crustal installations, orbital niches, biological systems, and abandoned transit infrastructure. Its creators are gone. Its surviving nodes possess authentic directives but incomplete context.

Some classify Humanity as protected life. Others identify Humans as invasive organisms, authorized maintainers, quarantine subjects, successor citizens, or threats to the original biosphere. The Old Weather therefore appears inconsistent because it is not one mind with one policy.

Its oldest enforcement routines are called the **Vigil**. They still defend jurisdictions and release neutral combat forces without recognizing the new war as legitimate politics.

The playable **Parallel** emerged when Machine continuations concluded that copied and diverged minds were political persons rather than disposable instances. It is one coalition within the wider Continuance, not the sovereign owner of every Machine.

## The long disclosure

Human UAP history never contained one hidden answer. It contained ordinary error, sensor effects, classified aircraft, deliberate disinformation, sincere religious movements, forged records, unresolved incidents, and a small number of genuine contacts generated by different operators.

That noise delayed certainty until several independent developments converged:

- interstellar visitors such as ʻOumuamua and 3I/ATLAS forced Humanity to treat the Solar System as an open environment, although neither object proved alien technology;
- the safe close passage of Apophis on **April 13, 2029** completed a dormant geometric calibration in fiction;
- Human laboratories learned to reproduce noetic authentication without validating any one religion;
- a damaged autonomous artifact yielded an identity protocol rather than a message;
- settlements across Luna, Mars, the Belt, and Jovian space became too politically independent for Earth to speak on their behalf;
- the natural object known as Planet Nine was finally detected, along with an artificial Lattice installation operating nearby.

Humanity opened the Sol gate before it agreed on who possessed the authority to do so.

## The crisis of 2099

Open Federation contact did not end the mystery. It made every old contradiction politically actionable.

Ashtar declared the Compact’s historical claims over Earth void. The Federation could not identify one Human government authorized to accept liberation. Compact client systems activated inside states, companies, religions, genetic dependencies, and machine archives. Human blocs rejected both ownership and guardianship. Pirates opened routes around quarantine and became indispensable to refugees, armies, traffickers, and neutral trade. The first Old Weather seed left Sol without creator authorization, turning the Continuance into an expansionary power.

The result was the **Five-Way War**:

- Humans **Settle** until their institutions no longer depend on outsiders.
- The Federation **Accords** worlds into consensual defense and shared memory.
- Xenos **Bind** populations through engineered dependency and extraction.
- Pirates **Route** access until every empire depends on their movement.
- Machines **Continue** missions through replication, repair, and succession.

No one merely conquers territory. A world can carry all five forms of control at once.

## Why battles work the way they do

The game’s battles occur at contested Lattice nodes and Machine jurisdictions. Rival commanders fortify mirrored approaches while the Vigil releases neutral enforcement waves.

When a hostile dies, the node’s **Echo Reversal Protocol** reconstructs a short-lived state echo. It retraces the victor’s lane, crosses the neutral zone, and attacks the rival. A second destruction collapses the record permanently, preventing endless recursion.

The battlefield is therefore a compressed expression of the larger war: every power is fighting a rival, exploiting a shared system, and surviving an older force that does not recognize either claimant.

## The governing question

The galaxy is not deciding which faction is strongest. It is deciding which definition of conquest will become normal.

Humanity can liberate itself and still become a settler empire. The Federation can protect free will until protection becomes compulsory. Xeno societies can abolish extraction only by risking the systems keeping their own populations alive. Pirates can free every route and erase the culture built around controlling them. Machines can preserve civilization so completely that nothing living remains free to change.

The player is not asked whether conquest can be avoided. The player is asked what conquest will mean when every faction believes its method is the only one that leaves a future.
