# Mechanics Mapping

## Core PvPvE loop

Battles occur at contested Lattice jurisdictions. Rival commanders establish mirrored claims while the neutral Vigil enforces extinct safety law. Destroyed enemies are processed by Echo Reversal: the local mesh reconstructs an unstable state echo, sends it back through the victor's lane, and redirects it against the rival. A second death destroys the record.

## Faction mechanics

| Origin | Live identity | Canon explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Human | Broad coverage, universal terrain attunement | Hybrid engineering and rapid local standardization. |
| Federation | Defense, protection opening, sabotage resistance | Authenticated cooperation, warded infrastructure, and shared targeting. |
| Xeno | Wound scaling, conversion, stronger echoes | Extraction becomes more efficient as a target destabilizes. |
| Pirate | Plunder, overload, heat, direct disruption | Safety governors removed; access and risk are converted into firepower. |
| Robotic | Linked lattice, deterministic bootstrap, late scaling | Infrastructure synchronization and replication compound over time. |

## Campaign structure

- Five systems form a Source-Lattice pentad.
- Seven worlds are inherited jurisdiction slots.
- The final world is the system charter seat.
- Two worlds preserve competing active claims and become three-way battles.
- Standard, fortress, forge, and Vigil-nest kinds represent civil/infrastructural functions.

## Progression

- **SOULS:** Source-Oriented Unified Lattice Signatures, authorization fragments used for persistent unlocks.
- **Stars:** stability and completeness of the victory record.
- **Ascension:** material and noetic re-indexing beyond a tower's former identity and safety limits.
- **Lives:** command-enclave defensive depth and ability to keep the jurisdiction online.
- **Rescued units:** asylum, recruitment, contract, emancipation, copying, or mission transfer rather than one universal ownership event.
- **Boons:** recoverable institutional advantages produced by the conquered world.

## Layered sovereignty

The map shows one controlling banner, but a world can simultaneously have Human territory, Federation consent, Compact dependency, Pirate routes, Machine continuance, and a contested public revelation. Future UI may expose those layers without changing the current victory transaction.
