# Tower Arsenal Bible

All 60 current tower IDs and mechanics remain intact. **Origin is an engineering doctrine, not a racial lock.** A player can acquire a foreign tower through treaty, capture, rescue, salvage, licensing, reverse engineering, or Machine precedent.

The display copy should answer three questions in this order:

1. What does this tower do on the board?
2. Who originally built the doctrine and for what non-game reason?
3. What moral or political consequence follows from using it?

## Cross-origin exceptions are intentional

Several towers visibly contain foreign technology. The Human `ARC` and `RECLAIMER`, Machine `SINGULARITY`, Pirate mixed-origin arsenal, and Federation use of Echo Reversal should not be cleaned into single-faction purity. They demonstrate the setting's main rule: technology travels more easily than ideology, and users inherit consequences they may not understand.

## Upgrade and specialization copy

Upgrade names may remain punchy, but branch descriptions should identify the doctrinal choice:

- **scale versus precision**;
- **speed versus continuity**;
- **consent versus emergency authority**;
- **yield versus long-term stability**;
- **freedom versus accountability**;
- **replication versus individuality**.

This gives each mechanical branch an in-world argument rather than a random escalation adjective.
