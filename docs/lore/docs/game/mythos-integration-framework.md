# Mythos Integration Framework

This document translates the 2026 Alien and Space Mythos Atlas into game-facing
content without replacing the five-faction structure or changing current mechanics.

## Binding principles

1. The five factions remain Humans, Federation of Light, Xenos, Pirates, and Machines.
2. Myth systems are institutions, technologies, subcultures, historical layers, and
   mission premises - never additional top-level factions.
3. A real-world source receives its evidence label before any fictional adaptation.
4. The same visual archetype can be used by multiple operators.
5. No real ethnic, religious, national, medical, financial, or political population
   is secretly an alien species in canon.
6. A source-inspired plot may preserve one operational detail while its public story
   remains false or contaminated.
7. Satirical media such as *Inside Job* is a design corpus, not a factual source.

## New content families

### Character families

- **Chronology Court officers:** judges, archivists, branch witnesses, and temporal refugees.
- **Serpent Court continuities:** adaptive-body diplomats, legal successors, and defectors.
- **Nine-Key custodians:** Federation jurists and Machine protocol interpreters.
- **Continuity Clinic specialists:** memory doctors, identity advocates, and fraud examiners.
- **Open Archive mythographers:** Humans who trace how stories mutate through documents and media.
- **Atmospheric jurisdiction crews:** weather scientists, cloud-seeding operators, and Old Weather negotiators.
- **Anomaly-station personnel:** scientists defending sensor custody at recurrent-light and Cross-Layer Fault sites.
- **Conspiracy maintenance workers:** ordinary technicians who keep inherited secrecy systems running.
- **Ontology Cell analysts:** teams who share observations but disagree whether the operator is extraterrestrial, cryptoterrestrial, noetic, Machine, future-Human, deceptive, or theological.
- **Synthetic-revelation engineers:** experts in projection, drones, augmented reality, broadcast compromise, and noetic identity spoofing.
- **Noetic reconnaissance teams:** carefully blinded observers, statisticians, tasking officers, and provenance auditors.
- **Secret-space veterans:** people whose service records, memories, and chronology proofs disagree.
- **Warning-form interpreters:** structural engineers, folklorists, and Lattice technicians who must separate omen, cause, and retrospective association.

### Planet and battlefield families

- **Polar Null Site:** ice, buried relay, contradictory postwar archives.
- **Route Shadow:** official map and actual transit network disagree.
- **Cross-Layer Fault:** geology, noetics, sensors, and actors produce different observations.
- **Coherence Reservoir:** sacred/technical site whose energy depends on consent history.
- **Ancestor Vault:** conscious historical simulation with disputed citizenship.
- **Population Ledger World:** demographic optimization built into infrastructure.
- **Luminous Valley:** recurring lights monitored by neutral instruments.
- **Tissue Survey Zone:** ecological and genetic sampling with mixed causes.
- **Temporal Fork World:** records, memories, and terrain belong to adjacent histories.
- **Deep Jurisdiction:** cryptoterrestrial habitat intertwined with Old Weather systems.
- **Revelation Stage:** dense city where every screen, drone, headset, and noetic receiver can be synchronized into a local false contact.
- **Solar Warden Relay:** a distributed sensor and catalog node later mistaken for an off-world fleet headquarters.
- **Collider Key Site:** accelerator complex that accidentally repeats a dormant Lattice handshake while prank footage poisons public trust.
- **Consensus Retreat:** formally powerless private forum whose access network changes policy without issuing orders.
- **Warning Zone:** recurrent manifestations cluster around infrastructure already near failure.

## Commander retrofit opportunities

### Humanity

- **Vanta:** becomes the leading myth-genealogy and archive-version specialist.
- **Nyx:** temporal and reality-editing technology tempts her redline philosophy.
- **Orin:** investigates weather and ionospheric technology stripped of conspiracy mythology.
- **Vess:** confronts Continuity Directorate emergency population controls.
- **Korrin:** discovers hidden programs through procurement and logistics rather than leaked confession.

### Federation

- **Seraph:** defends the difference between consensual coherence and devotional extraction.
- **Halder:** sees the Mercy Census as proof that protection must sometimes override refusal.
- **Lumen:** authenticates Ashtar claims, Nine Keys, and counterfeit revelation.
- **Mira:** investigates continuity clinics and postmortem consent.
- **Aurel:** oversees evacuation protocols vulnerable to cultic and Xeno imitation.

### Xenos

- **Mawlord:** seeks Causal Return Grid access to stabilize Severed identity.
- **Sevra:** is a natural Serpent Court legal-continuity operator.
- **Dregg:** turns Population Ledger optimization into military policy.
- **Ulgrim:** treats biological sampling as asset acquisition.
- **Nym:** weaponizes uncertainty by surrounding real operations with absurd versions.

### Pirates

- **Rake:** uses Route Shadows and mythic geography to evade every authority.
- **Scarlet:** treats timeline uncertainty as permission to act before evidence settles.
- **Grist:** makes useful hardware from discredited projects and hoax props.
- **Cinder:** exploits weather-control panic to conceal ordinary raids.
- **Vex:** trades identities, archive versions, and one-use backflow keys.

### Machines

- **Axiom:** argues that conscious simulations and branch copies possess personhood.
- **Nyx-R:** optimizes branch selection but rejects physical time travel as wasteful.
- **Lumen-R:** learns from identity-spoofing and Men-in-Black cleanup patterns.
- **Mawlord-R:** treats conspiracy corpora as training data and risks overfitting folklore.
- **Dregg-R:** audits the Continuity Calculus and discovers that optimization objectives are political.

## Asset direction

### Reptilian and serpent imagery

- Never make all reptiloids purple/evil or all Xenos reptilian.
- Serpent Court design should emphasize legal continuity: seals, layered masks, body records,
  inherited insignia, and multiple versions of one face.
- Federation and Pirate reptiloids must be visually normal members of those factions.

### Time and reality imagery

- Avoid generic clocks everywhere.
- Use conflicting timestamps, checksum halos, archive overlays, duplicate shadows, and
  geometry that almost aligns.
- Physical chrononauts should show severe containment and identity-stabilization equipment.

### Depopulation and population-policy imagery

- Avoid real political symbols and real demographic scapegoats.
- Use abstract ledgers, habitat-capacity maps, reproductive licenses, evacuation queues,
  and contradictory optimization targets.
- The threat should be the system and decision structure, not a racialized population.

### Mind control and noetic reconnaissance

- Real historical behavioral-abuse imagery should use institutional records, clinical rooms, consent gaps, and fragmented custody rather than supernatural hypnosis spirals.
- Noetic reconnaissance should show target blinding, competing taskers, confidence ranges, and false-positive risk.
- Project Monarch remains a later mutation, not a blanket label for all documented abuse.

### Secret space and lunar imagery

- Apollo hardware and exploration remain real in the setting. Later secret infrastructure must look chronologically and institutionally separate.
- Solar Warden should begin as catalogs, sensor relays, route plots, and duty rosters before any fleet image appears.
- Twenty-and-back characters need conflicting age markers, archived absence, and continuity equipment rather than generic supersoldier armor.

### Simulated revelation and elite-network imagery

- Blue Beam-style operations are local multisystem deception: screens, drones, AR, projection, compromised broadcasts, and noetic overlays.
- Private forums should look like access and consensus networks, not occult caricatures.
- Ontology Cells should use the same evidence wall with different explanatory overlays.

### Inside Job-inspired tone

- Secret offices should look worked-in, underfunded, inherited, and bureaucratic.
- Impossible systems need maintenance panels, forms, temporary fixes, and exhausted staff.
- Comedy can appear in logs and dialogue, but primary faction tragedy remains sincere.

## Implementation order

1. Add the Mythos Atlas and this framework to the lore wiki only.
2. Add Mythos Operation IDs to a data registry without loading them into the game.
3. Retrofit commander dossier text and relationships.
4. Add world tags and scenario hooks to deterministic planet dossiers.
5. Add dialogue variables and small pre-battle exchanges.
6. Commission new portraits, tower plates, and world art only after copy locks.
7. Implement any new mechanic in a separate balance-reviewed change.

## No-mechanics promise

This expansion does not itself change tower damage, costs, unit stats, campaign
randomness, saves, network state, scenario selection, or world generation. It supplies
lore, content tags, mission concepts, and future integration contracts.
