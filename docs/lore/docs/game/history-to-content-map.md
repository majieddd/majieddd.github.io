# History-to-Game Content Map

Use this page when revamping characters, towers, troops, planets, dialogue, loading screens, and campaign missions. It prevents historical references from becoming random flavor.

| Historical/canon era | Primary design purpose | Best faction usage | Content surfaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep Lattice / Severance | Establish the shared ancient strategic problem | Federation, Xeno, Machines | Relics, boss arenas, archive fragments, endgame choices |
| Earth concession era | Explain extraction without making one alien species responsible for everything | Xenos, Machines | Xeno towers, gene troops, noetic systems, Old Weather ruins |
| Ancient religious/source era | Show memory translation and Revelation Stack ambiguity | Federation, Xeno, Humans | Dialogue, archaeology worlds, theology-forensics missions |
| Premodern anomaly era | Demonstrate technology-shaped interpretation | Humans, Pirates | Archive missions, codex, translation puzzles |
| 1944-1969 official UFO era | Establish institutions, secrecy, sensor uncertainty | Humans | Archive War, research boons, military records |
| Cold War extreme-program era | Explain why secret-space rumors are culturally plausible | Humans, Pirates | Black-program ruins, decoy missions, false-flag dilemmas |
| 1970s-1990s myth merger | Populate contactee, Anunnaki, Archon, reptilian, depopulation, rescue, and hybrid source genealogies | Xeno, Federation | Religious factions, cults, counterfeit identities, client systems |
| 2004-2026 UAP data era | Teach provenance and sensor-chain logic | Humans | Tutorials, evidence missions, Archive War |
| 2029-2058 calibration/noetic era | Bridge real astronomy into fictional authentication | Humans, Machines | Technology tree flavor, research planets, dialogue |
| 2064-2086 artifact/gate era | Explain rapid technology mixing before open Federation contact | Humans, Machines, Pirates | Tower origins, salvage, commanders, gate worlds |
| 2089-2095 living-contact crisis | Establish commander biographies and faction recruitment | All | Commander dossiers, relationship dialogue, troop origins |
| 2096-2099 quarantine and Five-Way War | Direct playable premise | All | Galaxy map, battle intros, victory endings |

## Asset grounding rules

### Commanders

Every commander biography should include at least:

- birth or activation year;
- faction entry year;
- one event from the master chronology that changed their worldview;
- why their signature mechanic reflects their history;
- one relationship to another commander caused by a specific event;
- one belief about the Severance that may be wrong.

### Towers

Every tower needs:

- builder tradition;
- first deployment era;
- original noncombat or strategic purpose when applicable;
- reason for its current element/mechanic;
- how another faction obtained or copied the technology, if cross-origin versions ever appear.

### Troops and Vigil chassis

Each unit should answer:

- Was this body designed, born, copied, or repurposed?
- What did it do before the Five-Way War?
- What political authority does it believe it serves?
- What does death/reanimation mean to that ontology?

### Planets

Every campaign world should inherit:

1. a **deep layer**: Lattice, Machine, geological, or no deep history;
2. a **settlement layer**: who built the current civilization;
3. a **dependency layer**: what external system the world needs;
4. a **route layer**: how it connects to the galaxy;
5. a **revelation layer**: what its people believe about their past;
6. a **2099 crisis**: why control is contested now.

### Dialogue

Avoid exposition dumps. Dialogue should reveal disagreements over history.

Good:
- “Your archive says rescue. Mine says occupation.”
- “That key is authentic. Your authority is not.”
- “The treaty is real; the signatory wasn't.”

Weak:
- “As you know, the Source Lattice was built 6.4 million years ago...”

Chronology belongs in dossiers, tooltips, archives, planet histories, and post-battle unlocks; characters should argue over its implications.
