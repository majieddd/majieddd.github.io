## Doctrinal mechanics

- **Human Salvage Armor:** Human detachments turn battlefield wreckage into standardized protection. This is adaptation with a colonial shadow: everything becomes a compatible resource.
- **Federation Vow:** A shield can pass between signatories because the ward authenticates a shared defensive obligation. The Vow is scoped consent, not surrender of a soul.
- **Xeno Mass:** The Voracious Brood converts nearby death into growth. These five units are one Compact client-army, not the biology of every Xeno civilization.
- **Pirate Scuttle:** Pirate bodies and vehicles are designed to deny useful capture. Their deaths damage nearby infrastructure because autonomy includes deciding what survives you.
- **Machine Relay:** Vigil units leave forward nodes because every action is also infrastructure for the next instance.

## The Vigil is not a faction army

The neutral enemy roster is a family of legacy Old Weather chassis. Their shapes describe maintenance and enforcement functions that war has turned hostile.

| Vigil family | Current examples | Original mission | Battlefield behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Survey and pursuit** | Picket/Crawler, Outrunner/Sprinter, Mote/Mite, Lantern/Wisp | Inspect boundaries, carry readings, pursue unsafe traffic. | Baseline bodies, speed, flight, and early pressure. |
| **Containment** | Palisade/Bulwark, Aegis Sentinel, Ironmarch/Juggernaut | Hold hazardous material and survive environmental failure. | Armor, shielding, and heavy lives-cost. |
| **Service ecology** | Restorer/Mender, Divider/Cluster, Fragment/Spawnling, Foundry Carrier | Repair damaged nodes, divide workloads, and deliver replacement frames. | Healing, splitting, summoning, and reinforcement. |
| **Access control** | Interdictor/Jammer, Phase Courier/Blink, Ghost Chassis/Wraith, Nullifier | Deny unauthorized systems, pass sealed boundaries, or suppress unsafe functions. | Jamming, teleportation, phasing, and ability shutdown. |
| **Continuity enforcement** | Reconstructor/Revenant, Warden | Restore approved state and reject unauthorized displacement. | Revival and control resistance. |
| **Command pylons** | Marshal, Anchor, Cadence, Bastion | Publish local mission priorities to subordinate machines. | Auras for frenzy, anchoring, haste, and armor. |
| **Catastrophe frames** | Colossus, Vesper, Myriad, Oracle, Reclaimer, Harbinger | Execute emergency protocols too large for ordinary jurisdiction. | Miniboss and boss encounters with rules that cannot be solved by simple control effects. |

An enemy dossier should always state both the **current tactical behavior** and the **lost civilian or custodial purpose**. This keeps the Vigil tragic and alien without making it another evil robot swarm.
