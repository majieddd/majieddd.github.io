# Shadow Bureaucracy Design Principles

This module turns the strongest structural lessons from *Inside Job*, archive history, and conspiracy scholarship into original Cosmic Conquest design rules.

## The Cognito Checklist

Every covert institution needs:

| Question | Required answer |
|---|---|
| Mandate | What problem was it legally or practically created to solve? |
| Budget | Who pays, in what currency, and who can cut funding? |
| Staff | Who performs ordinary work and what do they want? |
| Procurement | Where do rare materials, bodies, software, and replacement parts come from? |
| Jurisdiction | Which world, population, medium, or timeline can it lawfully affect? |
| Oversight | Who can investigate, audit, or dissolve it? |
| Succession | Who inherits keys, memories, debts, and legitimacy? |
| Rivalry | Which office wants the same mission or budget? |
| Error rate | What mundane mistakes does it make? |
| Public interface | What cover organization or service touches ordinary life? |
| Failure state | What happens when it becomes visible or loses control? |

## Design prohibitions

- No omniscient cabal that flawlessly controls every faction.
- No “classified” label used as proof of the claimant’s preferred story.
- No real ethnic or religious group converted into an alien lineage.
- No conspiracy that lacks logistics, labor, maintenance, or institutional conflict.
- No timeline rewrite that erases its victims from moral accounting.

## Trust Debt

Trust Debt is generated by documented abuses, false denials, forged evidence, uncorrected archive errors, coercive emergency policy, and secret programs that later become public. High Trust Debt increases resistance to genuine warnings and makes counterfeit revelations cheaper. It does not raise the factual probability of unrelated claims.

## Gameplay hooks

- Budget votes can weaken a secret office without revealing it.
- Procurement raids expose technology provenance.
- Jurisdiction disputes create three-way battles without a villain faction.
- Succession crises generate multiple authentic command keys.
- Audit missions may prove misconduct without proving aliens.
- A cover story can be false while the concealed program is mundane.
- Rival offices can each possess one correct fragment and one damaging assumption.

## Character rule

Every commander needs a personal motive that survives if their faction doctrine is removed. Love, shame, grief, status, fear, ambition, guilt, curiosity, or loyalty should determine how they use institutional power.
