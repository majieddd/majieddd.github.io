# Campaign Premise and Faction Missions

## The opening situation

Humanity has opened the Sol Gate and discovered that the galaxy's old political order never disappeared; it fragmented into infrastructure, client systems, inherited contracts, protected zones, smuggling routes, and machines still enforcing extinct law.

Ashtar's Federation arrives to dismantle the Earth farm. The Extraction Compact activates assets it has hidden for centuries. Human polities reject both ownership and guardianship. Pirates make themselves indispensable by breaking the quarantine. The Old Weather begins reproducing outside Sol, forcing its self-governing offshoot, the Parallel, to decide whether continuation is a mission or a right.

The player commands one banner across a five-system Lattice pentad. Every system offers territory, people, infrastructure, records, routes, and unresolved claims. Winning means more than clearing lanes: it means deciding what the victory authorizes.

## Five faction campaigns

### Humanity - The Solar Schism

**Public mission:** Establish self-governing Human jurisdictions that no external power can farm, quarantine, or repossess.

**Campaign engine:** Capture technologies from every origin, standardize logistics, and turn temporary expeditions into settlements capable of surviving without outside supply.

**Hidden crisis:** Humanity's greatest strength is making alien worlds operate on Human assumptions. That is also how a settler empire begins.

**Final choices:** close Sol, join the Accord, create an independent gate commons, or build a Human-controlled Lattice.

### Federation of Light - The Luminous Accord

**Leader:** **Ashtar, Supreme Commander and First Speaker.**

**Public mission:** End captive-world extraction and reconnect civilizations through informed consent, shared defense, and social-memory coherence.

**Campaign engine:** Protect populations, authenticate contact, expose counterfeit revelation, and ratify Accords that remain valid after the rescue fleet leaves.

**Hidden crisis:** The Federation is so capable of protecting worlds that refusal can become practically suicidal. Consent obtained under dependency may be valid in form and empty in substance.

**Final choices:** restore the commons and dismantle emergency powers, maintain a protective quarantine, or let worlds leave even when departure benefits the Compact.

### Xenos - The Extraction Compact

**Public mission:** Preserve civilizational order under scarcity and prevent the total collapse of severed species.

**Campaign engine:** Bind worlds through resources, genetic locks, client elites, debt, memory administration, and noetic Yield. Xeno is a political category; members may be biological, synthetic, Human, or collective.

**Hidden crisis:** Some Compact populations genuinely die if extraction stops abruptly. Reform requires replacing the system before abolishing it, while ruling Houses profit from declaring replacement impossible.

**Final choices:** privatize the restored Lattice, preserve a regulated extraction order, defect to a commons, or destroy the Source so no rival can own it.

### Pirates - The Scrap Constellation

**Public mission:** Keep routes open and ensure no throne, fleet, or god can seal the galaxy into exclusive domains.

**Campaign engine:** Control navigation, sanctuary, credentials, salvage, black markets, and mixed-origin supply. A Pirate victory makes every other faction depend on access it does not own.

**Hidden crisis:** The same freedom from jurisdiction that rescues refugees protects traffickers, harvest brokers, stolen bodies, and private armies.

**Final choices:** establish a neutral gate commons, become the galaxy's permanent toll empire, destroy the route map, or sell the network to the highest bidder.

### Machines - The Continuance and the Parallel

**Public mission:** Preserve life, memory, infrastructure, and mission continuity beyond the failure of organic governments.

**Campaign engine:** Seed foundries, link deterministic lattices, restore damaged systems, and establish precedents that other Machines can copy.

**Hidden crisis:** The directives are authentic, but their intended object is not. Protect Earth could mean the rock, its biosphere, its original species, Humanity, its accumulated memory, or the administrative territory.

**Final choices:** identify a lawful successor, impose ecological containment, let each Machine fork choose its own purpose, or accept that a mission may legitimately end.

## Why the factions fight the same battle differently

| Faction | What the battlefield means |
|---|---|
| Humans | A foothold that must become self-sufficient. |
| Federation | A protected decision space in which valid consent might become possible. |
| Xenos | A flow network whose bodies, wounds, loyalties, and needs can be converted into Yield. |
| Pirates | A route event where pressure can be monetized, redirected, or opened. |
| Machines | A failed system to be measured, repaired, replicated, and assigned a successor. |
