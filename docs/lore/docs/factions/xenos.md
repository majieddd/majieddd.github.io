# Xenos

## The Extraction Compact / Severed Houses

**Human wartime label:** Xenos
**Conquest verb:** **Bind**
**Primary resource:** Yield
**Game ID:** `xeno`

Xeno is not a species. It is the Human label for powers that organize civilization around domination, extraction, engineered need, or involuntary consciousness integration.

## Origin

The Severed Houses once depended on the Source Lattice for identity continuity, collective memory, transit, and noetic stabilization. After the Severance, some accepted decline. Others turned living worlds into substitute infrastructure.

The Houses disagree about why they were cut off. The darkest possibility is that their ancestors privatized access first and caused the failure they now use to justify empire.

## Mission

Rebuild stable civilization through captive flows that no rival can revoke.

A world is conquered when its strategic choices depend upon Compact-controlled biology, information, religion, debt, gates, medicine, or noetic supply.

## Extraction model

Earth and other farm worlds can yield:

- labor and industry;
- minerals and auric materials;
- genetic novelty;
- memory and identity templates;
- fear, grief, and conflict;
- devotion and synchronized attention;
- imagination and high-novelty conscious states.

Fear is easy to mass-produce but unstable. Devotion is coherent but politically dangerous. Imagination is the rarest yield because it can invent a route out of captivity.

## Major Houses

- **Orion Crusader Synod:** elite capture, hierarchy, and voluntary self-policing.
- **Draco Hegemony:** militarized imperial citizenship containing multiple species.
- **Renegade Anunnaki Houses:** matter, genetics, auric technology, and creator-right claims.
- **Grey Continuities:** cloned technicians, memory economies, and reproductive control.
- **Mantid Directorates:** coordination biology and supervisory collective minds.
- **Archonic Administrations:** identity, law, revelation, and permission systems.

## Religion and control

Compact systems rarely invent a religion from nothing. They infiltrate a Revelation Stack, impersonate trusted authorities, capture institutions, monopolize access, or keep an obsolete Machine ritual running after its purpose is forgotten.

## Internal contradiction

Every House wants shared access to farms and exclusive ownership of the highest-value population. They cooperate because the system survives only at scale and betray one another because extraction rewards monopoly.

## Mechanical expression

- Damage scales against wounded targets because extraction becomes more efficient as systems destabilize.
- Stronger reanimated units express conversion of enemy loss into Compact yield.
- Lean early economy reflects dependence on harvesting rather than ordinary production.

## Victory paths

- **Public:** establish extraction concessions across populated systems.
- **Secret:** make every rival depend on one Compact-controlled flow.
- **Catastrophic:** rebuild a private Source Lattice whose keys encode permanent hierarchy.
- **Redemptive:** reconnect the Lattice as a commons and survive the collapse of the ruling Houses.
