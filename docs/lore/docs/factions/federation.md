# Federation of Light

## The Luminous Accord / Ashtar Command

**Leader:** **Ashtar, Supreme Commander and First Speaker**
**Doctrine:** *Agree to the Light of Unified Consciousness.*
**Conquest verb:** **Accord**
**Primary resources:** Consent Signatures and Coherence
**Game ID:** `light`

The Federation is a multi-species alliance built around one constitutional boundary: consciousness may unite only by consent.

## Ashtar

“Ashtar” is simultaneously:

- the founding commander;
- a constitutional office;
- an authenticated continuity identity;
- a memory lineage transferred through approved succession;
- an emergency command key;
- a set of physical, synthetic, and noetic avatars.

Several claimants can possess valid fragments without possessing the full office. This makes authentication a permanent political crisis rather than a simple impostor plot.

## Mission

Reconnect civilizations to a shared Source Lattice, dismantle farm systems, prevent extinction-scale conquest, and help worlds develop contact protocols that cannot be captured by one elite.

A world is conquered only when a valid consent signature joins it to the Accord.

## Institutions

- **Ashtar Command:** defense, liberation, and emergency response.
- **Assembly of Polities:** representation for conventional member civilizations.
- **Choir of Social Memories:** representation for collective and distributed minds.
- **Free-Will Jurists:** audit consent, coercion, emergency powers, and quarantine.
- **Contact Directorate:** manages first contact and authentication.
- **Liberation Fleet:** breaks extraction systems without automatically annexing the liberated world.

## Internal contradiction

The Federation believes manipulated civilizations may need protection before they can choose freely. The better it becomes at protection, the harder it is to demonstrate that later agreement was not manufactured by dependence.

“Agree” is constitutional, but the Federation can create conditions in which disagreement appears suicidal.

## Mechanical expression

- Defensive resilience and life preservation.
- Resistance suppression represents consensus targeting that holds protections open.
- Jam and sabotage immunity represent authenticated, distributed control.
- Higher starting quality reflects inherited collective memory rather than raw aggression.

## Victory paths

- **Public:** build a galaxy-wide network of freely ratified Accord worlds.
- **Secret:** make consciousness farms economically unnecessary by restoring shared coherence.
- **Catastrophic:** impose universal protective quarantine.
- **Redemptive:** dismantle emergency powers after victory and permit member worlds to leave.
