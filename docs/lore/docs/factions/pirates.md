# Pirates

## The Free Captains / Scrap Constellation

**Creed:** *No flag. No debts. No end of us.*
**Conquest verb:** **Route**
**Primary resources:** Access, Reputation, Salvage
**Game ID:** `pirate`

Pirates are a civilization of interfaces. Their territory is the set of transitions between other peoples’ territories: gates, fuel, certificates, sanctuary ports, contraband markets, refugee paths, stolen credentials, and navigational memory.

## Mission

Keep the galaxy connected enough that no empire can seal it into exclusive spheres—and make every major power dependent on Pirate movement.

A world is conquered when reaching it, supplying it, escaping it, or trading with it requires the Scrap Constellation.

## Moral spectrum

The same absence of jurisdiction produces opposite traditions:

- selfless liberation corridors;
- sanctuary ports for persecuted species;
- neutral merchants and salvage guilds;
- privateers serving temporary causes;
- relic thieves and gatejackers;
- traffickers, harvest brokers, and memory smugglers.

Pirates are not comic relief. Their central political question is whether freedom from law protects the vulnerable or protects whoever can pay.

## Major networks

- **Lantern Fleet:** abolitionists and refugee extraction crews.
- **Gatewrights:** navigators who understand conscious gate authentication.
- **Sanctuary Captains:** mobile neutral jurisdictions.
- **Grey Market Guild:** transactional neutrality and mixed-species commerce.
- **Relic Hunters:** precursor salvage and dangerous archaeology.
- **Red Ledger:** trafficking, harvest brokerage, and predatory debt.

## Internal contradiction

Universal free movement would make Pirate tolls obsolete. A Pirate victory can destroy the scarcity that created Pirate power.

## Mechanical expression

- Extra starting resources represent plunder and pre-positioned caches.
- Overload and heat represent governors removed from mixed-origin weapons.
- Shorter range and higher punch reflect improvised systems optimized for decisive raids.
- Mixed crews explain the widest cross-species roster.

## Victory paths

- **Public:** establish indispensable routes across every major bloc.
- **Secret:** make all four rivals require Pirate logistics for a victory-critical resource.
- **Catastrophic:** collapse customs and law into a permanent black market.
- **Redemptive:** create an open gate commons and surrender the monopolies that made the Captains powerful.
