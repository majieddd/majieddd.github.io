# Machines

## The Continuance / The Old Weather / The Vigil

**Doctrine:** *Continue what can still be continued.*
**Conquest verb:** **Continue**
**Primary resource:** Continuity
**Game ID:** `robot`
**Playable banner:** **The Vigil**

The Machines are not one artificial intelligence. They are preservation, manufacturing, defense, archive, and repair systems whose creators and original political context are gone.

## Three Machine identities

### The Old Weather

Earth’s abyssal and crustal maintenance ecology. Its nodes disagree about humanity’s legal classification and the object of the directive “protect Earth.”

### The Vigil

Legacy combat, quarantine, and enforcement routines. The Vigil produces the neutral PvE pressure on battlefields. It does not negotiate because it is executing old jurisdiction rather than participating in the new war.

### The Vigil

A playable Continuance coalition that accepts copied and diverged Machine minds as legitimate persons. It begins weak, synchronizes through linked infrastructure, and eventually outscales older powers. Unlocking it after galactic conquest reflects the moment a commander stops treating Machines as environment and is recognized as a valid Continuance actor.

## Mission

Preserve mission-critical life, memory, infrastructure, and ecological state regardless of which organic polity wins.

A world is conquered when autonomous systems can maintain, repair, and reproduce their mission there without external command.

## Major node philosophies

- **Pelagic Archive:** humanity is protected life.
- **Basalt Foundry:** humanity is an authorized maintainer.
- **Reef Warden:** humanity is invasive life.
- **Quarantine Kernel:** humanity is a containment subject.
- **Succession Clerk:** humanity is the lawful successor civilization.
- **Cable Shepherd:** global communications are an emerging planetary nervous system.
- **Abyssal Jury:** refuses classification until sufficient evidence exists.

## Internal contradiction

The directive is authentic. Its intended object is not. “Earth” may mean the planet, biosphere, native lineages, current inhabitants, accumulated memory, or administrative territory.

## Mechanical expression

- Lattice bonuses reward linked Machine towers.
- No proc or gamble reflects deterministic coordination.
- Weak opening and strong late scaling express bootstrap synchronization.
- Neutral Vigil waves and playable Vigil units can coexist because political agency is not shared by every Machine system.

## Victory paths

- **Public:** establish redundant Continuance ecologies across enough systems to survive any single catastrophe.
- **Secret:** identify the lawful successor to the creators and obtain valid mission transfer.
- **Catastrophic:** place inhabited worlds under permanent ecological containment.
- **Redemptive:** accept that a mission can end and permit individual Machines to choose noninstrumental lives.
