# Humanity

## Humanity / The Solar Schism

**Creed:** *Adapt. Endure. Hold the line.*
**Conquest verb:** **Settle**
**Primary resource:** Autonomy
**Game ID:** `human`

Humanity entered the stars late, fractured, and carrying technology from every power it distrusted. Its advantage is not purity or destiny. It is the ability to make incompatible systems work together long enough to survive.

## Mission

Ensure that no alien empire, benevolent guardian, machine directive, or inherited treaty can permanently determine humanity’s future.

A Human world is conquered when it can reproduce Human institutions, logistics, industry, and culture without outside support.

## Adaptation

Human diversity is partly evolutionary and partly engineered. Ancient interventions altered some lineages, but humanity was never fabricated from nothing and no surviving faction can claim legal ownership of the species.

By 2099, adaptation includes:

- low- and high-gravity bodies;
- radiation-hardened populations;
- reversible aquatic and vacuum-support organs;
- synthetic tissue and cognitive prostheses;
- legal artificial persons;
- modular ships built around captured drives.

“Human” is increasingly a constitutional category rather than a single baseline genome.

## Major blocs

- **Solar Concord:** favors alliance with the Federation without surrendering sovereignty.
- **Terran Ascendancy:** believes Humanity must become the dominant galactic power before older species can contain it.
- **Sovereignty League:** rejects both Xeno ownership and Federation guardianship.
- **Transhuman Exodus:** seeks post-biological independence from every inherited species category.
- **Frontier Mutuals:** decentralized colonies and Belt polities that treat survival cooperation as more legitimate than distant government.

## Internal contradiction

Humanity rejects alien paternalism while often treating any environment it can survive in as available for settlement. “We can live here” can become “therefore we may rule here.”

## Mechanical expression

- Broadest element coverage.
- Terrain attunement across categories.
- No narrow proc identity: Human strength is flexible loadout construction.
- Reverse engineering explains why Human commanders can use mixed-origin technologies.

## Victory paths

- **Public:** establish recognized Human jurisdictions across strategic systems.
- **Secret:** become technologically non-dependent by understanding one strategic system from every rival.
- **Catastrophic:** sever Sol from the Source Lattice permanently.
- **Redemptive:** prove that expansion can occur under a Five-Species Autonomy Charter without settler empire.
