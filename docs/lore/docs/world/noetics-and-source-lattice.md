# Noetics and the Source Lattice

## Noetic coherence

Conscious systems generate persistent patterned information: identity continuity, attention, memory, expectation, emotional synchronization, and models of possible futures. In 2099 this is measurable as **noetic coherence**.

Noetic coherence is not unlimited energy and does not suspend thermodynamics. It becomes technologically useful when coupled to systems that need:

- identity authentication;
- destination selection;
- distributed memory;
- probabilistic stabilization;
- coordination across long delays;
- persistence of collective minds.

## Source Lattice functions

The ancient Source Lattice combines four infrastructures:

1. **Transit:** stabilized interstellar gate endpoints.
2. **Identity:** authentication across bodies, copies, and collective minds.
3. **Memory:** redundancy and synchronization for civilizations whose persons span many substrates.
4. **Coherence commons:** shared stabilization that reduces the need to extract from captive populations.

## The Severance

The Severed Houses lost Lattice access. Competing explanations remain canonically viable:

- war damage;
- expulsion after abuse;
- privatization by the Houses themselves;
- emancipation by the Lattice’s original administrators;
- a safety response to something spreading through connected minds.

## Farm-world economics

Farm systems do not merely “eat fear.” They collect several different outputs:

| Yield | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Fear | High amplitude | Noisy, unstable, damages the population |
| Grief | Persistent | Slow and politically volatile |
| Devotion | Coherent | Can produce independent gods, movements, or revolts |
| Desire | Renewable | Difficult to standardize across species |
| Memory | Information-dense | Removing it erodes labor and identity |
| Imagination | Extremely novel | Generates escape strategies and unplanned futures |
| Shared purpose | Most stable | Cannot be reliably coerced without degrading it |

## Gold and auric lattices

Gold matters because engineered auric structures are chemically stable, highly controllable, and effective in noetic containment, sensing, and gate interfaces. Different Anunnaki stories preserve displaced fragments of atmospheric, computational, and field-engineering use. Gold is strategic material—not a magical substance and not the only resource the Houses seek.

## Consent Signatures

A valid consent signature records:

- the consenting entities;
- the scope and duration of agreement;
- what information they possessed;
- coercive conditions;
- delegation rules;
- authentication lineage.

Federation law treats consent as infrastructure. Xeno systems treat it as an attack surface. Humans treat it as a sovereignty problem. Pirates trade in it. Machines may recognize signatures older than the species presenting them.
