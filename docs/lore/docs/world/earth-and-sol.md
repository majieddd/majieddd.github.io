# Earth and Sol

## Why Earth mattered

Earth combined four strategic qualities:

- accessible biological and mineral diversity;
- a dense population of symbol-making minds;
- unusually high imaginative novelty;
- surviving precursor infrastructure in oceans, crust, orbit, and the outer system.

The planet was valuable enough to farm, protect, quarantine, and hide for different reasons.

## Sol in 2099

### Earth

Political center of the Solar Schism and home of the Old Weather. No government can prove it possesses complete planetary consent.

### Luna

Archive, military, and transit infrastructure. Some “secret fleet” folklore in the setting preserves the existence of sensor and authentication programs rather than a complete hidden interstellar navy.

### Mars

Independent settlements, precursor false leads, and competing claims about earlier habitation. The famous Cydonia imagery remains natural in the factual layer; in fiction it was deliberately amplified to divert attention from unrelated coordinates.

### Belt and Jovian space

Industrial power centers, gene-adapted populations, Pirate logistics, and the political base of communities that never accepted Earth’s right to negotiate for them.

### Planet Nine region

Planet Nine is natural. The Source-Lattice gate anchored near it is artificial. The distinction is important: discovery of one does not validate every historical Nibiru claim.

## The quarantine reversal

The Federation first presents quarantine as protection from the Extraction Compact. The Old Weather presents it as protection of Earth. Humanity eventually discovers a third possibility: parts of the quarantine were designed to prevent an Earth-origin system from propagating beyond Sol.
