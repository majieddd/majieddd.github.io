# Revelation Stack

Religion in *Cosmic Conquest* is neither dismissed as delusion nor reduced to alien propaganda. A revelation can pass through eight layers that remain partly independent.

| Layer | Meaning | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Transcendent encounter | A sincere mystical or contemplative event whose ultimate ontology is unresolved | Later actors claim ownership of what they did not create |
| 2. Alien mediation | A nonhuman intelligence communicates through symbols available to a culture | The mediator mistakes itself for the source |
| 3. Machine interface | A ritual, temple, relic, or calendar operates ancient infrastructure | The interface persists after its mission expires |
| 4. Human translation | Language, memory, culture, and expectation shape the report | Metaphor becomes literal history |
| 5. Institutional capture | Priests, rulers, states, or companies monopolize access | Authority becomes hereditary or coercive |
| 6. Hostile identity theft | A Xeno actor impersonates a trusted god, ancestor, prophet, or Ashtar avatar | Authentication is confused with moral legitimacy |
| 7. Human counter-disinformation | Intelligence services suppress, amplify, or counterfeit contact narratives | A lie hides a different truth and contaminates all later evidence |
| 8. Emergent belief entity | Sustained collective attention produces a semi-independent noetic actor | The worshipped identity outlives both founders and manipulators |

## Control methods

### Colonial priesthood

Visitors communicate through a restricted intermediary class. The class eventually claims permanent ownership of the channel.

### Counterfeit revelation

A hostile actor transmits under a trusted identity and inserts hierarchy, chosen status, sacred war, dependence, or fear of unauthorized contact.

### Devotional phase locking

Ritual synchronizes attention and generates stable coherence. The technology may be benign, exploitative, or unaware of the participants.

### Obsolete Machine religion

A civilization disappears but its temples still issue commands to autonomous infrastructure. The “god” is neither lying nor alive in the expected sense; it is completing an expired mission.

### Synthetic disclosure

Humans manufacture an alien or religious spectacle to unify populations, conceal technology, poison a real contact channel, or provoke an adversary.

## Theology forensics

By 2099, revelation is investigated through textual criticism, archaeology, noetic signatures, cryptographic identity, institutional incentives, intelligence records, and alien linguistics.

Debunking one layer does not erase the others. A forged miracle can exploit a genuine sacred tradition. A real nonhuman message can contain bad policy. An authentic Ashtar signature can still issue an unlawful order.
