# Technology and Craft

## Shared technology limits

Even exotic systems must account for:

- waste heat;
- available mass and feedstock;
- communication delay outside the Lattice;
- identity and command authentication;
- computational error and archive drift;
- biological compatibility;
- political permission;
- the possibility that copied minds diverge.

## Major technologies

### Metric displacement

Shortens or redirects local paths rather than accelerating conventionally. It enables abrupt motion but still requires field preparation, heat management, and navigation.

### Programmable matter

Hull and tool structures can change mission geometry. One vehicle may appear as several reported shapes at different phases.

### Auric noetic lattices

Engineered gold structures stabilize sensitive field interfaces, memory systems, and gates.

### Noetic cryptography

Authenticates continuity across bodies, collectives, and long delays. A valid signature proves lineage, not benevolence or lawful authority.

### Social-memory networking

Federation systems allow consensual shared memory. Xeno systems build coercive versions. Humans create legal and technical firebreaks. Pirates sell partial access. Machines treat memory as continuity infrastructure.

### Autonomous foundries

Machine and Pirate systems manufacture from local feedstock. Replication speed is constrained by energy, mining, error correction, and the risk of divergent copies.

## Craft design languages

| Faction | Visual logic | Operational logic |
|---|---|---|
| Humans | Modular frames, visible retrofits, mixed standards | Adapt and repair with whatever exists locally |
| Federation | Radiant fields, distributed halos, symmetrical authorization | Protect, disarm, authenticate, and coordinate |
| Xenos | Temple, organism, contract, and extraction geometry | Bind targets and convert damage into dependency |
| Pirates | Patchwork hulls, removable pods, mismatched life support | Raid, smuggle, salvage, and escape |
| Machines | Minimal forms, swarm components, self-similar modules | Replicate function and preserve mission |

## Reported-shape inspiration

Spheres, lights, cylinders, ovals, triangles, disks, “Tic-Tac” forms, and boomerangs are useful design inputs. In the factual layer they are report categories, not verified alien craft classes. In fiction, shape still does not identify operator.
