# Moon, Mars, and Secret Space Mythology

## The source families

Moon and Mars conspiracy lore merges several unrelated traditions:

- Apollo-denial claims despite surviving hardware, imagery, samples, tracking, and laser-ranging evidence.
- Lunar-anomaly and artificial-Moon theories.
- Black Knight and ancient-satellite folklore.
- Phobos-as-artifact speculation.
- Cydonia’s low-resolution “Face” and later natural-landform imaging.
- Area 51 and genuine black aerospace.
- Roswell retrieval mythology and S-4 claims.
- *Alternative 3*, a 1977 mockumentary about elite evacuation to Mars.
- Solar Warden, twenty-and-back, and breakaway-civilization narratives.

The game keeps each genealogy visible.

## Factual anchors

Apollo missions occurred. Area 51 tested advanced reconnaissance aircraft. Cydonia’s face-like image is explained by terrain, resolution, and illumination. Phobos is a natural irregular moon under ongoing study. Roswell has an official classified-balloon explanation. None of these facts prevents later fictional infrastructure from being built on the Moon, Mars, or Phobos; it prevents the fiction from claiming the older myths were therefore historically correct.

## The secret-space credibility kernel

Classified aerospace can create real unusual sightings, worker secrecy, cover stories, restricted airspace, compartmented contractors, missing public records, and technology gaps. It cannot by itself sustain an invisible interplanetary civilization without launches, logistics, power, life support, supply chains, casualties, communications, and succession.

## Proposal-Reality Gap

Every secret-space claim must be classified as one of:

1. Idea or scenario.
2. Briefing or concept art.
3. Proposed program.
4. Approved compartment.
5. Funded and staffed operation.
6. Operation with hardware.
7. Operation with offworld presence.
8. Demonstrated strategic result.

A dramatic name cannot skip the ladder.

## 2099 synthesis

- **Apollo Omission:** Apollo was real; a later unrelated relay was redacted and contaminated trust in the entire record.
- **Cydonia Redirect:** the Face is natural and deliberately exploited as an attention sink.
- **S-4 Compartment:** a real future laboratory reuses a legendary label to poison leaks.
- **Alternative Three:** an actual evacuation fleet adopts the mockumentary’s name, turning disbelief into security.
- **Solar Warden:** first a sensor-fusion network, later a small real patrol service whose name is mistaken for proof of the older fleet legend.
- **Phobos Installation:** the moon is natural; the installation is younger than the moon and therefore does not require an artificial satellite theory.

## Campaign stakes

The core conflict is not whether hidden space programs are exciting. It is who was excluded from the first offworld institutions, who owns the infrastructure built under secrecy, and whether a breakaway civilization can claim independence from the people who unknowingly financed it.
