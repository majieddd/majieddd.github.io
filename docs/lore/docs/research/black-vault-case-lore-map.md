# Black Vault and UAP Case Lore Map

The Black Vault is used here as a FOIA discovery and preservation layer. When the underlying NARA, DoD, AARO, CIA, Navy, Air Force, or other agency record is available, that primary record outranks commentary around it.

**Rule:** an authentic document proves that a report, investigation, classification rule, or administrative action existed. It does not automatically prove the reported object's origin.

| Archive or case | Evidence | What the record supports | What it does not support | 2099 lore use |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| **Project Sign, Grudge, Blue Book** | B | Long-running official investigation and a residual unidentified category. | That every unidentified case was extraterrestrial. | **Hynek Residual Index:** Vanta's archive separates unresolved identity from anomalous behavior. |
| **Kenneth Arnold, 1947** | B/C | A foundational sighting report whose public vocabulary changed during retelling. | A universal disk-craft category. | **Arnold Lexicon Problem:** the name attached to an anomaly can outlive the shape actually reported. |
| **Thomas Mantell, 1948** | B | A real fatal pursuit associated with the early UFO era and later competing explanations. | That an alien craft intentionally killed him. | **Mantell Rule:** no commander pursues an anomaly beyond sensor support and vehicle limits. |
| **Chiles-Whitted, 1948** | B | A documented aircrew report with a vehicle-like description. | A verified alien fuselage or propulsion system. | Early template for Human courier-craft rumors; later analysts preserve witness language separately from illustrations. |
| **AEC Station 47, 1950** | B | A report existed in an Air Force historical record, while a corresponding Blue Book file is not readily found. | Deliberate destruction or alien involvement. | **Missing-chain mission:** a real administrative gap becomes an Archive War objective without predetermining the answer. |
| **Tehran, September 19, 1976** | B | Military witnesses and official reporting described an unidentified object and instrument or weapons effects. | A demonstrated extraterrestrial jammer. | **Jafari Chain:** future doctrine requires witness, platform, sensor, and command records to remain independently addressable. |
| **Nimitz/Tic Tac, 2004** | B | A major modern military case with eyewitness and sensor narratives of different types. | One perfectly complete public data package or established origin. | The game tutorial for refusing to compress heterogeneous evidence into one confidence score. |
| **GoFast, 2015** | A/B | Authentic Navy footage; AARO analysis found no evidence of anomalous speed. | That an unidentified object must be exotic. | A sensor-geometry training scenario in which restraint earns the higher score. |
| **Aguadilla, 2013** | A/B | Authentic infrared imagery; AARO found no transmedium behavior and offered a conventional interpretation. | A verified craft entering the ocean. | Prevents the Old Weather from becoming a universal answer for every apparent USO. |
| **USS Omaha records gap** | B, administrative | FOIA responses and available releases show missing or unavailable logs and recordings for portions of the claimed timeframe. | That the absence proves a cover-up or alien origin. | A contested archive node where every faction benefits from a different explanation of missing evidence. |
| **UAP Security Classification Guide** | A/B | A real classification framework can restrict details about sources, methods, capabilities, and UAP evaluation. | That classified material necessarily contains extraterrestrial proof. | Human and Pirate factions fight over how classification protects sensors while destroying public trust. |
| **AARO satellite-flare and case resolutions** | A | Many reports can result from balloons, satellites, UAS, aircraft, birds, sensor artifacts, or geometry. | That all cases are thereby resolved or that unresolved means alien. | **Two-Key Rule:** every mystery keeps a conventional and speculative branch until evidence genuinely closes one. |
| **NARA Record Group 615** | A | A statutory UAP records collection is being assembled and released on a rolling basis. | That the collection validates every claim inside transferred records. | The institutional ancestor of Humanity's Open Archive and its machine-readable provenance rights. |
| **The Black Vault / John Greenewald archive** | A as publication history | A privately maintained FOIA archive has preserved and organized millions of pages since the 1990s. | Official endorsement of any interpretation. | **Greenewald Standard:** preserve the request, response, denial, appeal, release, metadata, and missing attachments as separate evidence objects. |

## Game-writing rule

Real names belong in historical files, protocols, memorials, archive quests, and scholarly disputes. Living or historical individuals should not be rewritten as secret aliens, conspirators, or faction agents without reliable source support. Fiction may name a **rule, index, or archival method** after their documented contribution while marking the future adaptation as F.
