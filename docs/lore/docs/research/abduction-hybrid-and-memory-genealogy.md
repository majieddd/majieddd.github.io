# Abduction, Hybrid, and Memory Genealogy

## Source sequence

The modern abduction complex developed across several layers: the Hill encounter and hypnosis; later missing-time narratives; medical and reproductive themes; Grey and Zeta iconography; Budd Hopkins and David Jacobs-style hybrid-program interpretations; Whitley Strieber’s *Communion* and its influential visitor image; sleep-paralysis and memory-reconstruction research; and later future-Human explanations.

These are not interchangeable evidence categories. A reported experience may involve an external event, sleep paralysis, medical trauma, suggestion, dissociation, dream material, deliberate staging, noetic contact, or several layers at once. Hypnosis can increase confidence without guaranteeing accuracy. A familiar image can alter subsequent memory even when the witness is sincere.

## Four separate questions

1. **Did the person experience something unusual?**
2. **What was present externally?**
3. **How was the memory formed, retrieved, and changed?**
4. **Who benefits from the interpretation?**

The setting never answers question two merely because question one is yes.

## Source Exposure Graph

Every witness record stores exposure to prior books, films, interviews, group discussion, investigator prompts, online communities, official briefings, altered memories, and synthetic media. Similarity after exposure has a different evidentiary meaning from similarity in sealed first accounts.

## Hybrid programs in canon

There is no one universal hybrid program. Several distinct systems exist:

- Xeno client-body engineering and reproductive dependency.
- Federation medical adaptation performed under disputed emergency consent.
- Human gene-clade settlement programs.
- Pirate identity and body markets.
- Machine reconstruction of persons from partial biological and memory archives.
- Future-Human attempts to preserve descendant probabilities.

“Hybrid” therefore describes a relationship among ancestry, body, memory, and law—not a moral category.

## Witness Interview Integrity Protocol

- Record individual first accounts before group discussion.
- Preserve exact questions and interviewer behavior.
- Timestamp drawings and confidence changes.
- Separate free recall from prompted recall.
- Record prior cultural exposure.
- Preserve model version and prompt history for AI interviewers.
- Never convert witness distress or sincerity into automatic external attribution.
- Never treat uncertain bodies or reproductive material as ownerless evidence.

## Gameplay use

Abduction missions should reward careful interview order, medical ethics, source separation, and relationship-building. The strongest reveal is often not “the witness lied” or “the aliens were real,” but that several actors manipulated different layers of one genuine experience.
