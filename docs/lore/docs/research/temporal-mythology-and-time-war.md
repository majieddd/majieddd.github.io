# Temporal Mythology and the Time War

## Historical source lanes

Time-travel conspiracy lore is not one tradition. The **Philadelphia Experiment** entered public mythology through 1950s Allende correspondence about a supposed 1943 naval experiment. **Montauk** literature later fused military mind control, psychic work, portals, missing children, and the Philadelphia story. **Project Pegasus** added testimony about secret government time travel. The **Chronovisor** supplied a religious retrocognition device. The **Mandela Effect** and simulation discourse made timeline alteration participatory and digital. *Inside Job* supplies a fictional reality-editing machine whose most useful lesson is that personal motives and maintenance failures matter more than abstract omnipotence.

## Temporal Technology Ladder

| Level | Capability | Information cost | Canon risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Retrocognition**: reconstructing a probable past from surviving state | Cannot recover erased context perfectly | Mistaken certainty and synthetic evidence |
| 2 | **Information backflow**: sending a small pattern backward | Creates self-authenticating rumors and impossible provenance | Prophecy, forged treaties, archive loops |
| 3 | **Branch selection**: preserving one possible outcome while alternatives remain recorded | Requires immense noetic coherence and destroys political legitimacy | Project Reboot, Mandela-like residue, erased lives |
| 4 | **Physical chronology transit**: moving a body or durable object across time | Rare, unstable, and never a general-purpose weapon | Descendant intervention and causal jurisdiction |

## Canon architecture

The **Chronology Court** does not decide which timeline is “real.” It decides which records, persons, contracts, debts, and crimes remain legally actionable after a branch event. **Temporal Jurisdiction** prevents future descendants from treating present Humanity as property. The **Source Exposure Graph** records whether an impossible memory came from experience, prior media, backflow, simulation, or another copied mind.

## Faction motives

- **Humans:** use backflow to prevent external ownership, then fracture over whether future survival justifies present manipulation.
- **Federation:** restrict temporal contact because manufactured consent can originate in the future.
- **Xenos:** create closed causal contracts whose subjects appear to have always agreed.
- **Pirates:** smuggle branch memories, extinct identities, and route keys that exist in only one history.
- **Machines:** preserve every branch as an archive and may value deleted simulations as living populations.

## Non-negotiable rule

A corrupted photograph, altered memory, or historical inconsistency is not automatically a timeline event. Conventional editing, expectation, metadata loss, memory reconstruction, and disinformation remain live explanations until the story deliberately resolves them.
