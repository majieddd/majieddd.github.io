# Research Backlog

These questions can improve the universe without expanding core canon prematurely.

## Archive and sensor fidelity - next pass

- Compare each Black Vault processed file in Releases 1-5 against its government parent and record hash, filename, MIME type, OCR engine, transcript source, and transformation history.
- Build a catalog-first inventory of the NSA, State, and FBI RG 615 series and note which items are digitally available, metadata-only, restricted, duplicated elsewhere, or not yet bulk-exported.
- Test a small set of historic videos against the Measurement Lineage schema: native or earliest surviving file, geometry, platform motion, calibration, transformations, and reproducible derived quantities.
- Trace the public evolution of Sandia's WATCHER and event-based sensing work without equating counter-UAS demonstrations with exotic-object detection.
- Inventory AARO material-analysis papers by specimen provenance, custody gap, test method, result, and residual question; negative findings remain first-class results.
- Design a Source-Subject Split field for every withheld item: object, sensor, platform, location, analytic method, foreign partner, witness, or unknown basis.
- Add explicit uncertainty-state validation to every future survey, transcript, witness form, and AI extraction: no answer may be silently converted to “no.”
