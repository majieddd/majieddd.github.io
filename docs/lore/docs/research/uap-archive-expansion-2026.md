# UAP Archive Expansion - Black Vault, NARA, and AARO (2026)

This addendum records material that became available or materially clearer after the initial 0.3.0 archive pass and the first 0.3.1 expansion. It adds fidelity to the **Archive War**, the Human Open Archive, Federation disclosure policy, Pirate information markets, Compact provenance poisoning, and Machine record law. It does not change the five-faction canon or convert any unresolved report into extraterrestrial evidence.

## FY2025 AARO details that matter for game logic

The report supports several useful distinctions:

1. **Report count is not incident count.** AARO merges reports when multiple submissions correspond to one event.
2. **Location density reflects sensor and reporting density.** More reports near U.S. military operations do not automatically mean more anomalous activity there.
3. **Narrative claims and technical validation are different evidence objects.** Some reports near critical infrastructure described apparently advanced behavior but lacked accompanying technical data.
4. **Materials procedures can exist before materials do.** AARO says no evidence indicates a U.S. public or private entity captured or exploited UAP-derived material, while also developing a process should material ever be received.
5. **Analysis changes over time.** New modeling methods can resolve old cases, which means an archive is an active analytical system rather than a tomb.
6. **The active archive is not a “mystery confirmed” list.** It is a queue of insufficiently supported cases retained for pattern analysis and possible reopening.

These distinctions should become explicit fields in Archive War content:

```text
record_status:
  raw_report | correlated_incident | resolved | technical_review |
  active_archive | withheld | no_records | custody_gap | appealed

attribution_status:
  identified | probable | unresolved_identity | unresolved_behavior |
  insufficient_data | disputed | not_assessed

provenance_quality:
  original | agency_copy | derivative_release | OCR_text |
  transcript | commentary | reconstruction

media_integrity:
  native_sensor | documented_export | secondary_display_capture |
  altered_copy | artistic_reconstruction | narrative_only

program_maturity:
  allegation | suggestion | briefing | proposed_compartment |
  approved_program | funded_operation | material_received | result_produced
```

## New lore concepts accepted

### Sensor Custody Doctrine

The 2099 Open Archive records observations before conclusions. Every derived velocity, size, acceleration, or trajectory remains linked to the sensor geometry, platform motion, calibration, metadata, assumptions, processing steps, and confidence interval that produced it. An adversary can therefore compromise an analysis without forging the original image.

### Native Master and Fork Law

The first immutable hash defines the **native master**. Every crop, stabilized clip, denoised image, contrast adjustment, transcript, OCR layer, reconstruction, and briefing slide is a fork with its own author and transformation history. Federation memory courts and Machine Mission Courts disagree over whether an authenticated fork can ever acquire equal legal status to its source.

### The Watcher Mesh

Human and Machine persistent-monitoring systems inherit event-based sensing rather than a magical all-seeing radar. They detect motion and periodic signatures efficiently, but identity still depends on baseline geometry, focus, calibration, correlation, and continuing custody. The network's most dangerous failure is not missing an object; it is assigning a political identity before the track has earned one.

### Refusal Is Data

By 2099, investigators distinguish `no`, `unknown`, `not remembered`, `not observed`, `withheld`, `not applicable`, and `refuse to answer`. Compact systems deliberately collapse those states into whatever answer makes a dependency contract appear complete.

### Institutional Shadow Map

The same subject can generate records in aviation safety, intelligence, military operations, scientific research, public diplomacy, news production, and records administration. These are not duplicates to collapse. They are institutional views with different purposes, vocabularies, custody rules, and blind spots.

### Known-Fake Control Set

Fabricated, staged, optically altered, and misidentified examples are preserved rather than discarded. Their value is calibration: a classifier, analyst, or noetic authentication system that cannot recognize known deceptions is not ready to judge unknown material. A control image never proves that an unrelated case is false; it tests whether the method can detect the kinds of error it claims to detect.

### The Open Archive is a versioned graph

Humanity's Open Archive does not store “the truth” as one file. It stores relationships among:

- original reports;
- incident mergers;
- sensor files;
- witness statements;
- agency copies;
- classification decisions;
- redactions;
- appeals;
- derivative OCR;
- transcripts;
- later analytical resolutions;
- missing or destroyed custodial records.

A record can be authentic while a claim inside it is mistaken. A transcript can be accurate while the speaker is wrong. A classified photograph can exist while its content remains unknowable. A “no records” response can describe a poor search, lawful destruction, wrong custodian, or true absence.

### The Federation's First Contact Communications Protocol

The NASA planning material becomes a factual institutional ancestor, not proof of contact. In fiction, the Federation judges a civilization partly by whether it can announce a discovery without:

- allowing one government to monopolize it;
- declaring speculation as confirmation;
- erasing minority religious interpretations;
- triggering avoidable panic;
- hiding uncertainty behind authority;
- letting a counterfeit transmission become the first global narrative.

Ashtar's first public transmission is therefore preceded by a conflict over **who is permitted to authenticate the speaker**, not simply whether the message can be heard.

### Negative-space records

Some assets are known only through denial letters, classification guides, transfer logs, indexes, attachment lists, or references in another office's file. They are not blank checks for extraordinary lore. They create constrained mission objects:

- prove that a record existed;
- locate the lawful custodian;
- determine why it was withheld;
- preserve its hash and chain of custody;
- distinguish source protection from content sensitivity;
- avoid inventing the unseen contents.

### The Mirror Archive Method

When a primary mailbox or file series is missing, investigators seek copies from every participant and system that could have retained it. This directly supports gameplay in which one faction destroys an archive but cannot erase:

- recipient copies;
- transit logs;
- calendar invitations;
- attachment hashes;
- backup indexes;
- legal-review records;
- downstream quotation;
- Machine memory witnesses.

### Recovery teams do not imply alien wreckage

Project Moon Dust and related debris-analysis history provide the correct procedural base for Human recovery units: field security, foreign-materiel exploitation, hazardous-material control, technical identification, diplomacy, and custody. The fictional leap is that a later team eventually receives genuinely nonhuman material. The historical program is not retroactively made extraterrestrial.

### Media Integrity Ladder

A media object is not defined only by what it depicts. The archive records which instrument generated it, whether the public file is a native export, whether another camera recorded a display, what alterations predated intake, and whether the image is only a reconstruction. Lower-fidelity material can preserve valuable testimony while supporting fewer kinematic or morphological claims.

### Proposal-Program-Operation Ladder

A code name can exist at several maturity levels: allegation, suggestion, briefing, proposed compartment, approved program, funded operation, material receipt, and produced result. KONA BLUE is the control case: the proposal existed, while AARO says the program was never established or supplied with material.

### Blind Metrics

An evaluation can disclose what an institution intends to measure while protecting why the measure matters, which capabilities are vulnerable, or what results were found. Classification of method cannot be used as a substitute for evidence about the evaluated phenomenon.

### Narrative Infrastructure

The Open Archive stores exact witness language, interviewer prompts, agency summaries, illustrations, media exposure, confidence, contradiction, and later retelling as linked layers. This preserves testimony without silently converting memory into sensor data.

### Null Search Paradox

Program existence, phrase occurrence, government discussion, congressional inquiry, and responsive records are separate propositions. Winning an appeal that forces a search establishes a lawful search obligation, not the truth of the underlying allegation.

## Archive-mining workflow

Do **not** download hundreds of gigabytes before reading catalog metadata.

1. Use the current RG 615 page to identify the agency series and NARA IDs.
2. Use the bulk page for packages that actually exist as ZIP/JSON exports.
3. Ingest JSON metadata first and deduplicate by NARA ID, file hash, agency case number, incident time, and source URL.
4. Keep original government files separate from Black Vault processed/OCR derivatives.
5. Record OCR quality and never quote a garbled scan without checking the image.
6. Distinguish event date, report date, transfer date, release date, publication date, and appeal date.
7. Preserve denial letters, appeal outcomes, “no records” determinations, and missing-attachment notes as first-class records.
8. Link, but do not merge, records that may describe the same event until time, location, platform, and provenance support the correlation.
9. Extract game-design tags only after the factual case summary and limitations are written.
10. Recheck current AARO and RG 615 pages before each lore release because both are rolling collections.

## Canon boundary

Nothing in this addendum establishes extraterrestrial origin, ocean bases, reverse-engineered craft, hidden treaties, or a unified secret program. The lore uses archival structure, procedural ambiguity, sensor limitations, and public-trust problems as historical scaffolding. The nonhuman history remains **F** unless a source is explicitly identified as another evidence class.
