# Chronology and Mythos Gap Audit - August 2026

## Audit result

The previous lore had strong faction motives and source discipline but a weak chronological middle. It jumped from broad ancient-source layers into the twentieth-century UFO era, and from the 2029 Apophis anchor into a sparse set of 2046-2099 events. That made individual concepts strong while leaving future writers to invent why one era produced the next.

Release 0.5.0 fixes that with:

1. a deep-time fictional reconstruction with explicit uncertainty;
2. a source-history timeline from archaeology and religious traditions through the modern UAP era;
3. a denser 2030-2099 causal divergence;
4. an explicit reason Earth becomes valuable;
5. an explicit reason the Compact needs autonomous rather than passive populations;
6. an explicit reason the Federation delays open contact;
7. an explicit reason Pirates become a polity;
8. an explicit legal cause for Machine expansion;
9. locked 2099 convergence logic.

## New source lineages added or strengthened

### M61 - Lost Age Stack: Atlantis, Younger Dryas, and Gobekli Tepe

**Source fact:** Gobekli Tepe contains monumental structures erected by hunter-gatherers between roughly 9600 and 8200 BCE. That demonstrates unexpectedly early Human social organization; it does not demonstrate an advanced vanished technological civilization.

**Controversy:** Published reviews reach sharply different conclusions about the Younger Dryas impact hypothesis. The lore therefore records the disagreement rather than using the hypothesis as fact.

**F adaptation:** Several factions weaponize the social desire for a lost golden age. The real archaeological sites remain Human; fictional hidden sites are separate discoveries.

Sources:
- https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1572
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2021.103677
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2023.104502

### M62 - Technology-Shaped Sky: airships, foo fighters, ghost rockets

**Source fact:** UFO descriptions repeatedly adopt the technological vocabulary of their era: mystery airships, phantom Zeppelins, ghost rockets, saucers, and modern drone-like reports.

**F adaptation:** The Translation Horizon is not cosmetic. Witness vocabulary is a lossy codec. An unknown object may be described through the nearest available technological category.

Source:
- https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/how-ufo-reports-change-with-technology-times-180968011/

### M63 - Premodern Contact Forms: Watchers and Utsuro-bune

**Source fact:** Enochic Watcher literature contains angelic descent and illicit/transmitted knowledge. Utsuro-bune is an Edo-period legend documented in multiple records and later compared to UFO narratives.

**F adaptation:** These are not declared alien reports. They become examples of how forbidden-knowledge and incomprehensible-contact structures recur long before the modern extraterrestrial vocabulary.

Sources:
- https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/fallen-angels-and-the-history-of-judaism-and-christianity/angelic-descent-and-apocalyptic-epistemology-the-teachings-of-enoch-and-the-fallen-angels-in-the-book-of-the-watchers/1627AF4FF02634B391079F067A8AB80C
- https://www.nippon.com/en/japan-topics/g00879/

### M64 - Engineering Scripture: Ezekiel spacecraft reinterpretations

**Source history:** Josef F. Blumrich's 1970s work is a modern engineering/ancient-astronaut reinterpretation of Ezekiel, not evidence that the biblical text is a technical manual.

**F adaptation:** Engineers in the Archive War sometimes discover that a religious description accidentally preserves geometry useful to a later machine interface while the original theological meaning remains independent.

### M65 - Vimana Mutation

**Source history:** Modern claims about detailed ancient aircraft often blur old Sanskrit aerial/vehicle imagery with the much later *Vaimanika Shastra*. A 1974 Indian Institute of Science critical study concluded the described aircraft were not aeronautically viable and traced the work to a modern authorial context.

**F adaptation:** A counterfeit ancient technical corpus can contain one authentic stolen material specification without making the corpus ancient or its aircraft workable.

Source:
- https://cgpl.iisc.ac.in/oldwebcontent/site/Portals/0/Publications/ReferedJournal/ACriticalStudyOfTheWorkVaimanikaShastra.pdf

### M66 - Planet X Convergence

**Source fact:** Modern Planet Nine is a specific unobserved astronomical hypothesis. Earlier Planet X searches, Nemesis, Tyche, Sitchin's Nibiru, and Internet doomsday stories are distinct lineages commonly collapsed online. WISE placed strong constraints on giant hidden companions while not deciding the modern Planet Nine question.

**F adaptation:** The greatest irony in the setting is that the public “Planet X” synthesis is wrong even though a real natural Planet Nine is eventually found near separate artificial infrastructure.

Sources:
- https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planet-x/
- https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-wise-survey-finds-thousands-of-new-stars-but-no-planet-x/
- https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/can-wise-find-the-hypothetical-tyche/

### M67 - Real Weird Space Programs

**Source fact:** Project Horizon studied a scientific/military lunar base; 1959 Air Force lunar research reports exist; Project West Ford attempted orbital copper-dipole communications; Starfish Prime created an artificial radiation environment that damaged satellites.

**F adaptation:** Secret-space conspiracy lore grows partly because real states genuinely explored plans that sound fictional when removed from their dates and status. The game tracks whether something was a study, approved program, deployed experiment, or operational capability.

Sources:
- https://sirismm.si.edu/EADpdfs/NASM.2020.0031.pdf
- https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-D301-PURL-gpo32511
- https://orbitaldebris.jsc.nasa.gov/Quarterly-News/pdfs/ODQNv17i4.pdf

### M68 - False-Flag Mutation: Northwoods to synthetic invasion lore

**Source fact:** The 1962 Northwoods memorandum genuinely proposed manufactured pretexts for intervention in Cuba. Proposal is not execution, and it was not an alien operation.

**F adaptation:** Later planners use Northwoods as a historical warning for why a synthetic alien attack cannot be dismissed merely because the idea is extreme. The evidence must establish an operation, not only capability or precedent.

Source:
- https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/CMC-60/joint-chiefs-pretexts-to-invade-Cuba-1962

### M69 - Rescue Prophecy and Failed Contact

**Source history:** UFO-religious movements and classic sociology of failed prophecy show that groups can reinterpret failed predictions rather than simply dissolve. Heaven's Gate is a tragic historical case and must be handled without sensationalism.

**F adaptation:** Federation evacuation orders require independently verifiable authentication and explicit anti-cult safeguards. A population's belief that a rescue is coming is not proof of Federation contact.

### M70 - Automatic-Writing Cosmic Bureaucracy

**Source history:** *Oahspe* (1882) predates the flying-saucer era and presents automatic-writing revelations involving elaborate heavenly administration.

**F adaptation:** Cosmic bureaucracy is not uniquely a modern UFO idea. This becomes a caution against treating later similarity to Federation institutions as evidence of retroactive contact.

### M71 - Human Technological Surprise

**Source fact:** The Antikythera Mechanism is an extraordinarily sophisticated ancient Greek astronomical calculator.

**F adaptation:** Archaeological surprise is deliberately used as an anti-ancient-alien control. The game awards better Archive War outcomes when the player first models the strongest Human explanation.

Source:
- https://www.nature.com/articles/nature05357

### M72 - Planetarium / Censored Sky

**Source theory:** The “planetarium” family of Fermi-paradox ideas asks whether observations could be manipulated by a sufficiently advanced civilization. It is speculative, not evidence our sky is censored.

**F adaptation:** Xeno and Machine powers can manipulate sensor access locally, but no faction can casually fake the entire observable universe. Large-scale deception has infrastructure costs and failure signatures.

### M73 - Aestivation

**Source theory:** Sandberg, Armstrong, and Cirkovic proposed that computation-maximizing advanced civilizations might wait for colder cosmic eras.

**F adaptation:** Some Continuance systems are not dead. They are dormant by strategy. Waking them can look like first contact even when they have occupied the region for eons.

Source:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03394

### M74 - Expansion Fronts / Grabby Civilizations

**Source theory:** Expansion-model approaches to the Fermi question explore how fast-growing civilizations could generate large spatial domains.

**F adaptation:** The five conquest verbs are different solutions to the same expansion problem. The galaxy can contain fronts of settlement, accession, dependency, route control, and replication without implying every civilization expands the same way.

### M75 - METI, Arecibo, and the dark-forest argument

**Source fact:** Humans deliberately transmitted the Arecibo Message in 1974. Active messaging debates therefore concern real Human behavior, even though no alien reply has been established.

**F adaptation:** The Archive War later asks whether Humanity's first “invitation” was a symbolic broadcast, Gate activation, a noetic handshake, or none of the above.

Source:
- https://www.seti.org/research/seti-101/project-phoenix/arecibo/

### M76 - Disclosure Feedback Loop

**Source fact:** The July 2023 House hearing recorded testimony from Ryan Graves, David Grusch, and David Fravor. NASA's UAP study emphasized poor metadata and data quality. AARO's historical review subsequently reported no verified evidence for hidden extraterrestrial reverse-engineering programs.

**F adaptation:** Allegation -> hearing -> legislation -> archive request -> media terminology -> later folklore becomes a self-reinforcing information loop. Official use of a term such as “non-human intelligence” in a definition or proposal is not itself confirmation that the category exists.

Sources:
- https://www.congress.gov/118/meeting/house/116282/documents/HHRG-118-GO06-Transcript-20230726.pdf
- https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report-0.pdf
- https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Vol_1_2024.pdf

### M77 - Sensor Threshold Cascade

**Source history:** In February 2023, heightened surveillance and changed radar attention after the Chinese high-altitude balloon contributed to detection of additional unknown objects.

**F adaptation:** The apparent “arrival” of a phenomenon may sometimes be a change in sensor settings. By 2099, every anomaly statistic records the detection-policy version that produced it.

## What remains intentionally missing

The audit does **not** recommend filling every year with a secret event. Empty years are useful. Canon needs causal anchors, not a conspiracy behind every historical change.

Do not add a new historical event merely to explain a game asset unless:

1. the asset's faction mechanic requires an origin;
2. the event changes a later political or technological constraint;
3. the source lineage adds a new type of evidence problem;
4. the addition can be explained in one causal chain without contradicting existing canon.


## Second-pass gaps: M78-M84

A final web/source audit found seven useful but previously under-specified lineages:

1. **M78 Brookings Artifact-Impact Mutation:** a real 1960 NASA-commissioned social-impact study later inflated into a concealment directive.
2. **M79 Hollow Moon / Spaceship Moon Mutation:** Apollo's genuine seismic ringing, the separate 1970 Vasin-Shcherbakov speculation, and later hollow-Moon claims must never be collapsed.
3. **M80 Transient Lunar Phenomena:** mixed-quality historical lunar events provide a perfect Event Segmentation test.
4. **M81 Chronovisor:** alleged retrospective viewing is useful for the fiction's reconstruction-vs-record distinction.
5. **M82 Project Looking Glass:** future-viewing/timeline-convergence lore is retained as D/E, with no public evidence of an operating temporal device.
6. **M83 Looking Glass Name Collision:** the real Strategic Air Command mission explains how an authentic codename can accidentally authenticate an unrelated legend.
7. **M84 Presidential Witness / Alien-Threat Rhetoric:** Carter's archived report and Reagan's 1987 hypothetical alien-threat rhetoric show how elite status changes narrative reach without changing evidence quality.

### New continuity rules from this pass

- **Codename collision is not lineage.** Shared project names cannot connect programs without documentary continuity.
- **Prestige is not corroboration.** A president, general, scientist, priest, or astronaut remains one source unless independent evidence exists.
- **An anticipated discovery is not a hidden discovery.** Planning for social effects of extraterrestrial contact does not establish prior contact.
- **An anomalous observation class is not one cause.** Transient lunar phenomena, UAP, USO, and technosignature candidates must be segmented case by case.
- **Retrocognitive output is a model until independently anchored.** A reconstructed past cannot silently become archival footage.
