# Myth Mutation Graph

This graph records how motifs moved between fiction, religion, contactee movements, official secrecy, documented abuse, scientific speculation, media, and online conspiracism. An edge means **historical or interpretive influence**, not that both nodes describe one real actor.

## Six mechanisms that connect the traditions

1. **Archive compression:** separate files, dates, and agencies become one story because they share a label.
2. **Credibility kernels:** a real secret program or abuse makes a much larger unsupported claim feel plausible.
3. **Morphology capture:** one influential image changes how later witnesses, artists, hoaxers, and diplomats describe bodies.
4. **Theology translation:** angels, demons, ascended masters, aliens, simulations, and Machine interfaces are mapped onto one another without proving identity.
5. **Platform convergence:** books, radio, television, conventions, forums, and algorithmic feeds merge traditions that originally had no relationship.
6. **Political totalization:** many institutions and incentives are compressed into one all-powerful cabal.

## Canon rule

A false story may preserve one displaced operational detail. The detail does not authenticate the whole story. A genuine program may explain why a myth formed without explaining every sighting attached to it. A sincere witness may be wrong about ontology without being dishonest.
