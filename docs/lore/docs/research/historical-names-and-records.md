# Historical Names and Records in the 2099 Timeline


Real names make the setting feel continuous with our world only when the game preserves what the historical record supports. People are used as authors, witnesses, archivists, investigators, scholars, or namesakes of later methods. The fiction does not turn them into secret aliens, traitors, or members of a hidden faction without evidence.

## Approved future namesakes

| Historical name or source | Supported historical connection | Fictional 2099 legacy |
|---|---|---|
| **Kenneth Arnold** | His 1947 report became foundational to modern flying-saucer vocabulary, and later language did not perfectly preserve the shapes he described. | **Arnold Lexicon Rule:** archive the witness's original words separately from later illustrations and category labels. |
| **Capt. Thomas Mantell** | Mantell died in 1948 after pursuing an unidentified object; the case accumulated competing explanations. | **Mantell Pursuit Rule:** no commander chases an anomaly beyond verified sensor support and vehicle limits. |
| **J. Allen Hynek** | Astronomer and Project Blue Book adviser associated with attempts to classify and evaluate reports. | **Hynek Residual Index:** separates insufficient identity data from genuinely anomalous behavior. |
| **Maj. Gen. John Samford** | Delivered the U.S. Air Force's prominent 1952 public UFO statement. | **Samford Threshold:** the evidentiary standard for when a military anomaly becomes a public-information event. |
| **Parviz Jafari** | Iranian Air Force pilot associated with the documented September 19, 1976 Tehran case. | **Jafari Chain:** witness, platform, sensor, weapon, and command records must remain distinct evidence objects. |
| **John Greenewald Jr.** | Founder of The Black Vault and long-running FOIA requester and archive publisher. | **Greenewald Standard:** preserve request, response, denial, appeal, release, metadata, redaction, and missing attachment separately. |
| **George Van Tassel, Robert Short, Ethel P. Hill, and Tuella** | Distinct contributors to historically fragmented Ashtar/contactee traditions. | Their works form the **Ashtar Provenance Archive**, used to detect later counterfeit command claims rather than prove the original claims true. |
| **Carla Rueckert, Don Elkins, and Jim McCarty** | Produced the documented Law of One/Ra Material corpus. | The **Rueckert-Elkins-McCarty Concordance** is studied as influential channeled literature and a precursor vocabulary for social memory, quarantine, and polarity. |
| **Zecharia Sitchin** | Popularized the modern extraterrestrial Anunnaki/Nibiru reconstruction. | The **Sitchin Mutation Map** teaches theology forensics how ancient text, modern interpretation, and internet embellishment diverge. |
| **Jacques Vallee** | Developed influential interpretive models involving cultural masks and a possible control system. | The **Vallee Mask Test** asks whether an encounter's form follows the observer's culture more closely than its alleged operator's technology. |
| **Ivan T. Sanderson** | Published a major book-length underwater-UFO interpretation. | The **Sanderson Split** requires analysts to separate the water association, observed behavior, and claimed undersea origin. |

## Naming restrictions

- Do not use a real person as a secret villain, alien, collaborator, or cult founder unless reliable sources establish the narrower historical fact being depicted.
- A memorial, protocol, archive, station, index, or scholarly dispute may carry a real name when its connection is documented.
- Living people should appear only in historical context unless legal and editorial review approves a fictionalized role.
- A named case remains an evidence problem. It does not become proof of the faction later inspired by it.
