# Next Deep Research Prompt - Alien and Space Mythos Provenance Pass

Conduct a source-genealogy investigation for the **Cosmic Conquest** 2099 universe.
The goal is not to prove conspiracies. The goal is to identify earliest accessible
sources, later mutations, evidence quality, contamination, cultural influence, and
responsible fictional adaptations.

## Fixed five-faction canon

- Humans / Solar Schism - Settle
- Federation of Light under Ashtar - Accord
- Xenos / Extraction Compact - Bind
- Pirates / Scrap Constellation - Route
- Machines / Continuance, Old Weather, Vigil, and Parallel - Continue

No sixth top-level faction may be introduced. New groups must be institutions,
subfactions, species, religions, technologies, or cross-faction movements.

## Evidence labels

- A Established core
- B Authentic record, unresolved attribution
- C Historical tradition
- D Traceable fringe claim
- E Contaminated, anonymous, forged, hoaxed, or circular claim
- F Original 2099 synthesis

For every source state:

1. What it actually says.
2. Earliest accessible dated version.
3. What its existence establishes.
4. What it does not establish.
5. Later embellishments and mergers.
6. Evidence for and against the extraordinary interpretation.
7. Harmful scapegoating or extremist contamination.
8. Best five-faction adaptation.

## Research divisions

### 1. Serpent and reptilian genealogy

Trace Robert E. Howard, Theosophy, Maurice Doreal, early reptilian encounter reports,
Draco lore, David Icke, Archon mergers, Anunnaki mergers, and antisemitic or racial
contamination. Identify the earliest dated source for each major mutation.

### 2. New World Order and depopulation

Separate real eugenics, forced sterilization, population policy, Georgia Guidestones,
Agenda 21, Great Reset, pandemic exercises, climate policy, and demographic forecasts
from the claim of one coordinated extermination program. Include international cases,
not only the United States.

### 3. Hollow Earth and polar myths

Trace Vril, Lemuria, Agartha, Saint-Yves, Shaver, Teros/Deros, Dulce, Nazi UFOs,
Operation Highjump, Admiral Byrd diaries, Akakor, Mount Shasta, Telos, and Antarctica.
Distinguish fiction, occultism, authentic expeditions, hoaxes, and later synthesis.

### 4. UFO religions and cosmic councils

Trace Ashtar, Aetherius Society, Unarius, Raelian movement, Council of Nine, Mark-Age,
Dorothy Martin/Sananda, Heaven's Gate, Urantia-related extraterrestrial interpretations,
and other relevant movements. Study failed prophecy, adaptive interpretation, energy
batteries, evacuation, embassies, creator claims, and cosmic government.

### 5. Abduction and hybrid programs

Compare Hill, Hopkins, Jacobs, Mack, Strieber, Mantid supervision, Zeta lore, screen
memories, missing time, reproductive claims, future-human models, trauma, hypnosis,
and cultural transmission. Preserve competing psychological, sociological, medical,
and extraordinary interpretations.

### 6. Prison planet and postmortem control

Trace ancient Gnostic texts separately from modern Archon, loosh, false-light, soul-net,
reincarnation-trap, Valdamar Valerian, Monroe, Castaneda, Icke, Scientology prison-planet,
and digital-community syntheses. Identify when each component first merged.

### 7. Time travel and editable reality

Trace Philadelphia Experiment, Montauk, Chronovisor, Project Pegasus, future-human
hypotheses, Mandela effects, simulation arguments, ancestor simulations, and fictional
influences including *Inside Job* Project Reboot. Separate observation, information
backflow, branch selection, and physical transit.

### 8. Environmental and atmospheric control

Study Project Popeye, Stormfury, cloud seeding, ENMOD, HAARP, contrail science,
chemtrail genealogy, geoengineering governance, earthquake-weapon claims, and ionospheric
research. Track the real program that provides each myth's credibility kernel.

### 9. Biological sampling and landscape messages

Review cattle-mutilation primary files, veterinary findings, FBI jurisdiction, animal
scavenging, clandestine sampling claims, crop-circle art and hoaxes, field-message claims,
and contemporary orbital-image authentication possibilities.

### 10. Persistent anomaly zones

Study Hessdalen, Marfa, Brown Mountain, Skinwalker Ranch, Bermuda Triangle, Bridgewater
Triangle, Uintah Basin, and comparable international locations. Separate location myth,
individual case quality, natural hypotheses, tourism/media effects, and instrumented data.

### 11. Hidden satellites and Solar System artifacts

Trace Black Knight, artificial Moon, Saturn-Moon matrix, hollow Moon, Shklovsky's Phobos
speculation, Mars monoliths, Cydonia, Bracewell probes, lurkers, co-orbitals, Trojans,
and artifact SETI. Distinguish obsolete scientific speculation from conspiracy mutation.

### 12. SETI political mythologies

Research zoo hypothesis, interdict hypothesis, dark forest, great filter, vulnerable
world, Berserker probes, grabby aliens, galactic clubs, and limits on total galactic
hegemony. Convert them into competing doctrines rather than evidence of actual aliens.

### 13. Inside Job corpus analysis

Analyze all eighteen episodes and creator interviews as a design corpus. Extract:

- organizations and domains of control;
- conspiracy references and source traditions;
- bureaucratic incentives;
- personal motives;
- reality-editing rules;
- satire techniques;
- methods for combining absurdity with sincere character stakes.

Do not reproduce dialogue or copyrighted story text. Do not treat the show as evidence.

### 14. Behavioral modification and psychic-warfare records

Trace BLUEBIRD, ARTICHOKE, MKULTRA, Project Monarch mutation, sensory deprivation,
hypnosis, drugs, memory research, the Gateway report, GRILL FLAME/STARGATE, remote
viewing, and later noetic-warfare claims. Separate program existence, ethical abuse,
statistical claims, operational utility, survivor testimony, therapy narratives, and
Internet synthesis.

### 15. Staged contact and synthetic revelation

Trace Project Blue Beam to Serge Monast and its later mutations; distinguish local
projection, electronic warfare, drones, augmented reality, broadcast compromise,
voice-to-target effects, noetic spoofing, and an implausible universal sky hologram.
Compare staged attacks, false flags, counterfeit Ashtar transmissions, and the legal
question of consent under synthetic revelation.

### 16. Secret space, lunar, collider, and chronology claims

Trace Solar Warden, non-terrestrial officers, twenty-and-back, breakaway civilization,
Apollo hoax literature, lunar anomalies, artificial Moon claims, Phobos speculation,
CERN portal stories, collider-apocalypse fears, ritual-prank contamination, Mandela

### 17. Elite forums, private networks, and ontology cells

Study Bilderberg, Chatham House Rule forums, think tanks, private clubs, intelligence
liaison groups, corporate boards, religious interpretations of UAP, John Keel's
ultraterrestrial model, the alleged Collins Elite, Mothman, window areas, and warning
entities. Distinguish agenda setting, social coordination, formal command, folklore,
religious interpretation, and evidence of an actual organization.

## Quality rules

- Use primary records and original publications wherever possible.
- Cite exact dates and distinguish event, publication, and declassification dates.
- Do not turn classification into proof.
- Do not turn an authentic file into authentication of its contents.
- Do not turn unresolved into extraterrestrial.
- Do not attribute modern alien theories to ancient religious texts.
- Explicitly identify antisemitic, racist, anti-Catholic, anti-Masonic, anti-migrant,
  or other scapegoating mutations and exclude them from canon.
- Never map a real population to an alien bloodline.
- Preserve at least two interpretations for every major unresolved mystery.
- Mark all 2099 synthesis as F.
