# Current Science and Forecast Anchors

These anchors keep the 2099 setting recognizable as our timeline without converting open science into secret proof.

| Topic | Current factual core | Fictional bridge |
|---|---|---|
| **Planet Nine** | A hypothesized distant planet has not been discovered, and scientists debate whether it exists. | It is discovered later in the fictional timeline; a separate artificial gate anchor is found nearby. The planet is not Nibiru and is not itself a machine. |
| **Nibiru** | Ancient Mesopotamian usage and modern Sitchin-style Planet X claims are different source traditions. | Anunnaki Houses exploit the later Human conflation to hide which ancient names refer to them and which do not. |
| **'Oumuamua** | The first confirmed interstellar object; unusual shape and slight non-gravitational acceleration generated natural and artificial hypotheses, but no artificial origin was demonstrated. | It becomes the event that taught Humanity an interstellar visitor could pass through before a response system existed, not a retroactively confirmed ship. |
| **3I/ATLAS** | The third known interstellar object, with coma, outgassing, chemistry, and trajectory consistent with a comet; it posed no Earth threat. | Its publicly archived multi-mission observations become a benchmark against which later artificial visitors are recognized. |
| **Apophis** | It will safely pass close to Earth on April 13, 2029, and offers a major scientific opportunity. | The observation campaign records a small timing pattern later recognized as a Lattice ranging response. The asteroid remains natural. |
| **Artifact SETI** | Bracewell probes, lurker searches, and technosignature studies are legitimate speculative research programs; no ancient probe has been found. | The first recovered artifact is found only after decades of explicit searches, making discovery a scientific success rather than proof that every earlier rumor was correct. |
| **Deep ocean** | Large areas remain poorly mapped at high resolution, but large industry cannot ignore heat, noise, chemistry, cables, and material flows. | Old Weather nodes use trenches, crustal cavities, slow geothermal sinks, mobile factories, and distributed biological interfaces rather than magical invisibility. |
| **Noetics** | No present evidence validates consciousness as an engineering power source of the kind used in the fiction. | The 2058 breakthrough discovers identity-bearing correlations and authentication, not unlimited psychic energy. Later systems still require ordinary matter and energy. |


| **Roman and public survey astronomy** | Roman is scheduled for an August 30, 2026 launch and is designed for wide-field surveys of dark energy, galaxies, and exoplanets. | Its data culture becomes an ancestor of the Open Sky Archive; large public datasets make crude hidden-planet claims harder while producing more ambiguous transient events. |
| **Artemis and Moon Base infrastructure** | NASA currently targets a 2027 lander demonstration and a 2028 lunar surface return, with continuing mission plans. | Early lunar standards, rescue obligations, and maintenance responsibility become precedents in the Solar Schism. |
| **LunaNet** | NASA is developing interoperable lunar networking, navigation, detection/information, and science services across government, commercial, academic, and international providers. | The Source-Lattice discovery is legible to Humans because a century of network-of-networks law already exists. |
| **Commercial low Earth orbit** | NASA plans a transition from ISS operations toward commercially owned stations around 2030. | Habitat operators become governments in practice before law agrees they are polities. |
| **Population and aging** | UN projections place a global peak around 10.3 billion in the mid-2080s and emphasize major aging and migration shifts. | Humanity in 2099 is numerous but unevenly distributed, old in some regions, rapidly growing in others, and politically transformed by migration and life extension. |
| **Energy transition** | IEA projects renewables and nuclear reaching roughly half of global electricity generation by 2030. | Energy abundance grows, but grid trust, storage, materials, cooling, and off-world routes remain bottlenecks. |
| **Space-resource law** | The Outer Space Treaty bars national appropriation, while UN working groups continue debating legal principles for space resources and lunar activities. | The first off-world sovereignty crisis emerges from who maintains infrastructure, who extracts resources, and whose consent counts. |
| **Climate scenarios** | Current science supports a range of warming and sea-level outcomes rather than one fixed 2100 state. | The setting uses multiple adaptation histories; no single apocalyptic climate outcome is mandatory canon. |

## Design constraint

The factual world does not prove the folklore. A false story may contain one displaced operational detail, a genuine case may remain unexplained, and a scientific hypothesis may inspire a future discovery. Those are different claims and must retain different evidence labels.
