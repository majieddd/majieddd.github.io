## Archive rules added to canon support

### Archive Version Skew

Never treat one page, mirror, ZIP, catalog entry, or local index as the archive's final state. Record:

1. agency transfer date;
2. NARA catalog state;
3. bulk-snapshot date;
4. secondary processing date;
5. local ingestion date;
6. later correction, appeal, or replacement.

### Media Integrity Ladder

Every image or video receives one of these provenance classes:

1. **Native sensor product** - original or validated export with sensor context.
2. **Documented export or transcode** - transformed, with a known conversion chain.
3. **Secondary display capture** - a camera recorded another display; added artifacts are expected.
4. **Altered copy, presented as received** - annotations, replay, enhancement, speed changes, or edits predate archival intake.
5. **Artistic reconstruction** - a visualization of testimony, not event footage.
6. **Narrative-only record** - no surviving visual object is available.

A lower-integrity class is not automatically false. It answers fewer questions.

### Proposal-Program-Operation Ladder

A name may refer to:

1. a suggestion;
2. a briefing deck;
3. a proposed compartment or PSAP;
4. an approved program;
5. a funded and staffed operation;
6. an operation that actually received material or produced results.

Game writing must never skip levels merely because a title sounds classified.

### Narrative Infrastructure

Witness testimony remains valuable, but it is stored beside rather than inside sensor conclusions. Reports preserve:

- exact original wording;
- later retellings;
- illustrations and media that influenced memory;
- interviewer prompts;
- agency summaries;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- contradictions that have not been reconciled.

## Second-pass additions: data quality, sensor custody, and classification

| New source lane | What is established | What is not established | Lore value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NPS data-vetting guidance** | Direct measurements and derived quantities must be separated. Estimates of range, speed, size, temperature, reflectivity, or radar cross-section require sensor-specific metadata; two separated optical sensors can provide triangulation and help reject near-field confusers. | That a multi-sensor report is automatically extraordinary, or that processed imagery preserves native photometric properties. | **Measurement Lineage:** every dossier records the raw observation, the assumptions used, the derived value, and whether another analyst can reproduce it. |
| **Native-master evidence protocol** | Current technical guidance recommends preserving original unprocessed files, hashing immediately after capture, keeping high-bitrate or RAW masters, logging UTC/location/equipment metadata, and labeling derivatives. | That a cryptographically intact file proves the depicted object is exotic. Hashes prove file integrity, not interpretation. | **Native Master Rule:** a faction may argue over meaning, but it cannot silently overwrite the evidence object from which the argument was derived. |
| **Event-based sensing and WATCHER** | Sandia's WATCHER pipeline demonstrates detection, tracking, and characterization of drone signatures in event-based sensor data. Event-based sensing can offer very fast response, low bandwidth, high dynamic range, and persistent monitoring. The cited demonstrations used known UAS targets. | Detection of alien technology or a finished universal UAP classifier. An initially unidentified cluster is a workflow state that may later become a known target. | **Provisional Unknown:** game sensors create tracks before identities. Players gain advantage by maintaining custody long enough to characterize periodic, linear, reflective, biological, or platform-motion signatures. |
| **AARO declassification guidance** | Imagery can remain classified because of the collecting platform, location, metadata, resolution, or source-and-method implications even when the depicted object is ordinary. | **Narrative reporting design** | AARO workshop participants recommended structured and narrative fields, provenance, optional metadata, human review, and an explicit “refuse to answer” option rather than pressuring witnesses to fabricate missing details. | That a standardized form removes memory limits, hoaxes, or interpretive disagreement. | **Refusal Is Data:** an unanswered field is distinguishable from a negative answer, forgotten detail, inaccessible record, or analyst assumption. |

### Measurement Lineage

Every computed claim must retain:

1. the direct measurement;
2. sensor and platform metadata;
3. calibration state;
4. assumptions;
5. transformation or processing history;
6. derived quantity;
7. uncertainty range;
8. independent reproduction status.

### Native Master Rule

Original files are hashed at intake and stored as immutable masters. Crops, contrast adjustments, stabilization, frame interpolation, denoising, transcription, OCR, and visual reconstruction are separate descendants. No derivative inherits the master's evidentiary class automatically.

### Provisional Unknown

“Unidentified” is a temporary analytical state, not a biological category. A track may move through these statuses:

`detected -> custody maintained -> characterized -> correlated -> identified`

or:

`detected -> custody lost -> active archive`

This becomes a core Archive War mechanic: preserving observation continuity can be more valuable than firing first.

### Source-Subject Split

Every withholding or classification record should specify which element is protected when known:

- observed subject;
- collection platform;
- sensor capability;
- location or operational pattern;
- analytic method;
- foreign-partner information;
- witness identity;
- unresolved combination of the above.

## What was deliberately not added

- No new historical case was declared extraterrestrial.
- No classified or withheld record was treated as proof of extraordinary content.
- No artistic recreation was treated as event footage.
- No proposed program was promoted into an operating program.
- No FBI or diplomatic file was treated as endorsement of the allegation it records.
- No NARA catalog lag was converted into a cover-up claim.
