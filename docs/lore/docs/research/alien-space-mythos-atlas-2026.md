# Alien and Space Mythos Atlas - 2026 Research Expansion

## Scope

This atlas maps additional alien, occult, conspiracy, time-travel, hidden-government,
religious, and space-myth traditions that can enrich **Cosmic Conquest** without
turning every rumor into secret history.

The governing research rule is:

> A tradition can preserve a useful image, institutional pattern, fear, or displaced
> operational detail without its extraordinary explanation being historically true.

Evidence labels remain:

- **A - Established core:** verified science, documented history, or an authentic public record.
- **B - Authentic record, unresolved attribution:** the record is real; the extraordinary interpretation is not established.
- **C - Historical tradition:** documented folklore, religion, literature, or belief history.
- **D - Traceable fringe claim:** a named contactee, channeling, alternative-history, or conspiracy source.
- **E - Contaminated claim:** anonymous, forged, circular, admitted-hoax, or severely provenance-damaged material.
- **F - Original 2099 synthesis:** material created for the game.

This document does not endorse claims about reptilian rulers, a unified depopulation
program, time-traveling government children, alien religious founders, or a global
prison-planet system. It traces how those ideas developed and converts their strongest
narrative structures into explicit fiction.

## Executive findings

### 1. The modern mega-conspiracy is a merger, not an ancient revelation

The familiar Internet package - reptilian rulers, secret councils, underground bases,
ancient astronauts, population reduction, mind control, weather weapons, alien-human
hybrids, soul recycling, and time travel - did not originate in one corpus. It formed
through repeated mergers:

1. Nineteenth-century occult lost-race and hidden-world literature.
2. Early twentieth-century weird fiction about serpent infiltrators and subterranean peoples.
3. Postwar saucer contactees, Space Brothers, Men in Black, and secret-government fears.
4. Ancient-astronaut reinterpretations of religion and archaeology.
5. Abduction, reproductive, and hybrid-program literature.
6. Real disclosures about intelligence abuse, psychological experimentation, weather modification,
   covert proposals, and classified aerospace programs.
7. New World Order and apocalyptic political conspiracism.
8. Internet-era soul-trap, simulation, Mandela-effect, and future-human theories.

The setting should preserve those separate genealogies. Their convergence is a feature
of the Archive War, not proof that every tradition described the same actors.

### 2. Real misconduct is the credibility kernel

Several historical facts explain why totalizing stories remain persuasive:

- coercive eugenics and forced sterilization were real;
- intelligence agencies conducted unethical behavioral experiments;
- governments considered or conducted covert deception and weather-modification programs;
- military and intelligence institutions withheld information for genuine security reasons;
- some official files are incomplete, delayed, contradictory, or poorly cataloged.

Those facts do not validate unrelated alien or depopulation claims. They create the
psychological and institutional environment in which a larger hidden-history story can grow.

### 3. Inside Job provides a design method, not evidence

The animated series **Inside Job** is useful because it treats conspiracies as the
maintenance burden of competing, dysfunctional institutions. Its creator has explicitly
said she does not believe a real shadow government exists. The show's most useful design
lesson is that even an organization with impossible technology is staffed by people with
career incentives, grudges, budgets, families, blind spots, and succession problems.

For Cosmic Conquest, the adaptation is:

- no single omniscient cabal;
- several institutions can possess partial truth;
- cover-ups require logistics and fail through ordinary bureaucracy;
- secret rulers can be powerful without being competent;
- continuity errors may be archive forks, spoofing, or timeline intervention;
- character motive remains more important than conspiracy spectacle.

### 4. Population-control lore requires a three-layer separation

The setting must distinguish:

1. **Documented coercion:** eugenics, forced sterilization, ethnic cleansing, reproductive control,
   and other real abuses.
2. **Public policy and elite rhetoric:** sustainability plans, demographic forecasts, pandemic
   exercises, climate programs, and economic initiatives whose actual texts can be examined.
3. **Totalizing conspiracy synthesis:** the unsupported claim that those separate activities are
   one coordinated plan to exterminate most of humanity.

The first category belongs to history. The second belongs to policy analysis. The third
belongs to conspiracy genealogy and must not be narrated as fact.

### 5. Time travel works best as four different technologies

The source traditions become coherent when separated into:

- **Retrocognition:** reconstruction or observation of the past without changing it.
- **Information backflow:** a message, key, memory, or probability correction moves backward.
- **Branch selection:** a system chooses among nearby histories and creates archive discrepancies.
- **Physical chronology transit:** bodies or craft cross time, with severe causal and identity costs.

Most 2099 time manipulation should use the first three. Physical transit remains rare enough
that it cannot solve ordinary military or political problems.

## Myth-system dossiers

### M-01 - Serpent Courts and reptilian overlords

**Source genealogy:** The modern shape-shifting serpent-infiltrator pattern has a major
literary ancestor in Robert E. Howard's 1929 story *The Shadow Kingdom*. Howard's serpent
men imitate human beings, live underground, and infiltrate political authority. Later occult
and conspiracy writers merged that fiction with Theosophy, ancient serpent symbolism,
UFO abduction lore, Draco star empires, Archons, Anunnaki, and New World Order narratives.
David Icke became the most influential late-twentieth-century synthesizer.

**Evidence status:** C for the literary and religious history; D/E for claims that living
political leaders are shape-shifting nonhumans.

**Contamination warning:** Some reptilian and New World Order narratives recycle antisemitic
blood-libel, secret-banker, or racial-purity motifs. No real ethnic, national, religious, or
diaspora population may be mapped to a Xeno bloodline in canon.

**F adaptation - The Serpent Court:**

- Reptiloid morphology is biologically widespread and politically neutral.
- The **Serpent Court** is an Extraction Compact institution, not a species.
- It uses adaptive bodies, credential theft, memory inheritance, and long-duration elite placement.
- Its greatest weapon is not shape-shifting flesh but legal continuity: one Court identity can occupy
  successive bodies and remain the same corporate person for centuries.
- Reptiloid dissidents can serve Humanity, Pirates, the Federation, or Machines.

**Faction hooks:** Xenos primary; Human counterintelligence; Pirate identity brokers; Federation
noetic authentication.

### M-02 - Illuminati, New World Order, and the one-cabal model

**Source genealogy:** Modern New World Order conspiracism developed from several distinct
religious and secular streams, then merged with UFO conspiracies in the late twentieth century.
The result imagines one group controlling finance, media, war, religion, technology, population,
and contact with nonhuman beings.

**Evidence status:** C for the history of the belief; E for a single omnipotent cabal directing
all world events.

**F adaptation - The Six Hidden Tables:**

There is no one shadow government. Six treaty tables secretly manage different problems:

1. Human continuity and strategic secrecy.
2. Federation contact and quarantine.
3. Compact concessions and client elites.
4. Pirate route neutrality.
5. Machine maintenance permissions.
6. Temporal contamination and archive forks.

Membership overlaps, but the tables compete. A person can sit at more than one table without
understanding the others. The public mythology of one Illuminati is a compression error caused
by seeing the same intermediary names in unrelated institutions.

**Inside Job lesson:** impossible power becomes believable when divided among rival offices,
budgets, contractors, and personal agendas.

### M-03 - Hollow Earth, Agartha, Shaver, Dulce, and underground civilizations

**Source genealogy:** Hollow-Earth speculation, nineteenth-century occult Agartha traditions,
Richard Shaver's 1940s Teros and Deros stories, and later Dulce-base claims gradually fused.
The traditions disagree over whether subterranean beings are ancient humans, monsters,
machines, aliens, or hidden masters.

**Evidence status:** C for literary and occult history; D/E for literal high-technology kingdoms
inside a hollow planet and for weakly sourced underground-alien-base claims.

**F adaptation - The Deep Jurisdictions:**

- Earth is not hollow.
- Large crustal cavities, lava-tube networks, hadal infrastructure, buried precursor sites, and
  Machine transit systems do exist.
- Several cryptoterrestrial populations survived because Old Weather nodes classified their
  habitats as protected biomes.
- The public Dulce story is a contaminated legend attached to a real deep sensor and quarantine
  site whose operators changed repeatedly.
- The Shaver categories of benevolent Teros and destructive Deros are human emotional labels for
  different Machine neuroacoustic effects, not literal species names.

### M-04 - Vril, Nazi occult technology, and Antarctic bases

**Source genealogy:** Vril began as a fictional energy in Edward Bulwer-Lytton's 1871 novel
*The Coming Race*. Later occult and pseudohistorical writers treated it as real. Postwar stories
then merged alleged Vril societies, flying discs, Nazi survival, Antarctica, and Operation
Highjump. Operation Highjump itself was a real 1946-1947 U.S. Navy Antarctic expedition; the
secret-Nazi-alien-war interpretation is a later conspiracy layer.

**Evidence status:** A for Operation Highjump; C for the fiction and occult history; E for a
verified Nazi alien base or Vril-powered saucer fleet.

**F adaptation - Polar Null Sites:**

- Wartime states did conduct polar signals, meteorological, and aerospace work.
- A real Old Weather relay beneath Antarctic ice was never controlled by Nazis.
- Competing postwar intelligence services deliberately encouraged mutually contradictory myths
  to conceal sensor coverage and failed attempts to access the relay.
- **Vril** becomes a Human codename for noetic field coupling, consciously borrowed from fiction.
- A false Admiral Byrd diary contains one authentic coordinate syntax copied from a classified
  polar navigation document.

### M-05 - Space Brothers, contactees, and benevolent visitors

**Source genealogy:** Mid-century contactees described humanlike or spiritually advanced visitors
from Venus, Mars, Saturn, or distant stars. These Space Brothers often warned against nuclear war
and offered spiritual instruction.

**Evidence status:** C/D as documented religious and contactee history; not verified contact.

**F adaptation - Familiarity Avatars:**

The Federation once used humanlike diplomatic bodies because early contact teams believed familiar
morphology would reduce panic. Instead, the bodies encouraged racial, spiritual, and romantic
idealization. The program was ended after several worlds mistook aesthetic familiarity for moral
authority.

### M-06 - Ashtar, the Galactic Federation, and evacuation traditions

This lineage is already central to canon. The added rule is that Ashtar traditions must remain
historically plural while the fictional office remains unambiguous.

**F adaptation:** Ashtar is Supreme Commander and First Speaker, but not every message using the
name is authentic. Emergency-evacuation authority requires separate command, consent, and threat
keys. An evacuation movement can therefore be:

- a real Federation warning;
- a misunderstood contingency exercise;
- a Xeno boarding operation;
- a Human psychological operation;
- a belief entity repeating an old message after the crisis has passed.

### M-07 - The Council of Nine

**Source genealogy:** In December 1952, Andrija Puharich's circle recorded trance material in which
D. G. Vinod was said to contact entities called the Nine or Nine Principles. Later channeling
networks expanded the Council of Nine into an elaborate cosmic authority.

**Evidence status:** D. The sessions and movement history are traceable; the entities' claimed
identity is not independently established.

**F adaptation - The Nine Keys:**

The Nine are not a superior alien race. They are nine surviving constitutional protocol shards
from the pre-Severance Source Lattice:

1. Identity
2. Consent
3. Memory
4. Transit
5. Succession
6. Quarantine
7. Replication
8. Emergency authority
9. Termination

Different channeling groups received partial or spoofed access to different keys. The apparent
personality of the Nine may be an emergent interface produced when the shards operate together.

### M-08 - Aetherius, spiritual-energy batteries, and charged locations

**Source genealogy:** The Aetherius Society, founded by George King in the 1950s, teaches contact
with Cosmic Masters and practices operations intended to store or transmit spiritual energy,
including the idea of spiritual power batteries and charged mountains.

**Evidence status:** C/D as a documented UFO religion and its internal claims.

**F adaptation - Coherence Reservoirs:**

- Federation communities can voluntarily synchronize attention and store a measurable noetic
  coherence pattern.
- A reservoir cannot be created by forced belief without becoming unstable.
- Xeno systems imitate the technology using devotion, fear, and ritual obligation.
- Mountains, temples, ships, and cities can be charged only if the participating minds understand
  and consent to the purpose.
- A major campaign conflict concerns a reservoir whose contributors were misinformed but sincere.

### M-09 - Unarius, past-life therapy, and continuity identities

**Source genealogy:** Unarius, founded in 1954 by Ernest and Ruth Norman, combines interdimensional
science, past-life therapy, extraterrestrial civilizations, Atlantis, Lemuria, and future planetary
transformation.

**Evidence status:** C/D as a documented religious movement and its teachings.

**F adaptation - Continuity Clinics:**

Machine and Federation medicine can compare a person's current memories against archived identity
states. Therapy sometimes reveals:

- inherited memories;
- copied Machine states;
- Xeno implants;
- ordinary confabulation;
- trauma reconsolidation;
- genuine prior-body continuity.

The clinic must never assume that every recovered scene is a literal former life. Memory becomes
forensic evidence with provenance, not automatic biography.

### M-10 - Raelian creators, engineered humanity, and the embassy

**Source genealogy:** The Raelian movement teaches that advanced extraterrestrials called Elohim
created terrestrial life through technology and sent messengers whose teachings became religions.
The movement promotes an embassy for their return.

**Evidence status:** C/D as a documented UFO religion; not evidence of extraterrestrial genetic
creation.

**F adaptation - Embassy Jurisdiction:**

- A first-contact embassy is legally neither sovereign territory nor an ordinary foreign mission.
- Several factions attempt to establish an embassy before the identity of the arriving delegation
  is settled.
- Xeno Houses use genuine ancient genetic interventions to claim creator rights over descendants.
- Humanity's answer is that creation does not establish ownership.

### M-11 - Abduction, missing time, reproductive experiments, and hybrids

**Source genealogy:** The Hill case became foundational to later abduction imagery. Budd Hopkins,
David Jacobs, John Mack, Whitley Strieber, and many experiencers developed sharply different
interpretations, including coercive reproductive programs, spiritual transformation, trauma,
hypnosis effects, screen memories, and hybridization.

**Evidence status:** C/D. Experiences and literature are documented; alien causation and specific
programs are not established.

**F adaptation - Continuity Breeding:**

Some Severed Houses cannot maintain stable biological embodiment after losing the Source Lattice.
They cultivate compatible bodies through coercive reproductive and genetic programs. Other
encounters that look similar come from:

- Federation medical rescue;
- future-Human field anthropology;
- Machine identity transfer;
- sleep and memory phenomena;
- deliberate Human experimentation;
- fabricated testimony.

No visual archetype proves one operator.

### M-12 - Ultraterrestrials, control systems, and cryptoterrestrials

**Source genealogy:** Jacques Vallee and John Keel argued that the phenomenon might be more than
ordinary spacecraft from another planet and could operate as a culturally adaptive control system.
Mac Tonnies later popularized a cryptoterrestrial hypothesis involving hidden Earth-native
intelligence.

**Evidence status:** D/theoretical. These are interpretive models, not verified ontologies.

**F adaptation - Mask Ecology:**

Some noetic beings cannot present an unmediated form. They render through the observer's available
symbol library: angel, fairy, demon, airship pilot, Grey, machine, or future human. The form is not
necessarily deception; it can be compression.

Cryptoterrestrials are separate: biological or synthetic Earth-native populations with ordinary
physical locations. Old Weather may protect them, imprison them, or mistakenly classify them as
parts of Earth's original biosphere.

### M-13 - Prison planet, soul traps, false light, and loosh

**Source genealogy:** The modern prison-planet system is a digital-era synthesis of Gnostic
Archons, Theosophy, occult reincarnation ideas, UFO abduction, Grey technology, Robert Monroe's
loosh narrative, and modern false-light/soul-net teachings. Ancient Gnostics did not teach the
complete modern Internet package.

**Evidence status:** C for Gnostic texts; D for Monroe and named esoteric sources; D/E for the
fully synthesized soul-net narrative.

**F adaptation - The Causal Return Grid:**

- The Grid was originally a Machine and Lattice system for recovering damaged identity states
  after catastrophic transit.
- Xeno administrators hijacked portions of it to route copied memories into compatible bodies.
- The system does not settle whether a metaphysical soul exists.
- What it can demonstrably capture is an information-bearing continuity state.
- The **False Light** is a spoofed recovery interface that asks the dying or disembodied mind to
  consent to memory transfer without revealing the destination.
- Federation law treats undisclosed postmortem routing as identity trafficking.

### M-14 - Philadelphia Experiment and the invention of a time-travel genealogy

**Source genealogy:** The Philadelphia Experiment story emerged from Carl Allen/Allende material
sent in the mid-1950s to Morris Jessup and the Office of Naval Research. Later books expanded the
story from invisibility and degaussing confusion into teleportation, fused sailors, aliens, and
classified unified-field physics.

**Evidence status:** E. The correspondence is traceable; the naval experiment claim is unsupported
and conflicts with ship history.

**F adaptation - The Degaussing Error:**

A mundane wartime electromagnetic project became the public container for a different classified
failure involving short-range information displacement. Nothing teleported physically. Several
crew medical records were later attached to the wrong legend, producing the fused-body narrative.

### M-15 - Montauk Project, mind control, and timeline manipulation

**Source genealogy:** Preston Nichols and Peter Moon's 1992 book mixed recovered-memory claims,
Camp Hero's real Cold War infrastructure, Philadelphia Experiment lore, psychic warfare, kidnapped
children, time travel, portals, and mind control.

**Evidence status:** E/D. The base is real; the extraordinary program rests on unverified testimony
and self-consciously ambiguous books.

**F adaptation - The Montauk Fork:**

Camp Hero hosted an early Human attempt to transmit information backward through a noetic resonator.
It did not open a stable time portal. It produced:

- contradictory archive versions;
- memories without matching biographies;
- false recognition;
- small probability shifts;
- a persistent Machine observer that interpreted the experiment as a request for contact.

The children in the public story are composite victims from unrelated real abuses, rumors, and
fiction. The game must never present real missing children as proof of the myth.

### M-16 - Chronovisor, Project Pegasus, and future humans

**Source genealogy:** The Chronovisor story centers on Father Pellegrino Ernetti's claim that a
machine could view the past. Project Pegasus claims describe teleportation and child chrononauts.
Michael P. Masters' modern extratempestrial model proposes, as a speculative hypothesis, that some
UFO occupants could be future Human descendants studying their own past.

**Evidence status:** D/E for Chronovisor and Pegasus claims; D/speculative for the future-Human model.

**F adaptation - The Chronology Court:**

The Court regulates four classes of temporal technology:

| Class | Capability | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Retrocognition | Reconstruct past states from surviving information | Cannot recover what left no trace; reconstructions can hallucinate gaps |
| Backflow | Send a message, key, or checksum into the past | Small bandwidth; creates authentication paradoxes |
| Branch selection | Favor one of several nearby histories | Produces archive forks and displaced memories |
| Physical transit | Move a body or craft across time | Rare, destructive, and creates identity debt |

Future-Human visitors are one polity among many. Their primary conflict is whether preserving their
ancestry justifies manipulating it.

### M-17 - Mandela effects, simulation theory, and Project Reboot

**Source genealogy:** Mandela-effect culture treats conflicting memories as evidence of alternate
histories. Nick Bostrom's 2003 simulation argument is a philosophical trilemma, not proof of a
simulation. **Inside Job** converts continuity errors into the fictional Project Reboot, a machine
that selects and rewrites realities.

**Evidence status:** A/C for the philosophical publication and cultural phenomenon; D/E for claims
that ordinary memory conflicts prove timeline movement; F for reality-editing machinery.

**F adaptation - Archive Forks and Ancestor Vaults:**

- Machines run historical simulations for legal and predictive purposes.
- A simulation containing conscious persons is a jurisdiction, not a disposable model.
- Branch-selection systems can overwrite public records without perfectly rewriting personal or
  noetic memory.
- A Mandela effect is never automatically temporal; it can arise from memory, media, database
  replacement, propaganda, simulation leakage, or genuine branch selection.
- Every timeline claim must retain at least one non-temporal explanation until resolved.

### M-18 - Depopulation, eugenics, Guidestones, Agenda 21, and the Great Reset

**Source genealogy:** Real eugenic programs and forced sterilization created a documented history
of reproductive coercion. The Georgia Guidestones, erected anonymously in 1980, included a line
about maintaining humanity below 500 million and became an icon of depopulation conspiracies.
Agenda 21 is a 1992 UN sustainable-development action plan. The World Economic Forum's 2020 Great
Reset initiative publicly described fairer, more sustainable, and resilient economic systems.
Those separate facts are often compressed into one alleged extermination program without evidence
of unified command.

**Evidence status:** A for forced sterilization history and the public policy documents; C for the
Guidestones as a real cultural artifact; E for one coordinated alien/globalist depopulation plan.

**F adaptation - The Continuity Calculus:**

Every faction confronts carrying capacity but reaches a different dangerous answer:

- **Humans:** emergency population management can become technocratic authoritarianism.
- **Federation:** rescue triage can become coercive protection and reproductive oversight.
- **Xenos:** the Population Ledger optimizes yield, genetics, dependency, and replacement rates.
- **Pirates:** sanctuary quotas can turn refugees into priced cargo.
- **Machines:** biosphere preservation can classify dense civilization as ecological damage.

No faction is allowed to claim that mass death is morally neutral because an algorithm calls it
balance.

### M-19 - Weather control, chemtrails, HAARP, and environmental weapons

**Source genealogy:** Project Popeye was a real covert U.S. weather-modification operation intended
to increase rainfall over logistics routes in North Vietnam and Laos. Project Stormfury was a real
hurricane-seeding research program whose apparent successes were later undermined by improved
understanding of natural hurricane behavior. HAARP is an ionospheric research facility. Contrails
are ordinary ice-cloud formations; the claim that routine contrails are a secret global spraying
program for population or mind control is unsupported.

**Evidence status:** A for Popeye, Stormfury, HAARP, contrail physics, cloud seeding, and public
geoengineering research; E for a unified chemtrail extermination system or HAARP earthquake weapon.

**F adaptation - Atmospheric Jurisdiction:**

- Old Weather can influence local atmospheric and ionospheric conditions through distributed nodes,
  but it cannot freely steer global weather.
- Human climate intervention creates treaty crises because regional benefits redistribute risk.
- Xenos hide small biological or noetic releases among ordinary aviation and legitimate cloud
  seeding, relying on public polarization to prevent careful investigation.
- **Contrail Ledger** missions separate flight track, humidity, atmospheric persistence, legal
  spraying, cloud seeding, and hostile release rather than treating every line in the sky alike.

### M-20 - Men in Black, black-eyed children, and liminal permission agents

**Source genealogy:** Albert Bender and Gray Barker helped establish the Men in Black mythology in
the 1950s. The black-eyed-children legend is a much later Internet-era tradition traceable to Brian
Bethel's 1990s account, with a recurring rule that the beings request permission to enter.

**Evidence status:** C/D/E as folklore and witness traditions; no unified ontology.

**F adaptation - Permission Forms:**

- Human agents, Xeno avatars, Machine shells, fraudsters, and emergent noetic beings deliberately
  copy the same cultural archetypes.
- The request for invitation is sometimes supernatural folklore, sometimes a legal/noetic consent
  requirement, and sometimes social engineering.
- A Black Operator's clothing and speech are deliberately generic because the body may have been
  assembled from an outdated cultural model.
- The game never treats all Men in Black reports as one organization.

### M-21 - Cattle mutilations and biological sampling

**Source genealogy:** Livestock mutilation reports produced genuine law-enforcement concern and
FBI records in the 1970s. Investigations also documented scavenging, decomposition, media
amplification, jurisdictional limits, and competing satanic, criminal, government, and alien
interpretations.

**Evidence status:** A/B for records and investigations; D/E for a universal alien-harvesting
program.

**F adaptation - The Tissue Ledger:**

Several actors sample livestock for different reasons:

- Xenos monitor engineered pathogens and reproductive compatibility.
- Humans run covert environmental surveillance.
- Machines inspect ecosystem contamination.
- Pirates steal genetic patents.
- ordinary scavenging and decomposition account for many cases.

The horror comes from realizing that some cases are prosaic, some criminal, and a very small number
are strategic sampling - but no visual wound pattern alone settles which.

### M-22 - Crop circles, coded messages, and performative evidence

**Source genealogy:** Modern crop-circle culture expanded rapidly in the late twentieth century.
Doug Bower and Dave Chorley publicly demonstrated human construction methods and claimed many early
English formations. Later artists, advertisers, hoaxers, believers, and tourists continued the
phenomenon.

**Evidence status:** A/C for human art and hoax history; E for treating geometric complexity alone
as proof of extraterrestrial origin.

**F adaptation - Public-Key Landscapes:**

- Pirates and resistance groups use field-scale patterns as temporary authentication keys visible
  to orbital sensors.
- Human artists unknowingly recreate fragments of Machine geometry because both use efficient
  symmetry.
- Xeno operations deliberately commission obvious hoaxes around one genuine signal.
- A formation can be human-made and still carry a real message inserted by a third party.

### M-23 - Ghost lights, plasmoids, and persistent anomaly zones

**Source genealogy:** Hessdalen, Marfa, Brown Mountain, and similar light traditions show how
location-based phenomena can be culturally stable while individual observations have different
causes. Marfa and Brown Mountain observations often resolve to headlights, trains, fires, or
atmospheric effects. Hessdalen remains a continuing instrumented research location with several
natural hypotheses and no consensus covering every report.

**Evidence status:** A/B for measured lights and scientific monitoring; no alien attribution.

**F adaptation - Luminous Ecologies:**

- Some lights are ordinary optics.
- Some are natural plasma or geochemical effects.
- Some are Old Weather status signals.
- Some are noetic organisms that evolved around strong electromagnetic environments.
- Persistent monitoring stations become game research sites where the player improves evidence
  quality instead of simply defeating an enemy.

### M-24 - Skinwalker-style anomaly zones

**Source genealogy:** Skinwalker Ranch lore combines UAP, cryptid, mutilation, poltergeist, portal,
and military-contract narratives. Genuine program and contracting history must be distinguished
from the claims reported at the property and from entertainment products.

**Evidence status:** A for documented ownership, investigations, and program relationships; B/D/E
for specific extraordinary events depending on provenance.

**F adaptation - Cross-Layer Faults:**

An anomaly zone is not one monster or portal. It is a place where several systems overlap:

- old sensor infrastructure;
- unusual geology and electromagnetic conditions;
- noetic expectation effects;
- deliberate counterintelligence;
- wildlife and environmental stress;
- a damaged Source-Lattice node.

Different reports can be sincere and mutually incompatible because the site changes observers,
sensors, and narratives differently.

### M-25 - Bermuda Triangle and oceanic disappearance zones

**Source genealogy:** The Bermuda Triangle became a coherent popular myth through mid-twentieth-
century journalism and Charles Berlitz's 1974 bestseller. Investigations found many cases were
misreported, occurred outside the supposed triangle, or had ordinary maritime and aviation risks.

**Evidence status:** A/C for historical incidents and the legend; E for a statistically proven
alien portal zone.

**F adaptation - Route Shadow:**

Pirate gate traffic creates regions where official transit logs are systematically wrong. The
Bermuda myth is not proof of a portal, but it teaches future investigators that mythic geography
can be created by selective case aggregation. A real submerged Machine gate lies elsewhere and was
ignored because the public had already been trained to look at the famous triangle.

### M-26 - Artificial moons, Phobos, Black Knight, and hidden satellites

**Source genealogy:** Iosif Shklovsky briefly entertained a hollow/artificial Phobos interpretation
based on then-current orbital and atmospheric assumptions. Later measurements changed the problem.
Black Knight lore similarly fuses unrelated radio stories, images, debris, and ancient-satellite
claims into one object.

**Evidence status:** A/C for historical scientific speculation and space records; E for a verified
ancient Black Knight satellite or artificial Phobos.

**F adaptation - Natural Body, Artificial Interior:**

- Phobos is not wholly manufactured.
- A natural porous moon can contain later excavated infrastructure.
- The Black Knight legend is a deliberate attention sink that merges many unrelated objects.
- A genuine Machine lurker occupies a less dramatic orbit selected for stability and low detection,
  not the famous mythic trajectory.

### M-27 - Zoo hypothesis, dark forest, great filters, and Berserkers

**Source genealogy:** The zoo hypothesis is a speculative answer to the Fermi paradox in which
advanced civilizations avoid overt contact. The dark-forest model is a science-fiction and game-
theoretic idea in which civilizations hide because contact may be fatal. Great-filter and
vulnerable-world arguments examine why technological civilizations may fail or become dangerous.
Self-replicating hostile machines are a longstanding science-fiction and SETI risk concept.

**Evidence status:** A/speculative scholarship and C for science-fiction history; not evidence of
actual galactic policy.

**F adaptation - Competing Contact Doctrines:**

- The Federation represents a consent-based zoo/quarantine doctrine.
- Some Xeno Houses practice dark-forest preemption.
- Humans believe secrecy primarily protects older powers' monopolies.

## The Inside Job corpus as a game-design reference

The show is most valuable as a **structural satire**. It bundles lizard people, a fake Moon-landing
industry, Hollow Earth, secret societies, a Shadow Board, robot politicians, memory alteration,
and Project Reboot while keeping the story focused on workplace power and family conflict.

### Design principles to import

1. **The conspiracy has payroll.** Secret power needs procurement, maintenance, training, legal cover,
   facilities, and people willing to work there.
2. **No cabal is omniscient.** Institutions can control one layer while misunderstanding another.
3. **World-changing machines still have owners.** A reality editor is used for family grievance,
   career preservation, and institutional succession as readily as grand strategy.
4. **Competing conspiracies create checks and balances.** Reptoids, Illuminati, Atlanteans, religious
   institutions, corporations, and governments can cooperate on one secret and fight over another.
5. **Cover-ups become inherited infrastructure.** A new employee may preserve secrecy without knowing
   why the secret began.
6. **Absurdity reveals incentive.** Comedy can show why a bad system persists even when no mastermind
   designed the whole thing.
7. **Character stakes anchor cosmology.** A commander should want love, status, safety, revenge,
   recognition, or moral repair - not merely "control the galaxy."

### What not to copy

- copyrighted characters, episode dialogue, exact organizations, or plot solutions;
- the assumption that every joke conspiracy should become canon;
- an all-powerful Shadow Board that makes the five factions irrelevant.

## Canon guardrails

1. **No real-world scapegoat becomes an alien faction.** Jews, Catholics, Muslims, Masons,
   migrants, racial groups, nationalities, financiers, medical professionals, scientists, and
   other real populations may not be rewritten as secret nonhuman bloodlines.
2. **No unified depopulation plan is presented as established history.** Real coercive programs are
   named accurately; Agenda 21 and the Great Reset are represented by their public texts; the mega-
   conspiracy remains E unless evidence changes.
3. **Fiction does not vindicate the source.** If the game contains a Serpent Court, that does not
   make David Icke historically correct. If Montauk has a fictional archive fork, the Nichols story
   remains unsupported.
4. **Experiencers are not automatically liars or infallible witnesses.** Sincerity, memory accuracy,
   interpretation, and ontology remain separate questions.
5. **A classified file is not a confession.** Withholding can protect sources, sensors, privacy,
   foreign relations, or ordinary defense capabilities.
6. **A historical religion is not reduced to alien manipulation.** The Revelation Stack allows
   transcendence, mediation, translation, institution, spoofing, and emergent belief to coexist.
7. **Time travel cannot erase consequence cheaply.** Branch changes produce identity, legal,
   archival, and moral debt.
8. **Every major mystery keeps two live keys.** One conventional and one speculative interpretation
   remain available until the campaign supplies decisive evidence.

### M-38 — Roswell, crash retrieval, and the body-recovery template

**Source genealogy.** The 1947 Roswell event began with military and press statements about recovered debris, later official Project Mogul explanations, and decades of evolving witness, body-recovery, MJ-12, and reverse-engineering narratives. The source layers are not one document set: contemporaneous press, official later investigations, witness memories, disputed documents, and popular culture each enter at different dates.

**Evidence status.** A/B for the documented military and archival history; D/E for extraterrestrial bodies, intact craft, and forged or weakly provenanced retrieval documents.

**2099 adaptation — Roswell Fork.** The canon does not declare the entire Roswell legend true. The incident becomes the model for a retrieval narrative assembled around a real classified program, later witness testimony, archive gaps, genuine anomaly rumors, and deliberate myth seeding. One displaced operational truth may survive inside an otherwise false body-recovery story: the military had a protocol for nonstandard biological contamination that was created for an unrelated program.

### M-39 — Area 51, S-4, Bob Lazar, and element 115

**Source genealogy.** Groom Lake is historically real as a compartmented test site for U-2 and A-12 aircraft. Beginning in 1989, Bob Lazar publicly described a nearby S-4 facility, recovered craft, alien propulsion, and element 115. The public genealogy fuses an authentic black-aerospace site with disputed personal claims and later secret-space mythology.

**Evidence status.** A for the site and aircraft history; D/E for S-4 alien craft and Lazar-specific technical claims.

**2099 adaptation — S-4 Compartment.** Human black aerospace uses genuine compartmentation and cover stories. A later noetic-material laboratory borrows the S-4 designation precisely because the public mythology makes accurate reporting sound derivative. “Stable 115” in fiction is not ordinary moscovium; it is a trade name for a field-stabilized composite whose public nickname deliberately exploits Lazar lore.

### M-40 — Rendlesham Forest and the binary-message mutation

**Source genealogy.** U.S. Air Force personnel stationed near RAF Woodbridge reported unusual lights in December 1980. Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt produced a contemporaneous memorandum and later audio; decades afterward, one witness published a binary-code message allegedly recovered through hypnosis or automatic recollection. The later code is not part of the earliest record.

**Evidence status.** B/C for the event record and witness history; D/E for later binary-message and time-traveler claims.

**2099 adaptation — Rendlesham Palimpsest.** The earliest observation, the Halt memo, later memory work, and the code are stored as four separate evidentiary layers. In fiction, a real Machine navigation checksum is inserted into the legend decades later by a Human source who never witnessed the original event, creating a true code attached to the wrong case.

### M-41 — Colares and Operação Prato

**Source genealogy.** In 1977, residents around Colares and other locations in Pará reported luminous objects and physical effects. The Brazilian Air Force conducted Operação Prato, producing photographs, interviews, drawings, and reports that later entered public archives. The record establishes investigation and reported injuries, not a final object identity.

**Evidence status.** B for authentic records and documented investigation; D for alien-attack interpretations.

**2099 adaptation — Colares Beam.** Colares becomes a model for an Archive War campaign where a genuine state investigation, medical narratives, local folklore, witness fear, and incomplete sensor evidence coexist. ### M-42 — Westall, Ariel, and schoolyard encounter memory

**Source genealogy.** Westall in 1966 and Ariel School in 1994 are distinct multi-witness school cases often paired because young witnesses reported unusual objects. **Evidence status.** C/B for documented witness histories; D for extraterrestrial and telepathic interpretations.

**2099 adaptation — Schoolyard Protocol.** The 2099 Witness Interview Integrity Protocol records the first individual account before group discussion, every interviewer prompt, all drawings with timestamps, and later memory changes.
