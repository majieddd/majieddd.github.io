# Alien and Space Mythos Connection Delta - August 2026

Release **0.4.0** expands the lore from 37 to **60 myth systems**, from 74 to **100 Mythos Operations**, and adds **28 global case dossiers**, **57 documented mutation edges**, **12 new cross-faction systems**, and an 18-episode *Inside Job* design map.

## Most important conclusions

1. **Mega-conspiracy is a merger.** Reptilian rulers, underground bases, crash retrieval, population reduction, time travel, secret space, prison planets, and staged contact entered the culture through different decades and source types.
2. **Real abuse creates Trust Debt, not unlimited proof.** MKULTRA, coercive sterilization, covert weather modification, black aerospace, and information operations make skepticism understandable; they do not validate every later extension.
3. **Inside Job contributes institutional design, not evidence.** Its useful lesson is that hidden power has budgets, layoffs, maintenance, succession, interpersonal conflict, and error.
4. **Global cases need a Translation Horizon.** Archive access and cultural reach determine which incidents become canonical far more than evidence strength alone.
5. **Time travel must be tiered.** Retrocognition, information backflow, branch selection, and physical transit have different proof burdens and campaign consequences.
6. **Population governance requires a ladder.** Public demographic planning, voluntary incentives, coercive control, selection, and extermination cannot be treated as one category.
7. **No real population becomes an alien proxy.** Antisemitic, racist, anti-migrant, and medical scapegoating mutations are documented only to exclude them from canon.
