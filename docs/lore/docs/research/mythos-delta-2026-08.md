## Load-bearing conclusions

### Mega-conspiracy is a merger

The contemporary package of reptilian rulers, occult elites, underground bases, alien treaties, population reduction, mind control, weather weapons, time travel, soul traps, and secret fleets developed through mergers among older fiction, religion, contactee movements, real classified programs, documented abuse, and Internet synthesis. The Archive War should preserve those separate lineages.

### Documented abuse does not validate unlimited extension

Forced sterilization, MKULTRA, covert weather modification, official secrecy, and intelligence disinformation are real historical credibility kernels. They do not establish a unified alien-directed depopulation or mind-control plan.

### Reptilian is morphology; Serpent Court is institution

Reptiloid beings can belong to every faction. The Serpent Court is a Compact continuity institution using adaptive bodies, inherited credentials, and legal succession. No real population is recast as an alien bloodline.

### Time manipulation has four layers

- Retrocognition observes or reconstructs the past.
- Information backflow sends data rather than bodies.
- Branch selection chooses among nearby histories and creates archive forks.
- Physical chronology transit remains rare, expensive, and identity-destabilizing.

### Inside Job is a design corpus

Its useful lesson is not that conspiracies are factual. It is that secret systems require staff, budgets, procurement, maintenance, succession, and personal incentives. No cabal is omniscient; impossible technology still belongs to somebody.
