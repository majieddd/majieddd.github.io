# THE GALAXY, RESCOPED (owner directive, Session 42)

Every star system in the game, on paper, before anything is built. This change
moves two of the five core systems and adds five bonus systems, and the cost is
measured in GPU hours, not minutes, so it is scoped first and rendered second.

*Every system below is a real object. Every lore attribution names its actual
source, because the game's whole hold on a player is that the conspiracy layer
is real and checkable.*

### Why the two reassignments are better than the ones they replace

**PROXIMA CENTAURI for the Pirates.** Both Barnard's and Proxima are red flare
dwarfs, so the existing seven world functions transfer almost verbatim and the
flare shelter world stays literally correct. Proxima is additionally the
CLOSEST star to Earth, which makes it the first port out and the natural home
of a power whose entire identity is passage. `GX_STAR_POOL` already names its
seat PROXIMA GATE.

**SIRIUS for the Vigil, and this one is an upgrade.** Their canon is that
the Ancients evolved past physical form entirely. Sirius B is a white dwarf: a
star once LARGER than Sirius A (a progenitor of roughly 5 solar masses) that
burned through, threw off most of itself, and collapsed into a core about the
size of Earth still carrying the mass of the Sun. The makers' own star shed its
form. That is a better home image than an irregularly dimming star, and it is
real astronomy. `GX_STAR_POOL` already names the seat THE DOG STAR.

**One correction, and it improves the world.** Sirius B is popularly called a
diamond star. It is not one yet: at roughly 25,000 K and only about 126 million
years into cooling, its carbon core has NOT crystallised, and the famous
crystallised white dwarf is BPM 37093, not this one. So THE DIAMOND SHELF is not
named for what the star is. It is named for what the Vigil knows it will
BECOME, which is the most in character thing this faction could do: they built
their repair yards on a promise the star has not kept yet, because they fully
expect to still be standing there when it does. A civilisation that outlived its
own bodies names a place after its future.

## 3. The five bonus systems, world by world

Not part of the five act campaign. Each hangs off one core act, unlocks when
that act is cleared, and is worth extra experience and lore rather than
territory. **These are where the neutral machine units and towers live**, which
is the owner's directive and also the reason they are faction neutral to render.

Deliberately small: two to three worlds each, never seven, so a bonus reads as a
detour and not as a sixth act.

### BONUS 1. TABBY'S STAR, hangs off SIRIUS
KIC 8462852, the irregular dimming star, Boyajian 2015, popularly the alien
megastructure star. Real dips of 15 per cent in 2011 and 21 per cent in 2013.
The leading natural explanation is now orbiting dust, because the dips dim blue
light harder than red, which is the signature of dust and not of a solid panel.
**The swarm survives here as somebody else's unexplained work**, which is why
displacing the Vigil from it costs the arc nothing. The in game tension
writes itself: the machines insist it is a structure, the light says dust, and
the game never settles it.

| World | Function |
|---|---|
| THE VEIL | the swarm scaffold, a collector lattice the size of a planet, still half built |
| THE DIMMING | the shadow band, ground under the 21 per cent dip, which has never once seen full daylight |

Machines here still run the build order. Nobody can say whose it is. The
Vigil can visit and fail to explain it, which turns their lost finale into
their best open question.

### BONUS 2. BARNARD'S STAR, hangs off PROXIMA CENTAURI
Red dwarf, 5.96 ly, proper motion 10.4 arcseconds per year, the largest of any
star: it visibly MOVES against the sky. Four sub Earth planets are now confirmed,
b through e, every one of them whipping round in under seven days. **The old free
roads, still running,** and a system where nothing ever holds still.

| World | Function |
|---|---|
| BARNARD b | the cold rock, a sub Earth on a three day orbit, too close in to hold air |
| HARBOUR NINE | the ghost port, the sanctuary bay the roads used before Proxima, still lit, mostly empty |

Keeping HARBOUR NINE as a ruin rather than deleting it means the Pirates' old
seat becomes a place their own campaign can mourn.

### BONUS 3. KEPLER, hangs off THE PLEIADES
The NASA Kepler catalogue. **The registry of Earth like worlds, a Federation
obsession**: the list of places worth protecting, which is also the list of
places worth taking.

| World | Function |
|---|---|
| KEPLER-186f | first Earth size world found in a habitable zone, 2014 |
| KEPLER-442b | the highest habitability index in the catalogue |
| KEPLER-452b | the older cousin, a sun like star well past our own age |

452b is the one that matters thematically: it is what Earth looks like with a
head start, and the Federation filed it rather than went to it.

### BONUS 4. ARCTURUS, hangs off THE EARTH SYSTEM
K0 III red giant, 25 times the Sun's radius and 170 times its luminosity, 36.7
ly, and no confirmed planets, which is honest and usable: there is nowhere to
stand here, only orbit. Norma Milanovich's *We, The Arcturians* is the actual
source. **NOT Law of One, and NOT Edgar Cayce**, whom an earlier pass of this
document wrongly credited.

Arcturus is also a metal poor Population II halo star moving at about 122 km/s
in the Arcturus stream, believed to be debris from a dwarf galaxy the Milky Way
swallowed. **The one power that refuses this war is itself the remnant of a home
that was eaten.** That is not invented for the game. It is why they watch.

| World | Function |
|---|---|
| THE AMBER COURT | an orbital ring under a swollen orange star, a power that will not take a side |
| THE LONG WATCH | the observation station that has been recording every faction for longer than any faction has existed |

The Arcturians are the one power that refuses the war. Visiting them is the
only place a commander is told, by somebody with no stake, what they look like
from outside.

### BONUS 5. VEGA / LYRA, hangs off ZETA RETICULI
Vega, 25 ly. Its rotation axis points within five degrees of Earth, so we see
the whole system **pole on, face on, like a target**: a real fact that hands the
art its composition for free, concentric rings viewed from directly above. The
debris disk is confirmed and unusually smooth. It spins once every 16.3 hours,
fast enough to bulge its equator by about a fifth. No planet there is confirmed.
Billy Meier's Plejaren contact reports are the source for the Lyran root race.
**NOT Law of One.** Where the human lineage is said to start.

| World | Function |
|---|---|
| THE DEBRIS RING | the disk itself, a real one, still colliding |
| VEGA b | the world that may not be there at all, inferred from a dent in the disk and never once imaged |
| THE FIRST NEST | the claimed origin of the human line, and no evidence either way |

Hanging Lyra off the Xeno act is deliberate: the faction that farms humanity
unlocks the place that claims to have made it.

**Attribution correction worth carrying into the writing.** The owner cited the
Law of One for Arcturus and Lyra. Law of One (Ra, channeled 1981 to 1984) does
discuss a **Sirius** social memory complex, and Ra itself is described as having
evolved on **Venus**. Arcturians and the Lyran root race come from other
contactee traditions. Attributing each correctly is worth more than blurring
them, because a player who looks one up should find it.

## 5. Decisions taken, so the work is not blocked

Three questions were open. Each is answered here with its reasoning, and each is
cheap to reverse before Phase A renders.

**5.1 The Vigil's finale moves to THE DOG STAR.** Their act currently ends at
THE VEIL. Sirius is now their home, so the finale becomes the origin of the
orders, which is the better ending for a campaign that is a mystery about who
writes them: the last thing they take is the desk the instructions came from.
THE VEIL survives as Bonus 1, unattributed, so the image is not lost and it
gains a question it never had.

**5.2 Bonus systems unlock by clearing the core act they hang off.** Not open
from the start. Each pairing in section 3 was chosen so the bonus comments on
the act that unlocks it: Kepler after the Federation files its registry, Lyra
after the Xeno act, Tabby's after Sirius, Barnard's after Proxima, Arcturus
after Earth. A bonus opened before its act is a spoiler; opened after, it is a
coda.

**5.3 Bonus systems render faction neutral, 60 plates, not 375.** They are
nobody's home, they are where the neutral machine units and towers live, and a
per faction treatment would cost more than the entire Phase A swap for content
the player sees once. Neutral is also thematically correct for Arcturus, whose
whole point is that it will not take a side.

## 6. Beat 5 stops being one image

Owner note: the flag going in is right for MOST worlds, and some should be a
commander at a distance watching it happen and asking whether this was the thing
to do.

Rule: **the flag plants on ordinary worlds; the seat world of each act, and any
world where the faction's arc turns, shows the watcher instead.** That gives
every campaign roughly five reflective beat 5 plates against thirty planted
ones, which is the ratio the owner asked for and also the ratio that keeps the
planted flag meaning something. A doubt shown on every world is not doubt, it is
a house style.

This is also where the per faction moral lands, because it is the only beat in
which a faction looks at what it just did.
