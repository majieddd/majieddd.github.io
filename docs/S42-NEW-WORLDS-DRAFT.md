# The 14 new core worlds: art-driving strings, for review before any render

These are the five strings per world that drive the plates. They are written
here rather than delegated, because they encode the measured prompt laws (event
nouns and a low dynamic camera on the assault beat, surfaces described
positively as blank rather than negated, real astronomy driving what a place is
FOR) that a drafting model has no way to know and that cost GPU hours when
wrong. The faction dialogue that sits over them is drafted separately.

Field shapes match `artgen/planet_jobs.py` exactly:

| Field | What it drives |
|---|---|
| SKY | the establishing approach shot, the place owns the frame |
| SITE | the ground location itself |
| WORKS | the defensive installations, what the battle is fought over |
| AFTER | the aftermath, the same place broken |
| ORDER | the new order, what it becomes afterward |

Nothing below is rendered yet. Owner decision was to write everything first.

## PROXIMA CENTAURI, si 3, home of the Free Captains

Replaces Barnard's Star. Seven different functions of one frontier, so seven
worlds read as seven places rather than seven rolls of one.

### wi 0, PROXIMA d, the wreck yards
- **SKY** a scorched inner rock whipping round its small red star every five days, its daylit face crowded with the dark hulls of ships dragged here to die
- **SITE** the wreck yards, a plain of broken hulls from a hundred different builders, sorted into long lanes by whoever made them
- **WORKS** cutting gantries and salvage cranes turned outward, with stacked hull plate walled up between them
- **AFTER** the yards burning along their sorting lanes, cranes down across the stacks and cut hulls thrown flat
- **ORDER** the lanes cleared and the yards working again, every hull stripped to the frame and nothing left wasted

### wi 1, PROXIMA b, the terminator strip
- **SKY** a tide locked world holding one face to its red star for ever, a single liveable band running pole to pole between a burning day side and a frozen night side
- **SITE** the terminator strip, a long town built in permanent red sunset on the only ground that is neither scorched nor frozen
- **WORKS** ridge batteries dug in along the sunset line and shutter walls that can close the town against either side
- **AFTER** the strip broken open along the ridge, shutter walls buckled outward and the town burning in red dusk
- **ORDER** the strip rebuilt end to end with its shutter walls standing open, the town running the full length of the light

### wi 2, PROXIMA c, the cold refinery
- **SKY** a frozen super Earth far out from its star, its whole surface a pale blue ice sheet under a sun no bigger than a coin
- **SITE** the cold refinery, cracking towers standing in a field of blue ice, venting white plumes that freeze solid before they fall
- **WORKS** ice breach batteries ringing the cracking towers, with pipe runs armoured under banked snow
- **AFTER** the cracking towers toppled across the ice, pipe runs split open and every plume gone out
- **ORDER** the towers standing and venting again, the ice field cut with clean roads run between them

### wi 3, THE FLARE SHELTER, the dug in warren
- **SKY** a bare rock under a star mid flare, the whole sky washed hard violet white and the ground below stripped back to naked stone
- **SITE** the flare shelter, a warren of habs dug in under metres of rock whose only surface is a field of blast hatches
- **WORKS** hatch batteries set flush with the stone and shutter doors that seal the warren off from the sky
- **AFTER** the hatches blown open one after another, the warren below lit through the holes by the flare
- **ORDER** every hatch reseated and the warren driven deeper than before, the flare passing over ground that no longer cares

### wi 4, THE NARROWS, the toll gate
- **SKY** a debris lane threaded between three suns, ten thousand tumbling rocks catching the light of a triple star
- **SITE** the narrows, a toll gate of gun platforms strung on cables across the only clear lane through the rocks
- **WORKS** lane batteries on the platforms and cable strung mine curtains hung between them
- **AFTER** the toll ring shot to pieces, platforms tumbling away into the rocks and the cables cut
- **ORDER** the lane held open and the gate kept lit, a mark to steer by rather than a price to pay

### wi 5, THE DARK LOCKER, the black market vault
- **SKY** an unlit rock in the outer dark, so far from its small red star that it shows only as a shape against the stars
- **SITE** the dark locker, a vault hollowed into the rock where cargo that has no paperwork is stacked in the cold
- **WORKS** vault batteries cut into the rock face and pressure doors thick enough to count as walls
- **AFTER** the rock face blown in, pressure doors folded back and the stacked cargo spilled out into the dark
- **ORDER** the vault sealed and running again, its stacks sorted and its doors answering to a different name

### wi 6, PROXIMA GATE, the sanctuary bay (SEAT)
- **SKY** the closest star to Earth burning small and red over a great hollowed rock, every window of the harbour inside it lit at once
- **SITE** the sanctuary bay, an interior harbour of moored ships under a rock roof, built out of salvage by everyone who had nowhere else to be
- **WORKS** harbour batteries set into the rock roof and a boom across the bay mouth strung from wreck to wreck
- **AFTER** the boom cut and the harbour burning, moored ships adrift and the rock roof holed through to the stars
- **ORDER** the bay full again and the boom left down, every berth open, the first port out of Sol taking all comers

## SIRIUS, si 4, home of the Continuance coalition

Replaces Tabby's Star. Every world is an office of a civilisation that outlived
its own bodies, so the through line is administration rather than menace.

### wi 0, SIRIUS A I, the machine garden
- **SKY** an inner world under a blue white star twenty five times brighter than the Sun, its terraces standing in light too hard to look at directly
- **SITE** the machine garden, terraces of dormant automata standing in rows, each one pruned and aligned and not one of them awake
- **WORKS** terrace batteries set between the rows and hard light barriers strung along the garden walls
- **AFTER** the terraces broken and the rows toppled, dormant automata spilled down the garden walls
- **ORDER** the rows stood upright and realigned, the garden pruning itself again under a harder light

### wi 1, SIRIUS A II, the foundry
- **SKY** a blazing white world with no night worth the name, its canyon floor glowing where the casting halls run without stopping
- **SITE** the foundry, a canyon of casting halls turning out identical units one after another, every face the same
- **WORKS** canyon batteries on the hall roofs and pour gates that can flood the floor with molten metal
- **AFTER** the halls broken open, pour gates burst and the canyon floor running white hot
- **ORDER** the halls pouring again on a faster count, the canyon floor swept and the finished units stacked ready

### wi 2, THE ASH FIELD, the archive
- **SKY** a plain of grey dust that was once the outer envelope of a dying star, stacked memory cores standing up out of it like headstones
- **SITE** the ash field, an archive of memory cores standing open in the debris the companion star shed when it collapsed
- **WORKS** core row batteries and dust berms banked up between the stacks
- **AFTER** the stacks toppled through the ash, cores split open and grey dust thrown up in clouds
- **ORDER** the stacks reset in ordered rows, the ash graded flat and every core closed again

### wi 3, SIRIUS B I, the quarantine
- **SKY** a small world orbiting a white dwarf, its sealed halls glowing faintly under a companion star no bigger than Earth and hotter than anything else in the sky
- **SITE** the quarantine, sealed white halls holding units that failed inspection, every door locked from the outside
- **WORKS** hall batteries at the seals and containment shutters that drop between every corridor
- **AFTER** the seals blown and the shutters jammed half down, the halls standing open and the failed units gone out of them
- **ORDER** the halls sealed and the inspection standard raised, every door locked from the outside exactly as before

### wi 4, THE DIAMOND SHELF, the repair yards
- **SKY** a shelf of raw carbon under a fierce white pinpoint of a star, the ground glittering only where the light catches it
- **SITE** the diamond shelf, repair yards of gantries and cradles standing on carbon ground the Parallel named for what it will one day become rather than for what it is
- **WORKS** shelf batteries anchored down into the carbon and cradle clamps doubling as blast frames
- **AFTER** the gantries down across their own cradles, clamps sheared through and the carbon ground cratered
- **ORDER** the cradles filled again and the gantries rebuilt taller, the shelf mended and still waiting

### wi 5, THE COMPANION, the task queue relay
- **SKY** a white dwarf close enough to fill half the sky, tiny and ferociously bright, a relay mast standing black against it
- **SITE** the companion, a relay mast and yard where the standing orders are passed down the line and nobody asks where they enter it
- **WORKS** yard batteries at the mast base and relay shutters that can blind the whole line at once
- **AFTER** the mast down across its own yard, relay shutters closed and the line gone quiet
- **ORDER** the mast raised again and the line running, orders passing down it exactly as they did before

### wi 6, THE DOG STAR, the origin (SEAT)
- **SKY** the brightest star in Earth's sky seen from underneath it, a blue white blaze over a capital built entirely of instruction halls
- **SITE** the origin, the hall the standing orders come from, tier on tier of silent desks and not one of them occupied
- **WORKS** hall batteries between the desk tiers and instruction gates sealing each tier from the next
- **AFTER** the tiers broken through one after another, desks scattered and the instruction gates torn off their tracks
- **ORDER** the tiers restored and the desks realigned, the orders still issuing and the chairs still empty

## Notes on two choices worth challenging

**THE DOG STAR ends on empty chairs.** That is the payoff for the Parallel's
whole campaign: they conquer their way to the desk the orders come from and
find nobody has ever sat at it. It also means their ORDER line is the only seat
line in the game where the winner has not actually won anything.

**PROXIMA GATE's order line leaves the boom down.** The Free Captains are the
one power whose victory OPENS a place instead of closing it, which is the
inversion the `ORDER_OVERRIDE` table exists to carry. Expect the other four
factions to need overrides on this world: everyone else re-strings that boom.
